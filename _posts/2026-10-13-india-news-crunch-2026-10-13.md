# Mantbyte Daily Digest: Key National Developments

### Political Contestation and Civil Liberties

Tensions surrounding public assembly and electoral administration escalated across multiple fronts today, highlighted by demonstrations over civic spaces in the capital and widespread political criticism of the Election Commission.

* **Reorganization of Designated Protest Sites in the Capital:** The de-notification of Jantar Mantar as a designated protest venue has triggered sharp reactions from civil rights organizations and opposition parties, who argue the move curtails the democratic right to demonstrate. Demonstrations organized by groups including the Disha Students Organisation emerged at Connaught Place to contest the decision. Activists pointed out logistical bottlenecks at alternative locations: municipal officials confirmed that Ramlila Maidan, one of the newly assigned sites, has only three available open booking dates through the end of 2026. Other alternative locations designated by authorities include Burari Ground and the CBD Ground.
* **Intensifying Demands for CEC Resignation:** The Congress party launched widespread protests demanding the resignation of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar over allegations of administrative partiality and electoral mismanagement. In Hubballi, party workers staged street demonstrations, while Congress Working Committee member Gidugu Rudra Raju publicly called for the CEC's departure, claiming mounting discontent among national political factions. Simultaneously, verification of Delhi's Special Summary Revision (SIR) notices has accelerated via revised parameters on the ECINET portal, despite activist concerns regarding process scrutiny.
* **Supreme Court Extends Center’s Deadline on Detentions:** A three-judge Supreme Court Bench led by Chief Justice of India Surya Kant granted the Union Government an extension until October 15, 2026, to finalize its reply affidavit addressing allegations of illegal detentions and administrative clampdowns. 
* **Relief for Rahul Gandhi in Defamation Matter:** A Supreme Court Bench comprising Justices Vikram Nath and Aparesh Kumar Singh stayed defamation proceedings against Congress leader Rahul Gandhi linked to remarks made against Prime Minister Narendra Modi in 2018. The court issued notices seeking responses from the complainant and the Maharashtra government within four weeks.
* **Major Gubernatorial Appointments:** The Union government announced a series of gubernatorial postings across multiple states. Rajendra Arlekar has been appointed as the full-time Governor of Tamil Nadu, while Sudha Yadav assumes the governorship in Karnataka. Other postings include O.P. Mathur in Keralam, Braj Mohan Ram in Odisha, Dhal Singh Bisen in Uttarakhand, and former BJP general secretary Dushyant Gautam in Sikkim.

### Economy, Infrastructure, and Digital Finance

Macroeconomic indicators and regulatory updates highlighted India's fiscal policy and infrastructure expansion.

* **Prime Minister Outlines Growth Fundamentals:** Citing April–June quarter gross domestic product growth of 7.8% and foreign-exchange reserves exceeding $700 billion, Prime Minister Narendra Modi reiterated that India’s economic trajectory operates in synergy with global markets. The Prime Minister credited structural reforms spanning labour laws, taxation, and corporate insolvency frameworks for sustaining long-term capital inflows and stability.
* **NPCI to Introduce UPI MDR Post-Diwali:** The National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) announced that Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) charges on select Person-to-Merchant (P2M) transactions over the Unified Payments Interface (UPI) will take effect following the Diwali festive season, with implementation dates to be specified via an upcoming formal notification.
* **Telangana Infrastructure Sanctions:** The Union Ministry approved key infrastructure initiatives for Telangana, including the L.B. Nagar–Hayathnagar double-decker corridor, five ropeway systems, and three road overbridges (ROBs). State Minister Komatireddy Venkat Reddy confirmed that technical officials have been directed to draft a fresh Detailed Project Report (DPR) for an elevated corridor on the Hyderabad–Srisailam NH-765, alongside affirmative preliminary discussions for the Hyderabad–Amaravati greenfield highway.
* **Resource Strain and Sectoral Disputes in the South:** In Kerala, Chief Minister Pinarayi Vijayan acknowledged in the state assembly that an acute shortage of construction materials is inflating building costs and slowing infrastructure projects, forcing the administration to seek supply assistance from neighboring Tamil Nadu. Concurrently, in Telangana, Bharat Rashtra Samithi (BRS) leader T. Harish Rao alleged that transferring 30.63 lakh agricultural electricity connections to Rythu Discom threatens the state's 24x7 free power policy due to excessive reliance on intermittent solar and seasonal hydel capacity.

### Judicial Rulings, Security, and Institutional Governance

State-level judicial determinations, policy recommendations, and law enforcement interventions addressed key institutional matters across several states.

* **Madras High Court Rules on AIADMK MLA Resignations:** The Madras High Court Bench of Chief Justice Sushrut Arvind Dharmadhikari and Justice G. Arul Murugan upheld the Tamil Nadu Assembly Speaker’s decision to accept the resignations of six AIADMK legislators. However, the bench directed the Speaker to conclude pending disqualification proceedings against four of those MLAs within a strict two-month timeframe.
* **Karnataka to Challenge Reservation Verdict:** The Karnataka state government confirmed it will explore legal avenues to contest a High Court ruling that struck down an enhancement of reservation quotas, an initiative originally expedited by the previous administration prior to the 2023 Assembly elections.
* **Nilekani Panel Proposes NEET Security Blueprint:** Tech leader Nandan Nilekani submitted recommendations aimed at eliminating question paper breaches in national medical entrance examinations (NEET), proposing decentralized, last-minute digital distribution of test papers alongside a wider dispersion of examination centers.
* **Cybersecurity and Public Order Incidents:** The Delhi Police Special Cell filed four First Information Reports (FIRs) concerning coordinated disinformation campaigns distributed via social media since October 10, probing potential cross-border linkages tied to Pakistan. In Kerala, administrative authorities suspended over 20 students at a Kozhikode collegiate institution following violent campus clashes that originated over an online social media post and spilled over into local medical facilities.
* **Discourse on Sustainable Healthcare:** During *The Hindu Sustainability Dialogues 2026*, medical and technological experts, including leadership from the SIMATS institution, underscored the necessity of deploying artificial intelligence strictly where it enhances healthcare affordability, equitable access, and patient outcomes.
