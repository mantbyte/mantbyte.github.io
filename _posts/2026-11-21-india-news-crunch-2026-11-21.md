# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National News and Events

## Governance, Infrastructure, and Policy Updates
Prime Minister Narendra Modi visited Bihar to inaugurate the Nitish Setu, a 9.76-kilometer-long, six-lane bridge across the Ganga built at a cost of ₹5,500 crore, connecting Kacchi Dargah. During his visit, he also recreated a poll victory moment by waving a *gamcha*. 

In academic engagements, the Prime Minister participated in the Platinum Jubilee celebrations of IIT Kharagpur, India's first IIT. Addressing the students, he urged them to break free of limiting beliefs, stating, "I challenge challenges," and praised the institute's ongoing role in nation-building. 

Meanwhile, Union Home Minister Amit Shah addressed regional infrastructure concerns, assuring that the grievances of the people of Siang Valley regarding a local dam project will be thoroughly addressed through dialogue. In administrative developments, the Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) released its 2027 exam calendar, scheduling the 72nd preliminary examination for January 17.

## Protests, Legal Actions, and Public Security
Thousands of government employees and teachers, organized by the Action Council of State Employees and Teachers, laid siege to the Keralam Secretariat to demand unpaid service benefits and pay revision. Inaugurating the agitation, Opposition leader Pinarayi Vijayan called the protest a stern warning to the State government over denied benefits. Keralam's state infrastructure also faced scrutiny over a critical shortage of clinical psychologists in government hospitals, leaving approximately 80,000 individuals with intellectual disabilities waiting for necessary certificates.

In judicial and law enforcement developments, a U.S. court sentenced Indian national Nikhil Gupta to 235 months in jail. Gupta had pleaded guilty to charges including murder-for-hire, conspiracy to commit murder-for-hire, and conspiracy to commit money laundering in connection with a plot to murder Pannun. 

In Tamil Nadu, the family of a man who died in a police station was officially granted permission to review CCTV footage following a judge's inquiry into leaked clips. In Mumbai, eight minors were detained after a group assaulted a man and a woman at the Pramod Mahajan Garden in Kurla West. Additionally, Kannada writer Ravi Hanj was detained at Kochi airport during security checks amid an ongoing controversy surrounding his *Basava Mantapa* book and will be handed over to Karnataka Police.

## Weather Disruptions and Regional Incidents
Jharkhand and neighboring regions experienced major travel disruptions due to bad weather triggered by Cyclone 'Akvan'. Ranchi airport reported the cancellation of 14 flights as overcast skies and light rain impacted the area since November 21. Odisha's Chief Minister indicated that heavy rainfall was expected in Mayurbhanj and Balasore, with potential impacts extending into Jajpur, Bhadrak, Kendrapara, and Jagatsinghpur districts.

On the roads, a tragic accident in Andhra Pradesh's Prakasam district resulted in the deaths of four individuals near Pelluru village. In scientific safety updates, the Kalpakkam DAE Centre conducted a comprehensive emergency preparedness drive to test its safety protocols.
