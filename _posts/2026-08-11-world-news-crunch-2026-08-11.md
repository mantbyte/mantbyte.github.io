# Global News Digest: Mass Casualties in Colombia Quake, Shifting US Vaccine Policy, and Middle East Tensions

## Devastating Earthquake Strikes Colombia Amid Global Security Alerts

A powerful magnitude 7.4 earthquake struck Colombia, leaving at least 132 people dead and scores injured across the country. The epicenter was localized in the Chocó province near the Pacific coast, but violent tremors were registered hundreds of kilometers away in major urban hubs, including Bogotá, Cali, Medellín, and Pereira. 

Images and video footage from the impacted regions revealed severe structural destruction along a corridor of cities near the epicenter. In Cali, soldiers were recorded fleeing collapsing buildings as military barracks flattened during the quake. Severe shaking was also captured inside the airport terminal in Pereira. While the national capital, Bogotá, was largely spared from cataclysmic damage, rescue operations are ongoing nationwide, and authorities warn the death toll is expected to rise as emergency crews clear rubble.

In North America, law enforcement in Halifax, Nova Scotia, placed a man in custody following a targeted bomb threat against the local U.S. Consulate. Police discovered a propane tank and several containers containing what are believed to be flammable liquids inside the suspect's vehicle parked in a nearby garage.

## Middle East Conflicts and Escalating Foreign Policy Demands

The ongoing conflict involving Iran is straining American defense reserves, exposing critical shortages in U.S. weapons stockpiles and prompting renewed scrutiny over long-term defense spending. Despite the military pressure, U.S. President Donald Trump publicly asserted that the Strait of Hormuz remains open. Trump has demanded financial compensation from Tehran for "50 years" of alleged damages and wartime casualties. His statements follow Iran's previous condition that any opening of the strategic waterway be contingent on receiving war reparations. 

Concurrently, diplomatic dynamics around the Israeli-Palestinian conflict face renewed scrutiny as observers question whether Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu can afford to defy President Trump's proposed Gaza peace plan. 

In South America, Colombia’s far-right administration announced its recognition of Israel’s sovereignty claim over the Golan Heights. The decision makes Colombia only the second country in the world to officially recognize the claim over the annexed territory.

## Major Executive Orders and Turkish Peace Legislation

U.S. President Donald Trump signed an executive order directing federal health guidelines to reduce the total number of recommended childhood vaccinations. The order specifically mandates splitting up the combined Measles, Mumps, and Rubella (MMR) shot into separate inoculations. The American Academy of Pediatrics immediately criticized the executive action, categorizing the recommendation as "dangerous" to public health.

In Ankara, the Turkish parliament passed landmark legislation aimed at ending its decades-long conflict with the Kurdistan Workers' Party (PKK). The newly enacted law sets concrete terms for an amnesty, allowing thousands of PKK members to reintegrate into Turkish society provided the outlawed group disarms entirely.

## International Briefs: Ukraine Drone Operations, Migration Crises, and Wildlife Updates

* **Ukraine:** Reports from eastern Ukraine offer a rare view inside a secret operational site where Ukrainian forces are preparing long-range, deep-strike drones designed to hit targets far behind enemy lines.
* **Spain / North Africa:** In the Spanish North African enclave of Ceuta, the local mayor has requested the immediate implementation of migrant detention protocols. The demand follows a massive influx of thousands of migrants, which has overwhelmed local infrastructure and triggered intense political tensions.
* **South Africa:** Citizens commemorated the 70th anniversary of the historic women's march that resisted apartheid-era pass laws. Organizers emphasized that despite historical victories, South African women continue to battle pervasive violence, poverty, and systemic unemployment.
* **Wildlife:** In California, Jackie—the bald eagle who gained global fame alongside her partner Shadow via the Big Bear Eagle Nest Cam—has died after receiving weeks of intensive veterinary care. Separately, in Queensland, Australia, a humpback whale that was found stranded on a beach by a young girl successfully freed itself and swam away after the tide rose.
