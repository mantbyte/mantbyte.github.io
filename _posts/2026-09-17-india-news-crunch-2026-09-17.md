# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National News and Key Developments

### Industrial Strategy, Tech Innovation, and Economic Oversight

India's push to establish itself as a global technology hub marked a major milestone at the SEMICON India 2026 conference. Inaugurating the summit, Prime Minister Narendra Modi announced that the country has entered the second phase of its semiconductor journey, transitioning from foundational capability building to commercial chip manufacturing. Addressing industry stakeholders, the Prime Minister emphasized that the domestic semiconductor ecosystem is expanding rapidly, creating broad-based avenues for investment, technological innovation, and sustainable industrial growth. 

Parallel advances in specialized engineering and indigenous agricultural technology also gained recognition. In Andhra Pradesh, Srikakulam-based researcher Maheswaram Bhanu Chandhar secured an entry in the International Book of Records by developing a silent wind turbine weighing just one gram and measuring eight millimetres. Constructed using copper sheets, enamel paint, and a micro direct-current motor, the micro-device holds prospective applications in aerospace and robotics. Meanwhile, the Indian Council of Agricultural Research–Central Institute of Brackishwater Aquaculture (ICAR-CIBA) introduced dedicated deboning technology designed to remove fine intermuscular bones from milkfish, a step intended to improve consumer adoption and stimulate domestic seafood processing.

On the regulatory and corporate oversight front, the Enforcement Directorate (ED) has broadened its investigative scrutiny into potential irregularities within corporate insolvency processes under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code (IBC). Established in 2016 to ensure the time-bound resolution and asset-value maximization of distressed entities, the framework is now under closer examination to detect possible procedural malpractices.

### Political Milestones, Governance, and Diplomatic Greetings

Prime Minister Narendra Modi marked his 76th birthday alongside the commemoration of his 25-year tenure in public office, eliciting greetings from domestic political figures and global leaders alike. In Varanasi, Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu participated in the launch of the BJP’s month-long "Seva Sankalp Abhiyan," organized to honor the Prime Minister’s public service. Key Andhra leaders—including Deputy Chief Minister Pawan Kalyan, Minister Nara Lokesh, and YSRCP chief Y.S. Jagan Mohan Reddy—extended their formal greetings. Cross-party messages were also issued by prominent opposition leaders, including Congress President Mallikarjun Kharge and Leader of the Opposition in the Lok Sabha Rahul Gandhi. Internationally, Russian President Vladimir Putin conveyed felicitations, praising the Prime Minister's historic role and strategic contributions.

In institutional and governance developments, a newly published assessment reported that academic freedom in India faces severe restrictions under the current administration, noting a broader trend where academic liberty has substantially declined in 50 out of 179 surveyed nations over the past decade. 

In Tamil Nadu, the political community mourned the demise of veteran Congress leader and former Union Minister E.M. Sudarsana Natchiappan, who passed away at the age of 78. Natchiappan was widely recognized for his high-profile victory in the Sivaganga Lok Sabha constituency during the 1999 parliamentary elections, where he defeated former Union Minister P. Chidambaram.

### Judiciary and Anti-Corruption Proceedings

The Supreme Court addressed the high-profile 1999 Graham Staines murder case, reviewing a remission petition filed by convict Dara Singh. The Odisha state government informed the apex court that it had officially rejected Singh’s application for remission. Singh’s capital punishment was commuted to life imprisonment by the Orissa High Court in 2005, a verdict upheld by the Supreme Court in 2011. During the proceedings, the bench directed the convict to modify his remission plea to facilitate further judicial consideration.

In Kerala, a Special Vigilance Court convicted former Director General of Police (DGP) Tomin Thachankary in a disproportionate assets case initiated by the Vigilance and Anti-Corruption Bureau in 2007. The prosecution established that Thachankary had illicitly accumulated assets beyond his lawful income between 2003 and 2007. 

In another financial inquiry, the probe into the CMRL-Exalogic transaction case saw industrialist Sasidharan Kartha retract statements previously provided to the Enforcement Directorate. This development follows similar retraction affidavits submitted in 2024 by CMRL's former and serving senior executives, including its former Chief Financial Officer.

Additionally, the Supreme Court agreed to prioritize the hearing of a petition challenging the electoral authorities' handling of the Delhi Special Summary Revision (SIR). Representing the petitioners, advocate Prashant Bhushan submitted that election authorities had failed to fulfill mandatory disclosure norms regarding individuals issued procedural notices.

### State Developments and Public Health

In Andhra Pradesh, the political environment grew heated as the opposition YSR Congress Party (YSRCP) criticized the Telugu Desam Party (TDP)-led coalition government. YSRCP leader Gadikota Srikanth Reddy alleged that the administration is reneging on existing welfare commitments while floating deceptive promises in advance of local body elections. Amid regional agricultural concerns, YSRCP chief Jagan Mohan Reddy convened a strategic meeting with senior party leadership to assess the prevailing drought conditions across rural Andhra Pradesh.

In Tamil Nadu, the Directorate of Public Health (DPH) addressed seasonal health trends, clarifying that the current spread of dengue across the state remains within baseline thresholds. The DPH stated that minor surges during the monsoon reflect expected climatic shifts and do not represent an abnormal or critical public health crisis. 

Across the manufacturing sector, community traditions were observed during Vishwakarma Puja. Industrial centers noted instances of communal cohesion, such as at a regional leather manufacturing unit where Muslim artisans joined Hindu colleagues in honoring the patron deity of craftsmanship, setting aside mechanical tools for the annual festivities.
