# Mantbyte Daily Digest: Key National News & Developments

### Campus Developments and Academic Administration

**IIT Bombay Undergoes Administrative Changes and Infrastructure Overhaul Amid Investigation**  
The Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (IIT Bombay) has initiated significant structural and administrative adjustments following the death of a student, Wakode. The Mumbai Crime Branch is reviewing CCTV footage from the examination hall—where the student was reportedly caught copying and subsequently escorted away by an invigilator—as well as his hostel premises. In response to student safety concerns, the administration has begun replacing hostel ceiling fans with wall-mounted units. Concurrently, IIT Bombay Director Shireesh Kedare removed Professor Doolla from the position of Dean (Administrative Affairs), though student groups continue to demand his full suspension from academic faculty duties.

**IGDTUW Partners with CyberPeace for Cyber Safety Program**  
Indira Gandhi Delhi Technical University for Women (IGDTUW), in collaboration with CyberPeace, launched the "CyberPeace Keepers and First Responders" program. The initiative aims to train students to detect, research, and respond to cyber threats, with a primary focus on deepfakes, AI-enabled deception, misinformation, online impersonation, and digital harassment.

**SSC Constable 2026 Examination Results Anticipated**  
The Staff Selection Commission is scheduled to announce the results for the SSC Constable (GD) examination 2026. Qualified candidates will be able to review their qualification status via an official PDF document released on the commission's portal.

### Governance, Public Policy, and Regulatory Measures

**Tamil Nadu Expands and Rebrands Flagship School Breakfast Initiative**  
Chief Minister Vijay officially launched the expansion of the newly renamed Perunthalaivar Kamarajar Breakfast Scheme (formerly the CM Breakfast Scheme) at an event in Tiruvanmiyur, Chennai. The expanded program scales up coverage to include approximately 15.3 lakh students enrolled in Classes VI to VIII across 15,454 government and government-aided schools statewide. 

**Kerala and Tamil Nadu Form Joint Surveillance Mechanism on Illegal Waste Dumping**  
The State Pollution Control Boards of Kerala and Tamil Nadu have agreed to formulate a joint monitoring protocol to curb cross-border illegal waste dumping. The framework establishes coordinated verification of vehicle consignments, inspection of documentation, detection of environmental violations, and synchronised enforcement actions across state borders.

**Priyadarshini Free Travel Scheme Hits 100-Day Milestone in Kerala**  
Kerala's flagship Priyadarshini free transit scheme completed 100 days of operation. Addressing regional transportation pressures, the state utility reported an average of 12.93 lakh daily passenger journeys under the initiative.

**Meghalaya Repurposes Board Hostel as Foreigners' Detention Center**  
Authorities in Meghalaya have designated an education board hostel as a detention center for 30 foreign nationals who have completed their formal prison sentences. The cohort consists primarily of Bangladeshi nationals, several of whom have remained in custody for over five years awaiting deportation or administrative resolution.

**Mandatory Veg and Non-Veg Labeling Extended to Personal Care Products**  
New consumer protection and packaging regulations mandate that everyday hygiene products—including soaps, shampoos, and toothpastes—must feature standardized vegetarian and non-vegetarian indicator markings. The marks must appear prominently at the top of the package’s principal display panel.

### Judicial Rulings, Law Enforcement, and Politics

**Allahabad High Court Mandates State Service Bond Compliance for PG Doctors**  
The Allahabad High Court ruled that medical professionals who receive state-subsidised postgraduate education cannot bypass their mandatory government service bond. The bench determined that administrative delays in assigning postings do not nullify the underlying legal bond, while also addressing irregularities in the candidate's initial counselling process.

**Legal Developments: Kerala HC Grants Bail to Media Executive; Maharashtra UCC Member Resigns**  
* Justice Kauser Edappagath of the Kerala High Court granted bail to Reporter TV Managing Director Anto Augustine in connection with an alcohol possession case, overturning a prior order by the Sulthan Bathery Judicial First Class Magistrate.
* In Maharashtra, former High Court Judge Justice R. C. Chavan tendered his resignation from the state panel tasked with examining the Uniform Civil Code (UCC).

**Parliamentary Poll Schedules and Legislative Tensions**  
* The Election Commission announced the schedule for Rajya Sabha elections across 12 seats spanning Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, and West Bengal.
* The Jammu and Kashmir Legislative Assembly witnessed volatile exchanges between National Conference (NC) and Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) legislators regarding the recognition of official holidays commemorating Maharaja Hari Singh and Sheikh Mohammad Abdullah, prompting intervention from the Speaker.

**Law and Order: Encounter in New Delhi; Punjab Police Clarifies Advisory**  
* Delhi Police apprehended a suspect following an encounter linked to the gang-rape of a teenage girl in a public park by three men impersonating law enforcement personnel.
* Punjab Police issued a clarification dismissing claims that officers were advised against wearing uniforms while off duty, following public criticism from the Congress and Shiromani Akali Dal (SAD) over the state's security climate.
