# Mantbyte Daily Digest: Political Alignments, Legal Rulings, and National Updates

### Political Developments and Regional Alliances

Significant political realignments are underway in Punjab, where the BJP and the Akali Dal (SAD) have officially announced an alliance for the upcoming Assembly election after six years apart. Prime Minister Narendra Modi hailed the move as prioritizing Punjab and the welfare of its people. Analysts note that a united front changes the dynamics, as separate runs would have split the opposition space against the AAP and the Congress. Meanwhile, ahead of the Uttar Pradesh elections, the Samajwadi Party has offered 26 seats as Akhilesh signals adjustments for a tie-up with the Congress. Additionally, Abhijeet Dipke stated that the CJP will remain neutral and not align with any political party, expressing hope for youth-centric elections untied to caste and religion. Internal friction has also surfaced within the Congress, as Rajmohan Unnithan challenged Shafi Parambil to a public debate.

### Law, Order, and Tensions in Tamil Nadu

Tamil Nadu is experiencing heightened security and political friction on the day of the Karthigai Deepam festival in Thirupparankundram, following a Supreme Court interim order allowing the lighting of a lamp on the disputed hillock. The Madras High Court directed the Tamil Nadu government to comply with the Supreme Court order and release detainees. Concurrently, police restricted and arrested prominent political figures attempting to reach the site. BJP leader L. Murugan was stopped in the Coimbatore district—where prohibitory orders were not applicable—and condemned the action as a "murder of democracy." T.N. BJP chief Nainar Nagenthran was arrested by police in Tirunelveli as he headed toward Thirupparankundram. In other legal and administrative news, Karnataka has moved the Supreme Court to restrain Tamil Nadu from undertaking work on the Cauvery–South Vellar–Vaigai–Gundar (CVG) Link Project without clearance from the Union government.

### Crime, Health, and Social Welfare

In law enforcement updates, the Uttar Pradesh Police arrested a man who strangled a woman for rejecting his advances and subsequently killed her grandson. In Pune, the family of a murdered techie has demanded a trial in a fast-track court and the death penalty for the accused. Regarding public health and welfare, BJP MLA Rekha Patra’s medical condition has improved; she is now able to speak and communicate discomfort, with significantly reduced speech slurring. In Kerala, a dedicated township for Wayanad landslide survivors is progressing, with 149 houses preparing for handover in December, bringing the total to 327 completed homes in the second phase. In corporate and consumer news, Bengaluru International Airport Limited (BIAL) clarified allegations regarding a meal served at the 080 International Lounge, stating an internal kitchen inspection confirmed the dish was made with sprouted dal rather than maggots.

### Economy, Infrastructure, and Foreign Affairs

India's artificial intelligence sector has experienced a surge in investment, with funding nearly doubling to $1.6 billion in the first nine months of 2026, according to a Tracxn report that places India ninth globally. Notably, Mumbai surpassed Bengaluru as the largest recipient of domestic AI funding during this period. In infrastructure and defense, geospatial surveys revealed fresh construction activity at a tunnel outlet downstream of Medog County associated with China's dam projects. In foreign affairs, the Centre informed the Supreme Court that it has limited scope to secure the return of an Indian man serving in the Russian Army, as he had formally renounced his Indian citizenship and acquired Russian nationality prior to enlisting.
