# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National News & Key Developments

## Environmental Crises and Disaster Management

### Assam Flood Toll Rises as Authorities Call for Scientific Solutions
Unregulated mining, quarrying, deforestation, and jhum cultivation have severely destabilized slopes across Assam, exacerbating the impact of severe seasonal deluges. The death toll from this year's flooding has reached 98, with the majority of casualties concentrated across three heavily impacted districts. Responding to the escalation, Assam Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma emphasized that simply declaring the annual deluge a "national problem" offers little practical benefit. Instead, Sarma advocated for a comprehensive, scientifically designed national framework to address the root causes of the recurring flood crisis.

### Flooding in Kerala’s Kuttanad Region Triggers Political Debate
In Kerala, rising water levels in the Kuttanad region have triggered significant political fallout. A high-level ministerial delegation, including the State Excise Minister and Agriculture Minister, conducted a field visit to the flood-ravaged area on Saturday to assess damages and oversee local relief operations amidst mounting criticism from opposing political factions.

## Policy Initiatives and Political Developments

### Andhra Pradesh Launches Infrastructure Push for 100% Mobile Connectivity
To eliminate telecommunication blackspots across rural habitations, the Andhra Pradesh government has announced a major support package for Telecom Service Providers (TSPs). The policy package includes rent-free land allocations, subsidized electricity rates, and viability gap funding to accelerate the deployment of telecom towers in uncovered regions.

### Student Protests Escalate in Jharkhand Amid Negotiations
Tensions remain high in Jharkhand following widespread demonstrations by student groups and civil service aspirants. Although the state government announced a fresh round of formal talks on Saturday following preliminary meetings, leading student bodies accused the administration of political maneuvering to defuse momentum rather than addressing core grievances.

### State Relief Under Consideration for Vulnerable Farmers
Addressing agrarian challenges stemming from erratic monsoons, Minister Basavaraj Rayaraddy confirmed that the government is evaluating financial relief mechanisms for affected agriculturalists. The proposed measures intend to support both farmers who have paid crop insurance premiums and those prevented from undertaking seasonal sowing due to deficient rainfall.

### National and Regional Political Developments
* **TDP Cadre Mandate:** Telugu Desam Party (TDP) National President N. Chandrababu Naidu issued a strict warning against anti-party activities, instructing party workers to actively publicize government initiatives while countering opposition narratives advanced by the YSRCP.
* **Allegations Over Agency Misuse:** Member of Parliament Mallu Ravi publicly criticized the Central government, alleging systemic misuse of federal investigative agencies by the ruling Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) against political opponents.

## Public Safety, Legal Actions, and Regional Incidents

### Law Enforcement Action and Fraud Investigations
* **Sabarimala Ghee Scam:** Kerala’s Vigilance and Anti-Corruption Bureau (VACB) has registered cases against former Travancore Devaswom Board (TDB) officials concerning a financial fraud case involving ₹2.27 crore in Sabarimala ghee procurements.
* **Ayanki Manhunt:** Kerala Police intensified tracking operations for Arjun Ayanki after tracing his recent movements to a private Ayurveda hospital operating in Edappal, Malappuram district.
* **Police Passing-Out Parade:** A passing-out parade was held at Muttikulangara for 347 police recruits. The cohort includes 127 officers from the KAP First Battalion, 110 from the KAP Second Battalion, and 110 specialized police drivers for the India Reserve Battalion.

### Accidents and Emergency Responses
* **Banjara Hills Commercial Fire:** Emergency services conducted a six-hour rescue operation following a major fire in a commercial building in Banjara Hills, Hyderabad, successfully evacuating 11 individuals trapped inside.
* **Fatal ORR Crash:** Two migrant workers from Rajasthan lost their lives on Hyderabad's Outer Ring Road (ORR) service road after a high-speed vehicle lost control and collided with a bridge pillar.
* **Fatal Wildlife Attack:** In Konni, Kerala, a mahout was killed by an elephant named Priyadarshini at a local elephant camp while attempting to clean and dress a pre-existing wound on the animal's trunk.

### Judiciary Update: Supreme Court to Review Waqf Suit Fee Ruling
The Supreme Court of India has agreed to hear an appeal challenging a High Court decision regarding Waqf litigation fees. The impugned High Court order, delivered earlier this year based on a December precedent, established that Waqf institutions are not exempt from paying mandatory court fees when filing suits before the State Waqf Tribunal.

## Education, Healthcare, and Community Development

### AP Welcomes First Private Women's University
Marking a milestone in higher education, Vishnu Engineering College has been formally transformed into Vishnu Women’s University, becoming Andhra Pradesh’s first private all-women university. Chairman K.V. Vishnu Raju stated that the institution will concentrate its academic programs on emerging technologies, advanced research, innovation, and strategic international and industry partnerships.

### Healthcare Expansion and Micro-Entrepreneurship
* **Sanathnagar TIMS Inauguration:** Telangana Health Minister Damodar Raja Narasimha announced that the state-of-the-art MCR TIMS hospital facility at Sanathnagar is slated for an official inauguration soon by the Chief Minister.
* **Support for Women Entrepreneurs:** NTR District Collector G. Lakshmisha inspected 27 operational stalls at the SHG Women Entrepreneurs-Self-Employment Expo in Guntupalli. Lakshmisha highlighted the role of the RISE Centre in offering technical and operational infrastructure to convert local self-help groups into viable industrial ventures.
* **Cultural Outreach in Amaravati:** Union Minister of State for Rural Development and Communications Pemmasani addressed the ‘Amaravati Chitrakala Veedhi’ event, emphasizing the role of art in maintaining cultural continuity and community engagement. The event drew participation from nearly 800 artists.
