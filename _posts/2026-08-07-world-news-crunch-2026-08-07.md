# Mantbyte Daily Digest: Global News and World Events

## Politics, Policy, and Judiciary

In United States politics, Republican Representative Andy Ogles lost his congressional primary despite receiving an endorsement from Donald Trump. Ogles recently faced severe backlash for stating that "Muslims don't belong in American society." Meanwhile, the administration is pursuing further restrictions on US immigration, with new executive orders aimed at limiting birthright citizenship by banning birth tourism and narrowing the eligibility definition for children born in the country.

On the judicial and corporate front, tech giant Meta has been ordered by a New Mexico court to pay $567 million in the second phase of a landmark trial. The ruling—representing one of the largest child safety judgments against a social media company—comes after the Instagram and Facebook parent company also lost a related case in March over how its platforms harm youths. In Argentina, police deployed tear gas in Buenos Aires to disperse thousands of demonstrators during ongoing protests. 

## International Conflicts and Security

In the Middle East, an Israeli military raid in the West Bank’s Qalandiya left 51 Palestinians injured. The operation coincided with dozens of arrests near Jerusalem, highlighting a broader, ongoing surge in military and settler violence across the West Bank.

In Africa, Nigerian security forces successfully rescued 308 individuals who had been abducted in separate attacks across northern Niger and Kwara states. 

Meanwhile, sports governance is facing continued friction as UEFA stated that a potential boycott of FIFA may still move forward. The European governing body noted that FIFA's renewed backing of President Gianni Infantino at a recent meeting "changes nothing."

## Science, Technology, and Infrastructure

In groundbreaking scientific developments, US researchers announced they have utilized artificial intelligence to create 16 viruses not found in nature. While the milestone sparks optimism for future medical advancements, it has simultaneously heightened concerns regarding the potential misuse of AI technologies. In economic policy, the US has imposed a 15% tariff on a key semiconductor chip material to counter Chinese competition and safeguard domestic firms.

Infrastructure and disaster management developments span the globe:
* **Antarctica:** An Australian aviation crew executed a high-stakes midwinter rescue at McMurdo Station, landing an aircraft in complete darkness and amid -43°C temperatures to evacuate a patient.
* **Japan:** Following deadly tremors a decade ago, Kumamoto successfully mitigated casualties during a major earthquake through strict earthquake-resistant construction standards and community search-and-rescue training.
* **India and Mozambique:** Heavy monsoon rains since July have displaced thousands and impacted over one million residents across India’s Assam state. Concurrently, Mozambique is battling secondary crises following severe floods, where destroyed harvests and severed supply lines have driven vulnerable families into acute food insecurity.
* **Vietnam:** A sharp influx of foreign visitors is testing the nation's Soviet-style criminal justice system as the government simultaneously attempts to aggressively supercharge its tourism economy.
* **Brazil:** Authorities formally accused 16 individuals over the 2024 Voepass plane crash that killed 62 people near São Paulo, stating the aircraft was irregularly cleared for departure.
