# Daily Digest: Key National Developments across Governance, Regulation, Safety, and Education

## Political Developments and Legal Controversies

Political discourse and legal accountability dominated the national landscape today, with key figures facing scrutiny and regional leaders pushing for administrative policy shifts.

* **Rahul Gandhi Targets PM Modi Over Adani Case:** Congress leader Rahul Gandhi raised questions following a US court's decision to drop a case involving the Adani Group, stating that "there is much more to this than meets the eye" and renewing calls for accountability from Prime Minister Narendra Modi.
* **Legal Action Against Kangana Ranaut:** An Agra-based lawyer filed a formal legal case against BJP MP Kangana Ranaut following her controversial "Generation gutter" remark aimed at medical candidates protesting the NEET examination.
* **Security Breach in Bengaluru Prison:** Former MP Prajwal Revanna, currently serving a life sentence after being convicted in a rape case, was found in possession of a mobile phone inside his cell at a Bengaluru prison, raising concerns regarding prison security and oversight.
* **Nagaland CM Appeals for FCRA Amendment Review:** Nagaland Chief Minister Neiphiu Rio urged Union Home Minister Amit Shah to reconsider proposed amendments to the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act (FCRA). Rio requested a parliamentary committee review to address public apprehensions while preserving transparency and national security.
* **Tamil Nadu Focuses on Lok Sabha Seat Delimitation:** Tamil Nadu Chief Minister M.K. Vijay is expected to introduce a resolution in the State Assembly seeking to freeze the total number of Lok Sabha seats at 543 to prevent potential regional disparities during future delimitation exercises.
* **'Har Ghar Tiranga' Campaign Remarks:** Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath lauded the ongoing 'Har Ghar Tiranga' initiative as a vital tribute to India's freedom fighters, expressing pride in citizens displaying the national flag at their residences.

## Regulatory Directives and Interstate Water Governance

Federal authorities and interstate panels implemented crucial policy enforcement measures spanning cybersecurity, environmental safety, and water allocation.

* **Cauvery Water Management Authority Orders Release:** The Cauvery Water Management Authority (CWMA) endorsed the Cauvery Water Regulation Committee’s (CWRC) directive instructing Karnataka to release 12,000 cusecs of water daily for 15 days to Tamil Nadu. Karnataka authorities stated that final action will depend on a detailed assessment of the state's total combined reservoir storage, currently standing at approximately 88 tmc ft. against a total capacity of 114.57 tmc ft.
* **Massive Crackdown on Cyber Fraud:** The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology blocked over 3,700 applications linked to financial fraud. The systemic crackdown successfully prevented illicit financial drains, saving an estimated ₹11,158 crore for citizens.
* **Plastic Packaging Banned for Pan Masala:** The national food regulatory authority issued a prohibition on using plastic packaging for pan masala products. Manufacturers must shift exclusively to glass, tin, paperboard, cellulose, or other natural materials.
* **Parliamentary Panel Flags Disability Skill Shortfall:** A Parliamentary Committee expressed grave concern over severe delays in achieving skill development targets for persons with disabilities, highlighting that the action plan failed to reach even 25% of its designated training objectives.

## Infrastructure Incidents and Regional Weather Briefs

Incidents in major metropolitan regions prompted emergency response operations alongside national weather monitoring updates.

* **Tragedies Reported in Mumbai:** 
  * A major landslide in Mumbai's Kurla area left at least two people dead, with emergency personnel attempting to rescue several individuals trapped beneath structural debris.
  * Separately, a residential building fire in Vile Parle resulted in two casualties and six injuries. Investigations are ongoing to determine the cause of the blaze.
* **Monsoon Forecast for Delhi-NCR:** The India Meteorological Department (IMD) issued a weather advisory predicting continuous light rainfall across the Delhi-National Capital Region (NCR) extending through August 17.

## Higher Education, Public Recruitment, and Social Welfare

Key updates were released regarding university admissions, public sector recruitment drives, judicial interventions, and astronomical events.

* **Delhi University Seat Allocations Released:** Delhi University published its seat allocation lists for the 2026 undergraduate admissions cycle covering Sports, Extracurricular Activities (ECA), and Integrated Teacher Education Programme (ITEP) quotas. Candidates are required to confirm their acceptances within the stipulated timeframe.
* **Jamia Millia Islamia Distance Admission Deadline Extended:** Jamia Millia Islamia extended the application deadline for its distance learning programs while adding 11 new courses. Revised entrance examination dates for B.Ed and MBA programs have been rescheduled to September 19.
* **SBI Announces Major Recruitment Drive:** The State Bank of India released a recruitment notification for 7,680 Junior Associate positions. The preliminary examination is slated for September 2026.
* **Court Demands Financial Aid for Pediatric Care:** A court directed both the Central and State Governments to provide ₹1 crore in financial aid to facilitate medical treatment for a young girl in Indore, following counsel confirmation that the funds are currently held by relevant public institutions.
* **Solar Eclipse Guidance:** Astronomical experts provided clarifications regarding the upcoming 2026 total solar eclipse, detailing its alignment phase and clarifying visibility parameters across the Indian subcontinent.
