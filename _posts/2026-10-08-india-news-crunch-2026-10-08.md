# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National Mourning for Nana Patekar and Escalating Electoral Tensions

### Cinema and State Mourn the Passing of Veteran Actor Nana Patekar

The Indian film industry and political leadership are in mourning following the death of veteran actor Nana Patekar, who passed away at his Goa residence during the early hours of Thursday. Reports indicate that Patekar was declared dead at approximately 5:30 a.m., just hours after texting a friend to disclose that he was feeling unwell. 

Tributes have poured in from across the political spectrum. Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu, Deputy Chief Minister Pawan Kalyan, and opposition leader Y.S. Jagan Mohan Reddy expressed their condolences, with Kalyan specifically highlighting Patekar’s iconic performance in *Krantiveer* and his distinguished service with the Indian Army. Beyond his well-known Bollywood career, Patekar’s legacy includes significant contributions to regional cinema, such as his pivotal role in director Bharathiraja’s Tamil film *Bommalattam*, a project that preceded his later high-profile South Indian appearances.

### Electoral Disputes and Political Volatility in the Capital

The appointment of Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) Gyanesh Kumar and two other election commissioners has faced a formal legal challenge in the Supreme Court. This judicial scrutiny coincides with intensifying street-level protests. In Bengaluru, organizations including CJP and AISA convened a "People’s Tribunal" against the Special Information Report (SIR) process, signaling a continued mobilization ahead of a planned major agitation on October 10.

In New Delhi, the political atmosphere remains charged as the INDIA bloc enters the third day of its protest agenda, which includes visits to Rajghat. Security has been heightened around the Election Commission headquarters following reports of violence; notably, a man was thrashed for raising pro-Modi slogans during an opposition protest. Simultaneously, fresh FIRs have been filed against Congress leaders Rahul Gandhi and Priyanka Gandhi as demonstrations intensify. In a related development, the Delhi High Court has sought responses from the Delhi Police and PWD Minister Parvesh Verma regarding a "slapgate" incident, where the minister allegedly assaulted a citizen during a road inspection.

### Governance, Judicial Precedents, and Infrastructure Concerns

Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu met with Union Home Minister Amit Shah and Jal Shakti Minister C.R. Patil in Delhi to advocate for the Godavari-Cauvery river-linking project. Naidu also pressed for the timely release of funds for the Polavaram project and the Jal Jeevan Mission. On the diplomatic front, India issued a formal condemnation of Houthi attacks on Riyadh airports, labeling the targeting of civilian infrastructure as "unacceptable."

In the judiciary, the Kerala High Court delivered a landmark ruling regarding personal liberty, backing a school headmistress in a dispute over her choice of attire. The court affirmed that "attire is a citizen’s choice," after the headmistress was allegedly denied entry to her school for wearing a churidar instead of a sari. Meanwhile, the Supreme Court has directed the Guruvayoor temple to maintain traditional practices regarding the Ekadasi Udayasthamana Pooja, with a full hearing scheduled for December.

Safety concerns have also emerged in Chennai, where a car fell into a massive sinkhole near Tidel Park. While the occupants escaped, the incident has drawn criticism as it occurred on the same stretch of road where a similar cave-in happened in May 2025. In Bihar, Chief Minister Nitish Kumar has sparked fresh controversy with remarks regarding molestation videos, questioning why the media "could find only five videos," a statement the opposition RJD characterized as insensitive.

### Social Trends and Academic Updates

A recent study highlighted by *The Hindu* has shed light on the persistence of colorism in India, suggesting that family influence is a primary driver in the use of skin-lightening products. The research indicates that individuals are significantly more likely to use such products if a sibling does, fueled by deep-seated cultural access and social pressure.

In the education sector, NIT Patna has announced the opening of PhD admissions for the 2026-27 odd semester, with an application deadline of October 25. Additionally, the Karnataka MSc Nursing exams have been postponed, while the AYUSH NEET Undergraduate option entry remains open until October 12. In Puducherry, authorities have launched a special drive to enroll eligible voters who may have been omitted during the recent SIR process, with a focus on first-time voters.
