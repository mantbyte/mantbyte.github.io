# Daily Digest: National Legal Battles, State Policy Shifts, and Public Safety

### Judicial Oversight and Legislative Developments

The Delhi High Court has granted four weeks to Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal, Manish Sisodia, and Sanjay Singh to file their responses in a pending contempt case. A bench of Justices Navin Chawla and Arun Bhardwaj has scheduled the next hearing for November 16. The extension comes as the court continues to examine the legal ramifications of the matter.

In Tamil Nadu, the Private Universities (Amendment) Bill, 2026, has emerged as a focal point of legislative controversy. While the government aims to streamline private higher education, critics have raised concerns regarding the implications for institutional autonomy. Simultaneously, the Madras High Court has dismissed a writ petition challenging the election victory of DMK MLA Markandayan from the Vilathikulam constituency. Chief Justice Sushrut Arvind Dharmadhikari and Justice G. Arul Murugan refused to grant the petitioner liberty to pursue further legal remedies.

In a separate legal action, Tamil Nadu Minister T.K. Prabhu has filed a ₹1-crore defamation suit against YouTuber Maridhas. The Madras High Court has allowed the application to proceed, marking another instance of high-profile litigation involving digital content creators and elected officials.

### Political Friction and Cultural Disputes

A significant cultural and political row has erupted in Karnataka following a state order restricting the performance of *Vande Mataram* to two stanzas during state functions. The order specifies that the full six stanzas will only be played in the presence of the President, Vice-President, Prime Minister, or Governor. In response, BJP legislators staged a protest in the Assembly and Council, reciting the full version of the national song and shouting slogans against the government’s decision.

In Tamil Nadu, political tensions are rising over the proposed construction of a new Secretariat complex at Pattinapakkam. TVK leader Stalin has warned of massive protests, urging the government to heed the "cry of fishermen" and withdraw the order to protect the coastal community's interests.

Further north, Rajasthan Congress leader Tikaram Jully has accused the BJP of misusing government agencies to harass and pressure elected councillors ahead of urban local body elections. Meanwhile, in Uttar Pradesh, the AIMIM, led by Asaduddin Owaisi, is intensifying its organizational activities, signaling a search for a larger electoral role with a significant milestone planned for October 2.

### Public Health, Safety, and Institutional Accountability

Authorities in Bengaluru have uncovered a large-scale pharmaceutical racket involving the repacking and relabelling of expired and low-cost medicines as expensive multinational products. The Food Safety and Drug Administration (FSDA) has cancelled 16 pharmacy licenses and suspended eight others, including a Pfizer depot, following a raid at a farmhouse in Bidadi. Seized items, including forged labels and injection vials, are estimated to be worth ₹3.38 crore.

In the education sector, IIT-Bombay has issued an apology regarding previous remarks made following a student's suicide. The institute has suspended the Dean of Student Affairs as part of an effort to address grievances and improve institutional sensitivity.

Environmental concerns have resurfaced in the Western Ghats following the Union government’s issuance of the seventh draft declaring large areas as eco-sensitive zones (ESZ). Based on the Kasturirangan report, the draft has triggered anxiety among local residents, who have 60 days to file objections regarding the potential impact on their livelihoods.

### Regional Incidents and Noteworthy Events

*   **Andhra Pradesh:** Vijayawada witnessed a "counter-dharna" as YSRCP supporters and Physical Education Teachers (PETs) clashed at the SAAP office over disputes regarding DSC sports quota jobs.
*   **Telangana:** BRS leaders have formally requested that the Mamnoor airport be named after the historical ruler Rani Rudrama Devi.
*   **Sports Tragedy:** The football community is mourning the death of player Rijohn Jose, who died in a road accident. Fellow player Gani Ahammed Nigam, who was with him at the time, is currently undergoing medical treatment.
*   **Global Education Trends:** Reports have surfaced regarding a specialized school in the United States that charges $40,000 per semester. The curriculum focuses on manual labor, including farmwork and wood chopping, while strictly limiting digital screen time.
