# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National News and Events

## Security, Crime, and Counter-Terrorism

Prime Minister Narendra Modi, celebrating Diwali with security forces in Bastar, declared that the eradication of Maoist terror from the country’s forests is a victory of correct policies and strong political will. Highlighting that every district is now free from Naxalism, the Prime Minister noted that government decisions have successfully destroyed fake currency networks and choked supply lines. However, he warned that "Dimagi Naxals" (ideological sympathizers) still hide among the public. 

In international and domestic law enforcement updates, Lawrence Bishnoi gang member Hari Boxer has been detained in the United States, with the extradition process currently underway following coordination between the Rajasthan Anti-Gangster Task Force, CBI, and Interpol. Meanwhile, tragic violence claimed the lives of a senior IT executive in Faridabad, who was shot dead over a parking dispute, and Punjab anti-drug activist Jhota, whose body was brought to Mansa for last rites following his broad daylight murder on October 13.

## Political Developments and Commemorations

The Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) celebrated the 100th birthday of veteran leader L.K. Advani with a *puja* and *havan* coinciding with Deepavali. Leaders lauded his long public career and organizational contributions, while BJP chief Nitin Nabin urged opposition parties to adopt Advani's approach to national security by uniting during national crises. 

In regional politics, the TDP-led NDA secured a victory in the Andhra Pradesh rural polls, with the YSRCP securing 20 ZPTCs and 1,050 MPTCs amid allegations of irregularities raised by Jagan Reddy. In Rajasthan, political circles mourned the passing of four-time MLA and former Lok Sabha MP Jaswant Yadav, who died at 73 after a prolonged illness. Additionally, a political row continues at the Sanskrit University, where CPI(M) leader M.V. Govindan backed an ongoing student protest demanding that suspended students be allowed to write their exams following an SFI-led demonstration over campus facilities. On the financial front, the Congress party reported a nearly 75% drop in donations—the sharpest decline in a decade—receiving ₹517.39 crore in contributions for 2024–25.

## Infrastructure, Public Safety, and Industrial Incidents

Public safety and industrial infrastructure faced severe scrutiny following a series of fatal incidents. In Assam, Oil India Limited (OIL) suspended four officials after a massive explosion and fire at its Installation Tank Farm (ITF) in Tengakhat resulted in three deaths. Concurrently, electrical fires remained in focus as a dominant cause in recent Indian fire incidents, with experts pointing to persistent regulatory gaps, such as the failure to adequately account for harmonic currents in safety codes and guidelines.

Road safety also made grim headlines. A high-speed crash on the Surat-Chennai Expressway in Karnataka resulted in four fatalities after a car rammed into a bridge, leaving the vehicle completely mangled. Internationally, the remains of Veeramani, a Dindigul youth who died in a construction site accident in Israel, were laid to rest in his native village.

## Administration, Education, and Global Greetings

Global figures and diplomatic missions extended warm Diwali wishes to India, with leaders from the US, UAE, China, and Canada celebrating the Festival of Lights through community activities and cultural showcases. 

In administrative and educational updates, Rajasthan's local body elections faced scrutiny after an activist pointed out a disparity of 30 million voters between the state election commission's rolls—dating back to January 2025—and the Election Commission’s final SIR list. Meanwhile, the Rashtriya Military School revised its Class 9 Social Science syllabus for 2026, and Madhya Pradesh announced the Round 1 process schedule for AYUSH PG Counselling 2026, with All India Quota registration set for November 10 to 11.
