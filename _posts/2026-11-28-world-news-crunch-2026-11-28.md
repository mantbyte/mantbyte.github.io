# Mantbyte Daily Digest: Global News and World Events

### Conflict, Security, and Humanitarian Crises

In Yemen, government forces have struck Houthi positions as the United Nations issues a stark warning regarding an escalating hunger crisis. According to the UN, approximately 1.8 million people now face emergency levels of hunger in government-held areas. Meanwhile, in Afghanistan, at least six students were killed following a blast beside a school in the Kunar province, with officials noting that casualty figures remain preliminary and may rise. 

In Sudan, residents of Khartoum have launched a clean-up drive as mountains of rubbish and rubble threaten public health across the litter-strewn streets. In South Asia, India has declared victory over Kashmir's separatist movement following the death of what it termed the "last local militant," though analysts caution that rebels are adapting by shifting their tactics, names, and geography. In Latin America, an 8-person casualty toll was reported after a Peruvian military helicopter crashed during a rescue mission in a remote area known for drug trafficking, leaving the pilot as the sole survivor.

### Politics, Diplomacy, and Elections

Spain is bracing for a significant political shift as the far right gains ground ahead of an upcoming national election on Sunday. Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez, celebrated internationally as a left-wing hero and a staunch critic of President Trump, faces a tough challenge to retain power domestically. In France, officials report that far-right vigilantes have inflamed ongoing unrest, engaging in street fights with left-wing counterparts across at least 10 towns over weeks of anti-government demonstrations.

Diplomatic friction arose in Costa Rica, where UN official Albanese was held at an airport before being released following a constitutional court verdict. The incident has created a political test for Rebeca Grynspan, a leading candidate for the UN chief position who recently topped a UN straw poll. In international sports diplomacy, FIFA is reportedly rethinking and may scrap the peace prize that leader Gianni Infantino presented to President Trump last year amid growing scrutiny over ties between the two figures. 

### Economics, Technology, and Public Health

In the United States, trade policy analysts note that former President Trump’s tariffs have effectively become America's "new normal," to the extent that even a future Democratic administration might struggle to reverse duties that help finance tax cuts. In the technology sector, Israel is fast-tracking data centres in an aggressive bid to establish itself as an artificial intelligence superpower, aiming to leverage data processing to bolster both its military strength and global clout. 

Public health initiatives and challenges continue to make headlines across the globe. Uganda has initiated the first-ever African clinical trial for an mRNA Ebola vaccine, targeting the less common Bundibugyo strain that was responsible for the recent outbreak in the neighboring Democratic Republic of Congo. In India, the tragic death of student Rishikesh Kumar—who died by suspected suicide months after gaining admission to an elite Indian Institute of Technology (IIT)—has revived urgent questions regarding student support systems. Meanwhile, severe El Niño-linked deluges have devastated parts of South America, causing 34 deaths and affecting 47,000 people in Ecuador, alongside heavy flooding that impacted 2,000 families in Peru's Piura region.

### Society, Culture, and Tributes

Royal watchers received a closer look into recovery following the release of *Heal*, a 30-minute authorized documentary offering glimpses into Princess Catherine’s recovery since her 2024 cancer diagnosis and treatment. In the world of entertainment and sports, tributes have poured in following the death of 38-year-old Australian wrestler Bronson Reed, who is survived by his pregnant wife, Paige, and their daughter. Additionally, for those seeking historical perspective on conflict resolution, conflict negotiators who brokered historic peace deals are sharing insights into the secrets of ending intractable wars in an ongoing media series.
