# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National Security, Global Diplomacy, and Regional Developments

### Global Diplomacy and the BRICS Summit
At the BRICS Summit, Prime Minister Narendra Modi underscored India’s strategic priorities, focusing on four foundational pillars: resilience, innovation, cooperation, and sustainability. Addressing the assembly, the Prime Minister flagged serious concerns regarding the "weaponisation of technology and critical minerals," warning that such trends could obstruct global progress. He further characterized the evolution of BRICS as a "win-win collaboration" that has successfully transformed a shared vision into tangible cooperation.

The summit saw the adoption of the "New Delhi Declaration," in which BRICS leaders voiced collective apprehension over the "growing risks" of nuclear danger and conflict. On the sidelines, United Nations Secretary-General António Guterres commended India’s mediation efforts between Russia and Ukraine. While acknowledging that the Security Council currently faces paralysis, Guterres maintained that the UN remains a vital institution. Additionally, a consensus was reached on the BRICS Early Warning System to enhance global responses to infectious diseases. From a regional perspective, Chinese media representatives at the summit noted that strengthening India-China ties remains strategically vital for the Global South.

### Law, Order, and Public Safety
Internal security and criminal justice remain at the forefront of national news. In Manipur, suspected militants launched an indiscriminate attack on Tollen Kuki village near NH-37, resulting in the death of a woman and injuries to her husband and son. Meanwhile, in Uttar Pradesh, the Balyan khap, led by Naresh Tikait, has issued an October 5 deadline for the arrest of the remaining accused in the Ankit Balyan murder case. The khap is demanding security, employment, and financial compensation for the victim's family.

In Madhya Pradesh, police apprehended Shivanjay Rajput, a wanted suspect in the state’s hooch tragedy, following a brief encounter in Sagar. In Kerala, four individuals have been remanded in judicial custody in Kozhikode in connection with a drug-induced gang rape, with authorities investigating potential wider involvement. On the roads, a tragic accident in Vizianagaram claimed three lives after a speeding lorry rammed into an auto-rickshaw, an incident attributed to negligent driving.

### Regional Governance and Political Friction
Political tensions are escalating across several states. In Tamil Nadu, DMK MP A. Raja expressed regret over remarks made against Minister C.T.R. Nirmal Kumar, following accusations from the TVK (Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam) of obscene language. Simultaneously, AIADMK leader R.B. Udhayakumar criticized the TVK administration for its inability to meet rising power demands, alleging that "lackadaisical" management has led to prolonged power cuts affecting students.

In Kerala, the Poothrikka panchayat bypoll has sparked speculation regarding a pragmatic alliance between the Twenty20 party and the BJP. Legal challenges have also emerged in the Madras High Court, where a plea seeks to stay the Madurantakam and Dharapuram byelections, arguing that frequent resignations and re-elections result in the wasteful expenditure of public funds. 

In other news, the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) has issued a mandate requiring government approval for any use of the Indian Patent Office’s official logo to prevent unlawful utilization. In West Bengal, traditional "Bonedi Bari" Durga Pujas have rejected calls for a "veg-only" festival, maintaining their non-vegetarian traditions despite external social pressure. Finally, the political landscape in Assam mourns the passing of former Minister Siddeque Ahmed, a four-term MLA who died at 63 after a long battle with cancer.
