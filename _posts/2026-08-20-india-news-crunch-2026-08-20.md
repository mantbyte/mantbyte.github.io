# National Infrastructure and Strategic Governance

The Union Cabinet has approved a significant investment of ₹13,041 crore for various railway and highway projects spanning five states. This move is aimed at bolstering regional connectivity and supporting long-term economic growth. In a parallel development regarding India’s digital economy, a former board member of the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) has weighed in on the ongoing debate over Unified Payments Interface (UPI) transaction fees, emphasizing that the platform’s success has been fundamentally rooted in its "free and instant" model for consumers.

On the international front, United Nations Deputy Secretary-General Amina Mohammed is scheduled to visit India later this month to engage with high-level officials. Domestically, the Tamil Nadu government has announced an increase in its annual allocation for archaeological excavations to ₹10 crore. This funding includes ₹2 crore for a modern archaeological laboratory designed to conduct advanced scientific analysis on artifacts unearthed across the state.

# Political Polarization and Electoral Developments

Political rhetoric has intensified as BJP President Nabin labeled the Congress party the "New Muslim League," following a resolution by the opposition regarding its stance on *Vande Mataram*. This exchange comes as the Rajasthan State Election Commission announced local body polls for 309 urban entities, immediately triggering the Model Code of Conduct. In response, the Congress has launched a specialized panchayat task force campaign to mobilize rural voters.

In Odisha, the Biju Janata Dal (BJD) has formally requested a 30-day extension for the claims and objections period under the Special Interim Revision (SIR), citing ongoing flood disruptions that may disenfranchise voters. Meanwhile, in Uttar Pradesh, State Congress President Ajay Rai alleged an "authoritarian trend" after his WhatsApp account was blocked, claiming the move was intended to disrupt party communication ahead of upcoming elections.

Institutional changes are also drawing scrutiny as the NCERT formed a new team to revise Political Science textbooks. The 15-member committee, led by Sandeep Shastri, includes four members with reported links to the RSS and ABVP, sparking a debate over the ideological direction of the national curriculum.

# Public Safety, Health, and Legal Oversight

The Maharashtra Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has launched a widespread crackdown on food safety violations. The agency suspended the license of Mumbai’s prestigious Otters Club over the use of "analogue paneer" and cited several other establishments for hygiene and pest-control failures. Additionally, two Blinkit distribution facilities in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar and Mumbai had their licenses suspended for serious violations, including the storage of expired products.

In Kerala, the state government reported significant progress in its "Operation Toofan" anti-drug campaign. Home Minister Ramesh Chennithala announced that 10,213 cases have been registered and drugs worth over ₹100 crore seized within 76 days. However, political friction remains high in the state as the Opposition Leader refuted Chief Minister Pinarayi Vijayan’s claims that previous administrations delayed the Vizhinjam International Seaport project.

In legal and social developments:
*   **Education:** The government confirmed a retest for three subjects of the UGC-NET following allegations of "reckless" use of AI in paper setting, a claim the National Testing Agency (NTA) continues to deny.
*   **Civil Rights:** In Ladakh, civil society groups (LAB and KDA) submitted a list of non-negotiable demands to the Ministry of Home Affairs, continuing their agitation for Statehood and Sixth Schedule protections.
*   **Law Enforcement:** Police in Pune have booked eight members of a right-wing group for assaulting a tourist over a companion's attire at a local fort, citing illegal moral policing.
*   **Judiciary:** The Bombay High Court is hearing a plea in a Savarkar defamation case, where the petitioner argues the lower court is overstepping its mandate by attempting to judge Savarkar’s historical character rather than focusing on specific written evidence.
