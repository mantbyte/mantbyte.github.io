# National News & Events Digest

### Governance, Policy, and Political Developments

* **Prime Minister Highlights Climate Inequality at NGT Conference:** Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated the National Green Tribunal's (NGT) climate conference, emphasizing global and domestic climate inequality. The high-profile event was attended by Chief Justice of India Surya Kant, Union Minister for Environment, Forest and Climate Change Bhupender Yadav, Attorney General R. Venkataramani, and NGT Chairperson Justice Prakash Shrivastava.
* **Kerala Examines Legal Opinion on ED Move Against CM Vijayan:** Kerala’s Home Department is currently scrutinizing the Advocate General’s legal opinion regarding a request by the Enforcement Directorate (ED) to initiate a case against Chief Minister Pinarayi Vijayan. Senior Congress leader Ramesh Chennithala stated that there is no requirement for Leader of the Opposition V.D. Satheesan to consult Congress leader Rahul Gandhi regarding the development during upcoming meetings in New Delhi.
* **TMC Suffers Bypoll Setback in Rejinagar:** In West Bengal, the candidate nominated by Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee for the Rejinagar bypoll withdrew their nomination, adding to the party's recent electoral challenges following the Nandigram outcome.
* **AIADMK Leaves Out Dissidents from Bypoll Campaign Roster:** The AIADMK released its star campaigner roster for upcoming bypolls, conspicuously sidelining prominent dissident legislators. The 13-member list of sitting MLAs featured in the campaign is led by party General Secretary Edappadi K. Palaniswami.
* **Sonam Wangchuk Announces 20-km March in Leh:** Climate activist Sonam Wangchuk is set to lead a 20-kilometer symbolic march in Leh on September 23 to highlight regional and environmental demands.
* **Bangalore Terriers Conclude Jammu & Kashmir Deployment:** The Bangalore Terriers battalion has returned to its home base following the conclusion of its deployment in counter-terrorist operations in Jammu and Kashmir.

### Campus Affairs, Education, and Legal Probes

* **ABVP Takes Lead in DUSU Elections Amid Procession Ban:** Counting for the Delhi University Students' Union (DUSU) central panel for the 2026–27 session showed the Akhil Bharatiya Vidyarthi Parishad (ABVP) leading the presidential race following the fifth round of counting. The multi-cornered contest involves the ABVP, the National Students' Union of India (NSUI), and the AISA-SFI alliance. The Delhi High Court has prohibited any post-result victory processions.
* **Tragedy and Unrest at IIT Bombay:** Protests have broken out at IIT Bombay following the suicide of a student who was reportedly apprehended using ChatGPT via a mobile phone during an examination. 
* **FTII Itanagar Suspends Classes Over Security Concerns:** The Students’ Council of the Film and Television Institute of India (FTII) Itanagar has suspended academic classes indefinitely. The decision follows incidents of assault against faculty and students, unauthorized campus intrusions, and escalating safety concerns, particularly among female students.
* **Bizarre Concealment Unearthed in Maharashtra TET Paper Leak:** Investigations into the Maharashtra Teacher Eligibility Test (TET) paper leak revealed that involved personnel hid question sheets inside their shoes and received payments of ₹8,000 each to facilitate the compromise.
* **IIM Visakhapatnam Commences Executive MBA Admissions:** The Indian Institute of Management Visakhapatnam has formally launched admissions for the fourth cohort of its Executive MBA program, featuring core management disciplines, specialized electives, and a capstone project.

### Public Health, Safety, and Regional Incidents

* **Pharma Violations in Mumbai Prompt FDA Crackdown:** A consumer survey across Mumbai found that 71% of respondents are rarely or never asked to produce a valid prescription by local chemists when purchasing medicines. Amid widespread non-compliance, the Maharashtra Food and Drug Administration (FDA) initiated punitive action against 880 drug licensees over a three-month span.
* **Mumps Outbreak Prompts Advisory in Thrissur:** Health authorities in Kerala’s Thrissur district have urged heightened vigilance following an uptick in mumps cases, identifying children between the ages of 5 and 15 as the most susceptible demographic. The District Medical Officer has advised isolation for infected patients.
* **Fatal Tractor Mishap at Ganesh Immersion in Dharmavaram:** A young girl was killed and seven others sustained injuries when an out-of-control tractor ploughed through crowds during a Ganesh idol immersion procession near Sivanagar Bramhamgari Temple in Dharmavaram, Andhra Pradesh. The injured were moved to the area hospital.
* **School Bus Collides with Truck on Chennai-Bengaluru Highway:** Four schoolchildren and a driver sustained injuries following a collision between a school bus and a lorry on the Chennai-Bengaluru National Highway near Ambur. Local residents subsequently staged a road blockade demanding immediate traffic safety interventions, while the lorry driver was taken into custody.

### Economy, Energy, and Culture

* **DGGI Unearths ₹115-Crore GST Evasion Across Andhra Pradesh Hotels:** The Directorate General of GST Intelligence (DGGI) uncovered an alleged ₹115 crore tax evasion operation spanning multiple hotels in Andhra Pradesh. Investigators revealed that the establishments systematically suppressed reported sales figures compared to the revenues collected from patrons.
* **WBPDCL Commences Surplus Coal Transfer to NTPC:** The West Bengal Power Development Corporation Limited (WBPDCL) has initiated the supply of surplus coal to central power generator NTPC, with plans to deliver a cumulative 3.5 million tonnes by March 2027.
* **Assam Remembers Zubeen on Death Anniversary:** Tributes were paid across Assam to mark the death anniversary of cultural figure Zubeen. Commemorative prayers, offerings of earthen lamps, and traditional *gamosas* were held, with Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma stating that the loss remains a void the state must learn to live with.
* **Broadcast Arrangements Revealed for Asian Games 2026:** Indian broadcast and streaming details for the Asian Games 2026 were confirmed, with digital streaming rights assigned to Sony LIV and linear television coverage set for Sony Sports networks.
