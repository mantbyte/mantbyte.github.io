# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National News and Events

## Judiciary and Legal Developments

A Division Bench of the Madras High Court has set aside a one-month simple imprisonment sentence previously imposed on senior IAS officer Anshul Mishra by a single judge in a contempt case. While vacating the punishment—which had also directed Mishra to pay ₹25,000 in compensation to two elderly petitioners—the Bench nevertheless expressed strong displeasure regarding the officer's delay in complying with court orders. 

In Kerala, a local court addressing a former top cop’s plea to ease bail terms raised questions regarding how the individual, Jacob Thomas Thachankary, managed to avoid spending actual time in a regular prison cell, remarking during proceedings that he "was never in jail."

## Security, Investigations, and Cyber Crime

The National Investigation Agency (NIA) has taken over the probe into the online radicalisation of Indian youths orchestrated by Pakistan-based gangster Shahzad Bhatti. Operating alongside radical Pakistani YouTubers allegedly acting on ISI instructions, Bhatti's network utilised social media campaigns to spread influence across 14 states, prompting the Home Ministry to designate the network as a terror group. In separate security operations, security forces in Baramulla, Jammu and Kashmir, arrested a man and recovered two hand grenades.

Financial and cyber crimes continue to challenge law enforcement. In Kerala’s Kozhikode, two separate online trading frauds resulted in losses nearing ₹1 crore, with funds systematically routed through mule and current accounts rented specifically for the operation. Additionally, a Dubai-based Keralite lost ₹2.11 crore in a separate forex trading scam operated via Telegram after being lured by a fake stock market intermediary promising high returns with minimal risk.

## Politics and Governance

Political realignments and controversies are gaining momentum ahead of upcoming state electoral contests. In Uttar Pradesh, AIMIM chief Asaduddin Owaisi announced Chandrashekhar Azad as the Chief Ministerial face of the Samajik Parivartan Morcha, with Azad indicating openness to alliances with the SP, Congress, or other parties to prevent the BJP from returning to power. Concurrently, activist Amitabh Thakur demanded immediate monitoring of the political social media war in U.P., announcing that his party, the Azad Adhikar Sena, will independently track unlawful propaganda. 

In Maharashtra, the Gokul Dairy election has sparked intense political outrage. The controversy stems from a state ordinance that permits the government to nominate voting directors to the cooperative’s board. Meanwhile, in Ladakh, activist Sonam Wangchuk was removed from a protest site and escorted home by police amid ongoing demonstrations against the scheduling of hill council elections on December 4, which protesters argue conflict with harsh winter conditions and seasonal migration.

## Infrastructure, Environment, and Regional Developments

Environmental concerns in the national capital have intensified following a new analysis by the Centre for Science and Environment (CSE), which revealed that Delhi entered the Deepavali season with its worst pre-festival air quality in three years. 

In regional infrastructure and administration, Telangana Agriculture Minister announced deadlines for the Koheda Fruit Market, setting the completion of all works for June 2, 2028, with the first phase targeted for March 2027. Meanwhile, the Telangana Anti-Corruption Bureau (ACB) conducted searches at properties linked to an Irrigation Department engineer in a disproportionate assets (DA) case. In southern wildlife reserves, villagers near Hasanur experienced panic after a leopard killed a cow left to graze near the reserve forest along National Highway 948.
