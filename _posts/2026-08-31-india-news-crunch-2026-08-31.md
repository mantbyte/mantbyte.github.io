# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National Policy Shifts, Infrastructure Milestones, and Regional Developments

### Tamil Nadu: Major Industrial Announcements, Civic Works, and Political Frictions

Tamil Nadu Chief Minister C. Joseph Vijay has announced a series of major industrial and infrastructure initiatives across the state. Key among these is the establishment of a Semiconductor Park at Kancheepuram, a FinTech Hub in Coimbatore, and a Multi-Modal Logistics Hub in Tiruchi. Bolstering the state's power infrastructure, the Chief Minister also announced a new supercritical thermal power station in Thoothukudi alongside the ₹33,066-crore Tamil Nadu Transmission Improvement Scheme to enhance distribution to key consumption centres. In the agricultural and dairy sectors, the state government increased the milk procurement price further to ₹44 per litre—following an earlier hike from ₹38 to ₹41—while the annual water release from the Mettur Dam, delayed due to poor monsoon conditions, has been scheduled for September 1, 2026.

Within the capital, the Greater Chennai Corporation (GCC) confirmed that over 70% of Chennai’s stormwater drains are targeted for desilting by the end of September. 

On the political front, coalition friction has surfaced with Tamil Nadu Minister S. Rajesh Kumar warning that the Congress will exit Chief Minister Vijay’s Cabinet if Rahul Gandhi is not accepted as the Prime Minister candidate, responding to efforts by Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) functionaries projecting Vijay for the role. In other regional political updates, former AIADMK minister and DMK Erode Central district secretary Thoppu Venkatachalam resigned from the DMK citing personal distress, while Tamil Nadu Assembly Speaker J.C.D. Prabhakar allotted a front-row seat to Congress floor leader Tharahai Cuthbert following seat requests submitted by DMK party whip E.V. Velu.

### National Affairs, Defense Commissioning, and Judicial Observations

* **Supreme Court on Investigative Agencies:** Hearing a petition by Sumit Roy, personal assistant to TMC MP Abhishek Banerjee regarding the Salboni land-grabbing case, the Supreme Court observed that probe agencies tend to proceed slowly against powerful individuals while acting swiftly against common citizens.
* **Legal Action in Uttarakhand:** An FIR has been registered against Congress leader Rahul Gandhi over remarks linked to a "purification yajna" performed by certain individuals at Haldwani's Ramlila Ground following an August 11 Congress public meeting.
* **'One Nation, One Time' Initiative:** The central government detailed the framework for the 'One Nation, One Time' system, noting that modern digital systems and expanding technological infrastructure require a unified national time reference.
* **Naval Capability Expansion:** The Indian Navy officially commissioned the deep-sea diving vessel *INS Nipun* in the presence of Chief of the Naval Staff Admiral Krishna Swaminathan, bolstering specialized underwater operational capabilities.

### Southern States: Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and Kerala

* **Veligonda Project Phase 1 Inauguration:** Three decades after laying the foundation stone, Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu inaugurated the first phase of the Veligonda irrigation project. The project releases approximately 10.70 tmc ft of Krishna water from the Srisailam reservoir into Nallamala Sagar, intended to irrigate 1.19 lakh acres and supply drinking water to 4 lakh people.
* **Ex Gratia for Accident Victims:** Chief Minister Naidu announced an ex gratia of ₹10 lakh each for the families of eight individuals from Veluru village who died on August 27 following a collision between a private travel bus and an auto-rickshaw in Chilakaluripet.
* **Telangana Urban Infrastructure:** Telangana Chief Minister A. Revanth Reddy is set to inaugurate a 400-metre-wide bridge connecting Moosarambagh and Amberpet, alongside unveiling the new logos for the CMC, MMC, and GHMC civic bodies.
* **Developments in Kerala:** A conservative Muslim cleric's public statement advocating that women remain confined to their homes drew sharp condemnation from a Kerala state minister. Meanwhile, Taliparamba MLA T.K. Govindan declined to offer comments regarding criticism from Chief Minister Pinarayi Vijayan. Separately, residents of Tazhakkavayal in Kottayam expressed shock following the arrest of former resident Anila in California, United States, on charges relating to the murder of two Keralite women.

### International & Diplomatic Affairs

* **Prime Minister's Visit to Kyrgyzstan:** Prime Minister Narendra Modi paid floral tributes to Mahatma Gandhi in Kyrgyzstan during an official visit at the invitation of President Sadyr Zhaparov to participate in the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) summit scheduled from August 31 to September 1.
* **Disaster in Nepal:** Severe flash floods across Nepal have resulted in a death toll exceeding 900, with more than 4,000 individuals reported missing. Prime Minister Balendra Shah characterized the ongoing crisis as an "unimaginable and heartbreaking" disaster as rescue efforts continue.
