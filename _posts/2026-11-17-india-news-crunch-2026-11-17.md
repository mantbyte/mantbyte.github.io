# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National News and Events

## Diplomacy, Defence, and Geopolitics
Prime Minister Narendra Modi is set to meet U.S. President Donald Trump, Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi, and Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese in Manila for the first Quad Summit since 2024, held concurrently with the ASEAN Summit. Meanwhile, in international maritime developments, a missile attack targeted an LPG tanker carrying 24 Indian nationals in the Strait of Hormuz; all crew members were reported safe. 

In bilateral and regional matters, Pakistan Climate Minister Musadik Masood Malik’s visit to New Delhi for the SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum has renewed discussions surrounding India’s Pakistan policy and its implications for regional stability.

## Domestic Politics and Governance
Political developments saw Maharashtra politics under scrutiny as Rohit Pawar presented a 49-page slideshow questioning Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis’ links to VSR Ventures following a plane crash, while Uddhav Thackeray labeled Amit Shah’s unveiling of Ajit Pawar’s bust "unfortunate," claiming the BJP made Ajit Pawar's life miserable. In Uttar Pradesh, BJP chief Nitin Nabin arrived in Mathura for poll strategy meetings, welcomed by State party president Pankaj Chaudhary and state in-charge Vinod Tawde. 

Rahul Gandhi voiced criticisms on multiple fronts, slamming the BJP's reaction to his 'Mumma in Papa's lap' remark and questioning the Election Commission’s claim of a 'unanimous' Special Intensive Revision (SIR) decision ahead of a Supreme Court hearing. In Delhi, the government announced plans to open former Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal’s former official bungalow at 6, Flagstaff Road to public viewing. In legal updates, Bilkis Bano is set to challenge a Supreme Court order granting bail to two convicts on the grounds that the order was passed without hearing her, and the Supreme Court admonished Sanjay Raut over remarks made against the Chief Justice of India.

## Security, Weather, and Incidents
Anti-terror operations continued in Kulgam, where security agencies retrieved the body of an accomplice of LeT terrorist Latteef Bhat from an encounter site following the release of surveillance footage. On the weather front, Cyclone 'Akvan' is projected to intensify into a severe cyclonic storm by November 19, bringing light to moderate rainfall across Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Odisha, and West Bengal. 

Tragedy struck Uttarakhand as twin road accidents—including a passenger bus plunging into a 100-metre-deep gorge in Tehri Garhwal—left 12 people dead and 32 injured. In public health and safety, nearly 40 students of a KGBV school in Andhra Pradesh's Anantapur district fell ill after dinner, prompting an inquiry by the DEO. In Eluru district, villages remain on high alert after a tiger named 'Explorer' was spotted in fields, with a Pune rescue team en route to tranquilize and capture the animal. Additionally, Thiruvananthapuram has reported a dengue spurt, with the University of Kerala’s Karyavattom campus and the Cliff House compound emerging as local hotspots.

## Sports and Culture
In sports, the Indian cricket team is preparing for the Test series against New Zealand following their T20I and ODI series victories, facing a crucial test of character with World Test Championship (WTC) hopes hanging in the balance amid pace-friendly conditions. In the arts, composer Manikandan Ayyappa discussed the creative process behind his viral hit ‘Ithino Aadame...’ from the film *Disco*, which has surpassed 23 million views on YouTube.
