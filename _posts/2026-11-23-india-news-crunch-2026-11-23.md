# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National News and Events

## Foreign Policy and Bilateral Relations

Prime Minister Narendra Modi announced a major development package exceeding ₹15,000 crore for Nepal during the Nepal Prime Minister's visit to India. The diplomatic engagement yielded several key initiatives, including the establishment of an offshore IIT campus in Nepal, expanded educational scholarships for Nepali students, and the introduction of a new weekly train service, the Ayodhya Cantt-Janakpur Dham Saryu-Kamala Express, connecting 10 stations and scheduled for departure every Monday. Hailing the diplomatic ties, the visiting Prime Minister remarked that relations between the two nations stand "as firm as the Himalayas and as vast as the oceans."

## Judiciary and Legal Developments

The Supreme Court directed the Election Commission of India and the Calcutta High Court to increase the number of tribunals hearing appeals regarding West Bengal’s electoral lists, instructing them to prioritize and fast-track appeals filed by deleted voters. In a separate ruling, the Supreme Court strongly rebuked a judicial officer from Bihar for initiating an FIR against a Superintendent of Police over the registration of a police complaint, advising the official to cease acting arbitrarily. Meanwhile, the Calcutta High Court extended interim protection to Trinamool Congress (TMC) MP Abhishek Banerjee in six criminal cases until February 26, 2027, or until further orders.

## Regional Administration and Governance

West Bengal is set to consider a formal proposal during its upcoming assembly session from December 4 to 14 to officially rename the State to 'Paschim Banga' in English, aligning it with its native Bengali nomenclature. In southern governance, Lakshmi Aparna was sworn in as the Mayor of Guntur alongside JSP’s Rajesh as Deputy Mayor, with the leadership pledging enhanced accessibility and grievance redressal through coordination between officials and elected representatives. In educational administration, the NCERT announced it is launching an investigation into printing defects and blurred text in Class 10 textbooks following a Supreme Court lawyer's complaint, assuring strict action against responsible printers.

## Politics and Civil Society

Political discourse intensified as former Vice-President Hamid Ansari, alongside CPI(M) general secretary M.A. Baby and economist Prabhat Patnaik, spoke at a seminar titled ‘The new age of imperialism,’ expressing concerns over the alleged erosion of democratic institutions in India. In Kerala, the CPI(M) and Chief Minister Pinarayi Vijayan welcomed a Kerala High Court directive ordering special examinations for protesting Sanskrit University students from working-class backgrounds, framing the decision as a significant moral victory for students' democratic rights. Concurrently, political friction arose as the CPI(M) accused a political counterpart of violating the oath of office by threatening to deploy executive law enforcement instruments aggressively against political opponents.
