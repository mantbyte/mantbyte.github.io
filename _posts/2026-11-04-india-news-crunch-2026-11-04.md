# Cyclone Impact and Political Shifts in Andhra Pradesh

Cyclone Murasu has weakened into a depression, but its impact continues to be felt across coastal Andhra Pradesh. Heavy rainfall persists, with Edlapadu in Palnadu district recording a state-high of 269.45 mm in a 24-hour period. In response to massive inflows from the Keesara, Munneru, and Budameru streams, authorities have opened all 70 gates of the Prakasam Barrage, releasing 71,000 cusecs into the Krishna River. Irrigation Minister Ramanaidu has placed officials on high alert as the Nallamada and Parchur drains exceed maximum levels near Bapatla and Chirala.

The environmental crisis coincides with significant political developments in the state. In the Vijayawada civic polls, Jana Sena’s Vangaveeti Radhakrishna secured the Mayor’s office with a substantial margin of 1,30,912 votes. However, the results have drawn criticism from Andhra Pradesh Congress chief Y.S. Sharmila, who alleged that the ruling alliance misused official machinery and police forces to suppress the opposition. 

On the economic front, Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu has sought federal intervention regarding 150 chilli containers currently held at Colombo port due to moisture content issues. In a letter to Union Minister Piyush Goyal, Naidu requested immediate assistance for losses exacerbated by the back-to-back impacts of cyclones Arnab and Murasu.

# Judicial Oversight and National Law Enforcement

The Supreme Court of India is currently examining a challenge to Section 16 of the Chief Election Commissioner and Other Election Commissioners Act, 2023. The petition, filed by RJD MP Manoj Jha, questions the legality of providing blanket, lifelong immunity to top election officials. In a separate matter, the Delhi Police defended the imposition of 60-day "ex-parte" prohibitive orders in key capital zones before the Supreme Court, citing the need to prevent unrest following violent protests witnessed in New Delhi earlier this year.

In Mumbai, the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC) executed a demolition drive in Borivali, removing 27 structures. This action follows a Supreme Court order from earlier this month that froze the salary of the civic chief due to non-compliance with judicial directions. Meanwhile, the Enforcement Directorate (ED) conducted raids at 11 locations across Kerala and Tamil Nadu, including Kochi and Chennai. The searches target alleged money laundering linked to the BLM cooperative society and its founding member, R. Premkumar.

# Infrastructure, Public Policy, and Economic Adjustments

Public transport costs are set to rise in Karnataka as the state government approved fare hikes for the BMTC, KSRTC, NWKRTC, and KKRTC. While the revision is significant, it remains lower than the 45% increase originally recommended by the Public Transport Fare Regulatory Committee. In Kerala, Chief Minister Pinarayi Vijayan defended the government's decision to opt for a power purchase deal with GNA over a Himachal Pradesh offer, asserting the move avoided a ₹2,200 crore liability. He further dismissed opposition claims that the transfer of KSEB’s chairman was linked to these transactions.

In healthcare and urban heritage, Telangana Health Minister has directed that the Warangal Super Speciality hospital be fully equipped before its formal inauguration. In Tamil Nadu, the Chennai Metropolitan Water Supply and Sewerage Board is marking the centenary of its pumping station at P.S. Sivaswami Salai in Mylapore. Additionally, the Tamil Nadu government has sanctioned ₹1.3 crore for a comprehensive conservation roadmap for the Bengal fox and golden jackal, focusing on threat assessment and habitat distribution.

# Diplomatic Relations and Social Developments

On the diplomatic front, Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri is in Nepal for a two-day official visit. Discussions with Nepal’s Foreign Minister focused on bilateral ties and preparing for the upcoming visit of Nepalese Prime Minister Balendra Shah to New Delhi. In a tragic international update, the Indian embassy confirmed that a second Indian national has died following injuries sustained in the October 10 Riyadh airport attack, bringing the total death toll to 14.

Domestically, social and political tensions have surfaced in Kerala and Telangana. In Kerala, the suspension of 16 SFI activists at Sree Sankaracharya University of Sanskrit has sparked a row; the university cited a "mock funeral" held for Vice-Chancellor Ciza Thomas as grounds for the disciplinary action. In Hyderabad, police have arrested two individuals after a viral video showed women being slapped and threatened over allegations of religious conversion. Finally, in a gesture of political courtesy, Prime Minister Narendra Modi spoke with Sharad Pawar via telephone, advising the veteran leader to take complete rest following recent health concerns.
