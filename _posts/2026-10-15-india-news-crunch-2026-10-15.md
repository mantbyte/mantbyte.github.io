# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National News & Developments

### Protests, Political Tensions, and Scrutiny over Civil Liberties

The fallout from demonstrations against the Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) continues to reverberate across political and judicial corridors. In the Supreme Court, a bench is scheduled to consider petitions filed by Rahul Gandhi and leaders of the INDIA bloc regarding the Special Intensive Revision (SIR). Parallelly, Delhi Police Commissioner Anurag Kumar and Union Home Secretary Govind Mohan submitted an unconditional apology to the apex court following a contempt notice linked to the detention of demonstrators on October 10. In their submission, police maintained that detainees were provided proper meals and that restrictions were limited to cellular internet suspensions.

The political fallout deepened as the Aam Aadmi Party's Delhi leadership, led by Saurabh Bharadwaj, lodged a formal complaint alleging a "life-threatening" physical assault by police personnel during recent demonstrations, demanding an independent inquiry into criminal conspiracy. Meanwhile, in Delhi, Cockroach Janta Party founder Abhijeet Dipke announced a "Lehra Do Tiranga" gathering at Central Park, Connaught Place, for October 16. In Kerala, the Congress party filed a police complaint against BJP councillor R. Sreelekha over alleged derogatory remarks directed at Rahul Gandhi following his characterization of the Delhi protests. 

The demonstrations have also prompted contentious administrative action against actor Imran Khan. Mumbai Police instructed Khan to leave the country by October 28 following a complaint by former BJP MP Kirit Somaiya over his participation in the October 10 anti-CEC assembly at August Kranti Maidan. The incident has intensified the legal debate concerning the rights of Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) cardholders participating in domestic political protests. 

Beyond Delhi, public unrest surfaced in Punjab, where commercial markets and private schools remained closed across several districts in response to a "Punjab Bandh." The shutdown was called by the family of a murdered anti-drug activist, drawing joint support from the Congress, Shiromani Akali Dal, BJP, and Akali Dal Waris Punjab De. In the agricultural sector, sugarcane farmers announced they will maintain their ongoing demonstrations despite welcoming a newly formed Central committee, stating that protests will continue until the Fair and Remunerative Price (FRP) and Minimum Support Price (MSP) are revised.

### Judicial Rulings, Governance, and State Administration

In a landmark verdict, the Supreme Court ruled that individuals who convert from Hinduism to Islam are not automatically entitled to backward class reservation benefits. The top court upheld a High Court judgment that quashed a Tamil Nadu government notification attempting to grant backward-class quota privileges to such converts.

Institutional leadership also saw notable changes in the south. Rajendra Vishwanath Arlekar formally took charge as the full-time Governor of Tamil Nadu, concluding his interim tenure discharging dual duties while serving as the Governor of Keralam. 

In the Keralam Legislative Assembly, opposition lawmakers staged a walkout following an adjournment motion concerning hardships faced by farmers due to an Idukki District Collector order on dismantling fences in Chinnakkanal. Both the Forest Minister and Revenue Minister defended state actions, clarifying that enforcement actions are strictly limited to unauthorized and illegal structures. 

In Andhra Pradesh, the Vizianagaram district administration is mobilizing extensive logistics as dates for the historical Sirimanotsavam festival coincide with municipal mayoral elections on October 27 and 28. District authorities have instituted leave cancellations for essential administrative staff to manage crowd security and civic procedures concurrently.

### Economic Indicators, Corporate Investment, and Maritime Trade

In macroeconomic developments, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) expressed formal support for India's revised Gross Domestic Product (GDP) methodology. Krishna Srinivasan, Director of the IMF's Asia and Pacific Department, described the updated statistical framework as a "welcome development," noting that the methodology aligns directly with recommendations provided by the fund's statistics division.

In the financial markets, foreign investor participation surged as Fairfax pursued a $6 billion deal for IDBI Bank. The move underscores accelerating global institutional interest in acquiring prime domestic banking and financial assets.

Logistical growth remained strong on the eastern coast, where the Visakhapatnam Port Authority announced it had processed 50 million tonnes of cargo in 196 days for the 2026–27 financial year. The port achieved the 50-million-tonne benchmark 17 days faster than it did during the preceding fiscal cycle.

### Law Enforcement, Public Health, and Civic Updates

Enforcement agencies conducted targeted crackdowns against narcotics and food safety violations. In Vijayawada, a joint operation by the Railway Police and the EAGLE anti-narcotics wing intercepted the Bengaluru-Howrah Express, leading to the arrest of a woman and the confiscation of approximately 100 grams of MDMA. 

In Telangana, food safety task force TG SAFE intercepted an unhygienic consignment of khoya transported in non-food-grade plastic covers into Hyderabad without mandatory temperature-controlled refrigeration, initiating confiscation procedures.

In civil service and academic developments:
* **Administrative Appointments:** Deepika Agrawal, who transitioned from a corporate career to join the Indian Police Service, has assumed duties as Assistant Superintendent of Police (ASP) in Rewari, Haryana.
* **Higher Education:** The Indian Institute of Technology reopened registration for the GATE 2027 examination. Eligible candidates have been provided an extension window to submit their applications with an applicable late fee up to October 16.
* **Cultural Endowments:** The Sri Durga Malleswara Swamy Varla Devasthanam at Indrakeeladri received an offering valued at ₹75 lakh. A devotee from Srikakulam donated a 494-gram gold crown embedded with multi-colored stones to the presiding deity, Kanaka Durga.
