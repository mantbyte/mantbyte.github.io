# Mantbyte Daily Digest: Global News and World Events

## Conflict, Security, and Emergency Preparedness
In the Russia-Ukraine conflict, a Ukrainian drone attack resulted in five deaths in Russian-occupied Zaporizhia. Concurrently, Russian forces launched a ballistic missile attack targeting the Ukrainian capital of Kyiv, hitting a shopping centre and other infrastructure. 

In the Middle East, Israeli military actions in Gaza killed a senior education official—specifically Khaled Abu Nada, undersecretary of the Education Ministry—alongside seven other people, as confirmed by Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu. 

Meanwhile, European governments are increasingly urging citizens to prepare for emergencies following a wave of cyberattacks and bomb threats. These unexpected disruptions have pushed survivalism into the mainstream, with a growing number of citizens heeding official readiness warnings. In domestic safety news, the US Army reported that a controlled blast of explosives in Iowa was a "huge success," with officials stating that no lead has been detected in ongoing tests of the surrounding area.

## International Relations and Diplomacy
A new UK-EU treaty is set to feature a 10-year termination clause, with the full text and accompanying legislation to enact the bulk of the deal published recently. In Asia, diplomatic questions have arisen over whether Japan's Prime Minister Takaichi was snubbed at China’s major summit, as images highlighting differences in the welcomes afforded to leaders in Shenzhen prompted complaints from Japanese officials. North Korea has also expelled a Swiss diplomat from Pyongyang following a dispute centered around the Songdowon International Children’s Camp. 

On a cultural note bridging geopolitical divides, Pakistan’s first astronaut recited verses by the renowned Urdu poet Iqbal while aboard China's space station, marking the continuation of a unique orbital tradition connecting India and Pakistan beyond Earth.

## Human Impact, Health, and Environment

In Latin America, El Salvador’s President Nayib Bukele has gained widespread support among Haitians exhausted by gang violence, who hope he can replicate his anti-gang efforts in their country. 

Environmental monitoring has taken an innovative turn as researchers deploy seabirds equipped with GPS and audio gear to detect hidden fishing boats operating without tracking signals and assess environmental hazards. Meanwhile, extreme weather and industrial incidents are causing widespread concern. In Bangkok, authorities urged residents to evacuate after a large explosion rocked an eastern oil depot, injuring seven people and sending thick smoke into the sky. In India, soaring summer temperatures are severely impacting Delhi residents' health and ability to work, prompting researchers to experiment with solutions like specialized roof tiles to reduce indoor heat.
