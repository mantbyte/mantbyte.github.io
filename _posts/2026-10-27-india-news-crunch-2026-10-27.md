### Extreme Weather: Cyclone ‘Arnab’ Nears Landfall Along Eastern Coast

State administrations along India’s eastern seaboard have activated large-scale emergency protocols as Cyclone ‘Arnab’ intensifies over the Bay of Bengal. According to the India Meteorological Department (IMD), the severe cyclonic storm is projected to make landfall near Yanam along the Andhra Pradesh coast, bringing torrential rains and wind gusts reaching up to 150 kmph. 

In Andhra Pradesh, nearly 89,000 residents across vulnerable settlements in the Kakinada coastal belt are being evacuated to designated relief shelters. The railway network has experienced immediate disruptions, with 61 train services cancelled to mitigate operational risks. Precautionary measures have also been escalated in neighbouring Odisha. Further inland, the IMD has placed eight districts in Jharkhand under a 24-hour "yellow alert" beginning the morning of October 28, warning of widespread heavy rainfall triggered by the system’s outer bands.

### Judiciary, Regulation, and Institutional Shifts

The Supreme Court has issued notice to the Union Government in response to a petition filed by the Digital News Publishers Association challenging India’s new data protection legislation. The media collective argued before the apex court that the statutory privacy requirements could imperil standard newsroom operations and fundamentally disrupt news reporting. The bench has scheduled the matter for a detailed hearing on November 17.

In a separate matter before the Supreme Court regarding the Chief Election Commissioner (CEC), the Election Commission of India, CEC Gyanesh Kumar, and the two Election Commissioners submitted a joint affidavit. The submission cautions that any attempt to restrain the functioning of the CEC in relation to decisions taken during summary revision exercises runs contrary to constitutional principles.

In the corporate sector, Anup Bagchi has officially assumed charge as Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer of HDFC Bank. An ICICI Group veteran, Bagchi is the first external appointee to lead the private lender in its 30-year history, succeeding Sashidhar Jagdishan amid heightened regulatory scrutiny over governance frameworks.

At Jamia Millia Islamia in New Delhi, the university administration issued an ultimatum to protesting students, directing them to immediately vacate an on-campus demonstration site or face strict legal and disciplinary action.

### State Governance, Political Flashpoints, and Security Lapses

A major security breach unfolded in Tamil Nadu following a jailbreak at Theni, where 17 inmates managed to escape. Director General of Police (Prisons) Sandeep Rai Rathore attributed the escape to human error, noting that a comprehensive security audit of state penitentiaries had already been instituted following a recent escape at Puzhal prison. Tamil Nadu Law Minister C.T.R. Nirmal Kumar stated that law enforcement agencies are actively pursuing 13 absconding prisoners, while rejecting allegations of political vendetta concerning the arrest of former DMK Minister Anbil Mahesh. The incident triggered severe criticism from DMK leader M.K. Stalin against the Vijay-led administration over prison security failures.

In Kerala, legislative proceedings were disrupted as the Opposition staged a walkout, protesting against alleged police misconduct and what they termed a "dual justice system." The confrontation intensified after senior leader Ramesh Chennithala conceded that procedural lapses had occurred during the police investigation into the Cheranalloor car crash. Concurrently, the Kerala government announced the "Cyber Safe Kerala" initiative to address escalating online fraud. Chennithala highlighted that high standard literacy in the state has not translated into digital literacy, announcing plans for a real-time police dashboard to monitor complaints and FIRs.

In Jammu & Kashmir, Chief Minister Omar Abdullah expanded his Council of Ministers. Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha administered the oath of office to four new cabinet members: Ali Muhammad Sagar, Shameema Firdous, Mir Saifullah, and Altaf Kaloo, alongside Independent MLA Satish Sharma.

In the national capital, merchant associations representing 21 commercial markets across Old Delhi have petitioned authorities to postpone a planned Congress rally on November 1. Traders cautioned that the resulting transport bottlenecks could halt trade and cause estimated losses of up to ₹10,000 crore.

### Infrastructure, Welfare Policies, and Cultural Retrospective

* **Admission Discrepancies in Kerala:** Tribal and Dalit advocacy groups in Kerala have approached the state government alleging procedural discrimination against Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) applicants. The controversy centres on the seat-allotment sequencing implemented by the LBS Centre for Science and Technology during ongoing nursing admissions, which advocates claim disproportionately sidelines marginalised candidates.
* **Tamil Nadu Infrastructure Upgrades:** The Tamil Nadu Highways Department has initiated repair work across damaged corridors in Chennai. Concurrently, state BJP chief Nainar Nagenthran petitioned authorities for an immediate safety audit and emergency resurfacing of the Palar bridge, citing hazardous potholes that threaten commuters and school transport.
* **Culinary Legacy of Chef Damu:** Tributes were paid to the late Chef K. Damodaran (Chef Damu), who passed away after a career dedicated to regional heritage. A prominent culinary figure, Damodaran was credited with documenting forgotten Tamil recipes, popularising regional food traditions internationally, and educating aspiring chefs through extensive television broadcasts and literature.
