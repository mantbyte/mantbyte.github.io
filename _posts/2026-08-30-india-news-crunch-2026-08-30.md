# Mantbyte Daily Digest: Indian National News and Events

### Diplomacy and International Strategic Engagements

Prime Minister Narendra Modi has commenced a significant diplomatic tour of Central Asia, beginning with a two-day state visit to Uzbekistan. Following his stay in Tashkent, where he participated in a yoga session and held discussions with President Shavkat Mirziyoyev, the Prime Minister is scheduled to travel to Bishkek for the 26th Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) Summit. 

On the trade front, India is strengthening its economic ties with South America and Russia. The Union Government announced its commitment to concluding a Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA) with Chile within the year, aiming to bolster cooperation in technology and resilient supply chains. Simultaneously, India has emerged as a critical petrol supplier to Russia, a reversal of traditional roles necessitated by refinery disruptions in Russia. This follows India’s 2022 pivot toward Russia to meet its crude oil requirements.

Regionally, India continues its role as a first responder in South Asia. The Ministry of External Affairs confirmed that 68.5 tonnes of relief material, including a Bailey bridge and tunnel expertise, have been delivered to flood-hit Nepal to assist in recovery efforts.

### National Governance and Political Developments

In his latest *Mann Ki Baat* address, Prime Minister Modi issued a call to action for a "drug-free" India. He also highlighted the success of the PM SVANidhi Yojana, noting that the welfare scheme has empowered lakhs of women street vendors to expand their businesses and secure their financial futures.

Domestically, the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) is refining its political messaging. In Jammu and Kashmir, BJP spokesperson Altaf Thakur reiterated that while statehood would eventually be restored, the abrogation of Article 370 is permanent, citing the Supreme Court's validation of the 2019 decision. Meanwhile, internal BJP brainstorming sessions regarding "Gen Z" outreach have concluded that the demographic is not "homogenous," with leaders suggesting a shift toward broader terms like "youth" to better address diverse age groups.

Internal friction has surfaced within the opposition as well. Bihar Congress leaders are planning a protest in Delhi on September 8, seeking the removal of the state chief and in-charge over allegations of "disorder" during the Assembly elections. In Kerala, the CPI(M) is facing scrutiny in Kannur over alleged financial irregularities regarding an education society that has failed to produce a promised college after nearly a decade. 

In administrative news, Divya Mittal, a 2013-batch IAS officer in Uttar Pradesh, has resigned from her post, describing her 13-year career as an "unforgettable" journey.

### Public Safety, Law Enforcement, and Infrastructure

Security operations and legal rulings have sparked significant developments this week. In Patna, a violent confrontation along the Son River resulted in the arrest of one criminal after a gunfight in which 62 rounds were fired; two police personnel sustained injuries. In a separate matter, the Armed Forces Tribunal’s decision to clear the record of a Brigadier involved in the Poonch custodial deaths case has drawn outrage from Gujjar leaders. The case stems from a December 2023 ambush near Dera Ki Gali that resulted in the deaths of four soldiers.

Public safety concerns have been raised regarding food and aviation standards. Reports indicate that one in five food samples in India fails quality tests, yet conviction rates remain remarkably low. In the aviation sector, it was revealed that the DGCA had twice flagged safety concerns regarding Tecnam aircraft—primarily used for flight training—prior to a recent crash in Kanpur.

Infrastructure and transit incidents also marked the day. A gas pipeline leak along the Samruddhi Highway in Maharashtra caused significant traffic diversions before being resolved early on August 30. Tragic news arrived from Arakkonam, where a woman and her two young sons were killed after being struck by a train while crossing the tracks.

### Education and Employment Updates

The National Testing Agency (NTA) and other recruitment bodies have issued several updates for candidates. ISRO has opened applications for Scientist and Engineer SC posts across Electronics, Mechanical, and Computer Science disciplines. Regarding national examinations, the NEET-PG 2026 exam at a Jaipur center has been rescheduled to September 5 following a power outage. Additionally, candidates for the UGC NET June 2026 are awaiting certificate releases as the NTA prepares for re-examinations in three remaining subjects.
