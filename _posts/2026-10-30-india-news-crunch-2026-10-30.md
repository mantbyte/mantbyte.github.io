# Mantbyte Daily Digest: Key National Developments

### National Politics & Economic Debates

**Clash Over IDBI Bank Divestment Intensifies**  
A sharp political exchange has erupted over the proposed privatization of IDBI Bank. Congress leader Rahul Gandhi voiced strong opposition to the sale, asserting that public sector banks serve as essential instruments for nation-building rather than assets to be offloaded. He questioned the Union government’s strategy regarding employee safeguards and depositor protections. In response, Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman pushed back, stating that the institution had been handed over to the current administration in severe financial distress, justifying the corrective restructuring measures underway.

**PM Modi Launches Projects in Gujarat; Outlines Governance Resolve**  
Prime Minister Narendra Modi concluded a high-profile visit to Gujarat, where he inaugurated developmental initiatives exceeding ₹14,000 crore, disbursed the latest tranche of the PM-KISAN scheme, and visited the Bharat Bovine Breeding and Research Centre. Addressing a public gathering, the Prime Minister affirmed his commitment to serve the nation "until my last breath." The event also prompted political friction following the chanting of the slogan *"Modi nahi jayenge"*, which drew a dismissive response from the Congress party.

**INDIA Bloc Reschedules CEC 'Public Impeachment'**  
The opposition INDIA alliance postponed its planned "public impeachment" proceeding against Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. The demonstration, initially set to occur on the lawns of Congress President Mallikarjun Kharge's official residence in New Delhi, was rescheduled to October 31 following heavy localized rainfall that left the venue waterlogged.

**Diplomatic Engagements and Civic Controversies**  
External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar has arrived in the United States to represent India at the G20 Foreign Ministers’ meeting, joining deliberations centered on pressing geopolitical issues. Domestically, the Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) leveled corruption allegations against the Delhi Jal Board (DJB) regarding the procurement and application of chemical defoaming agents used to disperse persistent toxic froth on the Yamuna river.

### Judicial Rulings & Constitutional Affairs

**Kerala High Court Voids Employment Ban on Women in Toddy Shops**  
In a major constitutional ruling, the Kerala High Court struck down a provision of the Abkari rules that prohibited women from being employed in toddy shops. The court declared the restriction invalid, affirming that it contravened fundamental rights guaranteed under the Constitution, specifically the right to equality, freedom from discrimination, the right to practice any trade or profession, and the right to life.

**Madras High Court Bars Rallies Targeting Judges**  
A division bench of the Madras High Court, comprising Chief Justice Sushrut Arvind Dharmadhikari and Justice G. Arul Murugan, instructed the Tamil Nadu Police to deny permission for public demonstrations organized to target members of the judiciary. The direction was issued during the hearing of a public interest litigation filed against personal attacks directed at Justice G.R. Swaminathan. 

Separately, the same bench issued notices to the Union Government and Cockroach Janata Party co-convenor Saurav Das pursuant to a petition challenging Das's nationality.

### Corporate Financial Scrutiny & Law Enforcement

**Central Agencies Move on Banking and Insolvency Irregularities**  
The Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) has registered a case against Chandra Proteco and its directors over alleged bank fraud. The investigative agency cited systematic financial impropriety, including operating undisclosed accounts with non-consortium banks and devolving letters of credit via associated corporate entities.

Concurrently, the Enforcement Directorate (ED) executed searches across eight locations in West Bengal. The probe targets the suspected misuse of the corporate insolvency resolution framework involving Kohinoor Power, financial creditors, and associated entities.

**Look-Out Notices Issued in Overseas Theft Inquiry**  
Law enforcement authorities have issued look-out notices against four individuals in connection with the theft at the KMCC Qatar office. Investigators recently arraigned two additional suspects from Payyoli—Khamaruddeen and Razak—raising the total number of individuals formally accused in the case to eight.

### Regional Crises & Administrative Updates

**Cyclone Arnab Floods Coastal Andhra Pradesh; Rayalaseema Faces Agrarian Stress**  
The aftermath of Cyclone Arnab has displaced thousands in Andhra Pradesh, with approximately 10,000 residents moved to relief camps in the severely inundated town of Ichchapuram. Key logistics lines, including sections of National Highway-16, remain submerged, while preliminary agricultural assessments in Srikakulam district indicate crop damages spanning 21,519 hectares. In the state's Rayalaseema region, persistent rainfall deficits and rapidly falling water tables have triggered extensive distress among sweet lime growers, posing severe economic risks to local orchards.

**Weather Fluctuations in Telangana**  
Telangana documented widespread morning minimum temperatures between 18℃ and 25℃, with the Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC) area recording 22.2℃. Meteorological authorities have issued thunderstorm warnings across 11 districts spanning Friday afternoon through Saturday morning.

**Transition at APSRTC**  
The Andhra Pradesh State Road Transport Corporation (APRTC) observed a key leadership transition as Balasubrahmanyam took charge as Vice-Chairman and Managing Director, pledging to augment operational fleet quality and strengthen statewide public transit networks. The development comes as Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu and state officials mourned the demise of retired IPS officer and former APSRTC chief N. Balasubramanyam, commemorating his long-standing institutional service and administrative integrity.
