### Costco Expands into Healthcare with SCAN Group Partnership
In a notable strategic move, membership warehouse giant Costco is entering the healthcare sector by launching Medicare Advantage plans in collaboration with the SCAN Group. This partnership marks a significant diversification for the retailer, leveraging its trusted brand loyalty to capture market share in the senior healthcare space.
