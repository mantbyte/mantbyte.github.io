# Mantbyte Daily Digest: Indian National News and Events

## Law, Order, and Internal Security

A series of security lapses have been identified behind a recent burglary at a Tanishq showroom in Madurai, following revelations that an earlier attempt earlier this month went entirely unnoticed by showroom authorities. In law enforcement developments, a police personnel was shot at and injured in West Bengal while attempting to arrest a suspect, who subsequently managed to flee. Meanwhile, the CRPF terminated a jawan from service on November 13, 2026, following a formal inquiry into a social media post where the individual issued a death threat to their Commanding Officer. 

In political accountability and legal actions, Trinamool Congress (TMC) MP Abhishek Banerjee appeared at a police station for questioning over an FIR registered in May. The complaint targets a social media post allegedly aimed at Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar and Gujaratis during the Assembly polls. In Jharkhand, two individuals have been booked for allegedly forcing a minor domestic help employed by a Congress MLA to level false rape charges against him.

## Political Developments and Policy Shifts

National and regional political landscapes saw notable movement as BJP's Hardeep Singh Puri resigned as a Rajya Sabha member from Uttar Pradesh. Rumors surrounding an NCP merger were swiftly dismissed, with Sunetra Pawar issuing a firm "no" and Supriya Sule calling a "full stop" to the speculation. Political tensions also escalated in Karnataka, where Chief Minister DK Shivakumar hit back at Tamil Nadu Congress president Manickam Tagore over the Mekedatu Dam row, maintaining that the project is the state's right. In Telangana, BJP president N Ramchander Rao alleged that party leaders were placed under house arrest to prevent peaceful protests against a rally by Congress leader Rahul Gandhi.

On the policy and welfare front, Rahul Gandhi announced major campaign promises for gig workers in Telangana, including ₹5 lakh in accident insurance, ₹10 lakh in health insurance, and a social security contribution mechanism funded by a 1% transaction levy. In governance and institutional disputes, Higher Education Minister Roji M. John criticized MG University's new media access order as undemocratic and damaging to transparency. Meanwhile, the Thiruvananthapuram Corporation denied permission for a cultural event featuring singer Pushpavathy at Manaveeyam Veedhi, drawing accusations of curbing freedom of expression from activists.

## Infrastructure, Economy, and Public Health

In infrastructure and corporate expansion, V-Xpress is actively building key logistics hubs across Karnataka, targeting major cities like Mangalore, Davangere, and Gulbarga, alongside multiple industrial zones. In Kerala, the PWD received ₹2.68 crore funded by the Cochin Port Authority to fortify three rehabilitation sites designated for Vallarpadam project evictees. Aviation safety came into focus following an incident at Solapur Airport where a trainee aircraft skidded off the runway during takeoff, leaving the aircraft tilted forward with its nose resting on the ground.

In scientific and public health initiatives, the Central Food Technological Research Institute (CFTRI) introduced the NutriQA-2026 initiative. The program is designed to standardize micronutrient testing in fortified rice, aiming to reduce laboratory result variations and strengthen nationwide quality assurance. Additionally, Tamil Nadu marked Children's Day 2026 with a first-of-its-kind initiative launched by CM Vijay, distributing cakes, greeting cards, and sweet pongal to school students across the state.

## Foreign Affairs and Historical Discourse

On the diplomatic front, India extended its support to a United Nations General Assembly (UNGA) resolution concerning the permanent sovereignty of the Palestinian people. The resolution demands that Israel cease the exploitation, damage, and depletion of natural resources in the Occupied Palestinian Territory, which includes East Jerusalem. 

In historical and political discourse, an opinion piece by Shashi Tharoor shed light on Sonia Gandhi's personal history and the quiet rupture with Maneka Gandhi following Sanjay's death. Concurrently, Sonia Gandhi’s recent mentions of the Godhra riots drew public pushback, with an ex-CBI official, Asthana, publicly criticizing the remarks as creating doubt and being entirely wrong.
