# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National News and Events

## Politics and Governance

The political landscape saw significant shifts following organizational overhauls and internal dissent. BJP national president Nitin Nabin announced a fresh team of national office-bearers and executive members, marking a piecemeal reset for the party. Notably, Smriti Irani and Ram Madhav have made political comebacks, while Amit Malviya has stepped down from his long stint leading the BJP’s social media department, with Deepak Mhaskey taking over. Additionally, cricketer Yuzvendra Chahal was appointed as a special invitee to the BJP’s OBC Morcha executive committee. 

In regional politics, Telangana Congress found itself embroiled in a row after Chief Minister Revanth Reddy sparked controversy with a sharp swipe at dissenting MLAs, stating they were “bitten by dogs.” Meanwhile, an administrative blunder occurred in Uttar Pradesh when the police force mistakenly extended a guard of honour to AIMIM chief Asaduddin Owaisi in Saharanpur. The local police team had originally gathered at the circuit house to prepare for an expected Supreme Court judge's arrival, prompting authorities to order an immediate probe. In West Bengal, the Adani Group officially entered the state's power sector after Adani Energy Solutions Ltd. received a Letter of Award to supply 1,750 MW of Renewable Energy Round-the-Clock power, drawing calls for transparency from the ruling Trinamool Congress.

## Infrastructure, Transport, and Environment

Public transport and infrastructure projects yielded notable developments across various states. In a boost to eco-friendly and efficient transit, the installation of the i-ALERT system across 1,500 Ashok Leyland BS-VI buses for the RTC has resulted in substantial resource savings. The system has helped conserve 6,603 litres of high-speed diesel daily, amounting to nearly 1.98 lakh litres saved per month. 

In Telangana, infrastructure investments are yielding safety dividends; the National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) reported a 47% drop in accidents at identified black spots following an investment of ₹2,304 crore. Further development is underway in the state as the Telangana State Education Welfare Infrastructure Development Corporation (TGEWIDC) invited bids for works worth ₹480 crore to upgrade academic and student welfare facilities at Osmania University. In Kerala, however, administrative friction surfaced resulting in the suspension of an electricity engineer following a power cut during a function attended by State Electricity Minister Sunny Joseph.

## Crime, Justice, and Social Issues

The National Commission for Women (NCW) has taken *suo motu* cognisance of a media report detailing the rape and murder of a woman whose body was discovered inside a stationary train in Nagercoil four days after the incident. The commission has demanded the expeditious identification and apprehension of the culprits. 

In higher education, student protests continued at the University of Hyderabad. Agitating students are demanding the immediate termination of a faculty member over email communications sent between April and June 2026, which reportedly contained casteist slurs and derogatory remarks directed at former students from Scheduled Caste and marginalised communities. Meanwhile, in judicial outcomes, a court in Yadgir, Karnataka, sentenced a man named Abdul Nabi to life imprisonment for the murder of an 11-year-old boy.

## Defense, Border Affairs, and Opportunities

At the international border, Indian and Chinese military commanders held high-level talks in Arunachal Pradesh aimed at mitigating border tensions and preventing localized face-offs in the region. In diplomatic clarifications, Indian sources dismissed recent reports claiming that India had requested Nepal's airspace and military access for flights into Bagdogra, confirming that no such proposal exists.

On the professional and educational front, the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) announced its 2026 Paid Internship program, offering selected students a monthly stipend of ₹5,000, with applications closing on December 11, 2026.
