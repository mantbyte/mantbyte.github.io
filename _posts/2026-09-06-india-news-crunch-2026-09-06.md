# Political Developments and Policy Realities

### PM Modi Addresses SRCC Centenary; Opposition Trades Barbs
Addressing the centenary celebrations of Shri Ram College of Commerce (SRCC) in Delhi, Prime Minister Narendra Modi delivered a youth-centric address while sharply critiquing what he termed "Dimagi Naxals" (intellectual Naxals). In response to recent political rhetoric, Congress President Mallikarjun Kharge pushed back against the Prime Minister's barbs, particularly taking issue with Modi's "Naraz Fufa" jibe aimed at the Congress party, remarking that political adjectives used by the government continue to shift inconsistently. 

### Congress Restructures Leadership Ahead of Key Assembly Polls
In a significant organisational shakeup, the Congress leadership reshuffled key state in-charges. Sachin Pilot has been assigned poll-bound Punjab, stepping into a landscape marked by factional friction between former Chief Minister Charanjit Singh Channi and state party chief Amrinder Singh Raja Warring. Meanwhile, Randeep Singh Surjewala has been assigned charge of Gujarat, and Bhupesh Baghel has been reassigned to oversee Assam. Amid these political shifts, Bahujan Samaj Party (BSP) chief Mayawati warned against the political "hijacking" of issues affecting the youth, arguing that established political parties are co-opting genuine movements led by Gen-Z, students, and the unemployed.

### RTI Reveals Dormancy in Jan Dhan Accounts
A Right to Information (RTI) application has revealed that one in every four accounts opened under the flagship Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) is currently inoperative. While total accounts stand at more than 59 crore with deposits exceeding ₹3.15 lakh crore, approximately 5.72 crore accounts hold a zero balance, pointing to persistent structural challenges in sustaining grassroots financial inclusion.

### Administrative and Regional Politics in Andhra Pradesh
The Andhra Pradesh government has conceded to key demands by the Andhra Pradesh Non-Gazetted Officers' Association (APNGOs), approving additional surrender leave for police personnel and enhanced pensions for retirees. On the electoral front, the Jana Sena Party announced coordinators for local body polls across north Andhra Pradesh, tasking Naga Madhavi with Srikakulam and Vizianagaram as the party aims to establish a foothold in the local municipal and parishad tiers. In neighbouring administrative movements, Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath is slated to visit Nellore, Andhra Pradesh, to participate in the silver jubilee celebrations of the Swarna Bharat Trust.

# Public Safety, Law Enforcement, and Systemic Failures

### Spurious Liquor Tragedies Strike Madhya Pradesh and Tamil Nadu
Illicit alcohol distribution has triggered fatal outbreaks in central and southern India. In Madhya Pradesh’s Sagar district, a minimum of 12 people died and several others fell severely ill after consuming poisonous liquor across two villages in Banda tehsil. Authorities have deployed health teams to screen affected villages. The tragedy parallels devastating hooch incidents in Tamil Nadu, where twin tragedies claimed 22 lives and left 45 hospitalised. The deaths in Tamil Nadu occurred despite state claims that illicit brewing had been eradicated, exposing entrenched underground bootlegging syndicates.

### Police Escalate Charges in Jantar Mantar Assault Case
Following sustained protests by Citizens for Justice and Peace (CJP) activist Saurav Das and a minor student activist at Delhi's Parliament Street police station, the Delhi Police amended an FIR concerning an assault on the activist’s father. Investigators added charges under the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act and attempt-to-murder provisions, giving assurances that accused Swatantra Bhardwaj will be apprehended within 72 hours. 

### Healthcare Staff Targeted in Mumbai; Trial Delays Continue in Prominent Cases
In Maharashtra, hospital staff were assaulted on camera by Shiv Sena workers following an argument over billing disputes regarding medical treatment for 'Govindas' injured during a Dahi Handi festival. 

Separately, justice remains elusive eight years after chargesheets were submitted in the high-profile assassinations of journalist Gauri Lankesh and scholar M.M. Kalburgi, with both trials continuing to drag on as their death anniversaries passed this week.

### Police Blunder in Punjab Leads to Mistaken Cremation
In a stark procedural failure, a family in Punjab that had cremated a body handed over to them by local authorities received a phone call 15 days later informing them that their son, Ravi Kumar, was alive. Kumar had previously been detained by the Jalandhar Police in connection with a loitering case, revealing grave errors in identification protocols.

# Science, Technology, and Humanitarian Operations

### ISRO Advances Earth Observation Amid Workforce Privatisations Concerns
The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) successfully launched the EOS-05 Earth observation satellite into geosynchronous orbit aboard its GSLV-F17 launch vehicle. However, the operational milestone comes alongside rising domestic labour concerns; staff associations within the agency have formally sought clarity on the trajectory of commercialisation and privatization. The employee bodies have raised pointed questions regarding whether core functions—such as the design, realisation, integration, testing, and launching of PSLV, LVM3, SSLV, and future launch systems—will remain under ISRO's direct mandate.

### Industry Confronts Data Centre Thermal Limits
As infrastructure demands for advanced computing expand, industry analyses highlighting firms like Google and Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) demonstrate the severe physical limits of air-cooling large-scale facilities. Cooling a 1-gigawatt data centre purely through conventional air-based mechanisms has proven largely impractical. While liquid-cooling infrastructure demands steep upfront capital costs, relying entirely on air introduces heavy efficiency and performance penalties.

### Cross-Border Humanitarian Search in Nepal; Opposition Stance on Activists
In neighbouring Nepal, forensic authorities are relying on mass DNA testing as a measure of last resort to identify hundreds of recovered bodies following catastrophic flooding on August 26, attempting to bring closure to families of missing individuals. Back in India, Congress leader Alka Lamba affirmed that her party, alongside Leader of the Opposition Rahul Gandhi, stands in support of women and student activists—specifically mentioning Shaheen Khan, Nafisa Khan, and Akriti Chaudhary—seeking legal and systemic justice.
