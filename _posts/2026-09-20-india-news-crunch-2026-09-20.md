# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National News and Events

### Governance and Political Developments

The Election Commission of India (ECI) has issued notices to several high-profile figures, including Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta and former Chief Minister Arvind Kejriwal, as part of the Delhi Special Institutional Review (SIR). The ECI published a list of 33.1 lakh electors flagged for "logical discrepancies" or a lack of mapping. Other prominent names appearing on the list include former Vice President Jagdeep Dhankhar.

In Tamil Nadu, the administrative structure under the TVK regime is drawing scrutiny from bureaucratic circles. The experiment of appointing two advisers to work alongside the Chief Secretary and a team of four IAS officers is reportedly producing "mixed vibes" regarding the state's traditional governance hierarchy. Meanwhile, in Kerala, Minister K.M. Shaji is embroiled in controversy following the viral spread of photographs showing him using a "loaned" luxury car as an official vehicle, complete with the national flag.

In West Bengal, political tensions remain high as police arrested the Rejinagar bypoll candidate, following the recent arrest of Nandigram candidate Milan Pradhan. The bypolls, scheduled for October 6, were necessitated by vacancies left by Suvendu Adhikari and Humayun Kabir. On the policy front, a new report has highlighted a significant decline in academic freedom in India under the current administration, noting that India is among 50 countries where such freedoms have been "severely restricted" over the last decade.

### National Security and Legal Rulings

The Union Home Ministry has designated the network of Pakistani social media influencer Shahzad Bhatti as a terror group. Bhatti, known as the "digital recruiter," has allegedly conducted campaigns to establish fear and influence across 14 Indian states. In another security development, Punjab Police confirmed that the accused in the murder of a PCR constable, Harjit Singh, was killed in an encounter. Authorities are currently investigating potential Pakistani terror links associated with the suspect.

In a significant legal ruling, the Allahabad High Court has directed the Uttar Pradesh Police to ensure the safety of a same-sex couple in a live-in relationship. Citing Supreme Court precedents, the court affirmed that living together by choice is a fundamental right for adult citizens. 

In criminal investigations, influential industrialist ‘Gem Granite’ Veeramani is facing a widening probe under the POCSO Act following allegations of paedophilia. In Pune, details have emerged in a murder case involving Siya Goyal, who allegedly used a fake Instagram ID to lure her husband to Lohagad Fort before he was tailed and killed. Additionally, tragedy struck Narela, where a Delhi Police head constable and his wife were found dead in their flat, which was locked from the inside.

### Industry, Technology, and Education

Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw has cautioned the semiconductor industry regarding potential cyberattacks and disruptions. As India pushes to move beyond chip packaging into a broader domestic supply chain, Vaishnaw emphasized the need for robust security to protect the nation's growing technological infrastructure. In the eastern region, the West Bengal government has launched the ‘Seva First Innovation Challenge’ portal to solicit grassroots solutions for healthcare, agriculture, and waste management.

In the education sector, candidates are awaiting the provisional answer key and response sheets for the NEET PG 2026, which are expected to be released shortly. Simultaneously, the Education Ministry has announced a national-level Solid Waste Management Quiz for students in Classes 6 to 12, running from September 17 to October 17. In Andhra Pradesh, the revival of the Veligonda project offers new hope for regions long plagued by drought and fluoride contamination.

### Notable Passings and Public Engagements

The journalism community is mourning the sudden passing of senior journalist Payal Mehta at the age of 46. Mehta, who covered Parliament and the BJP for years, passed away in Mumbai after developing sudden health complications. Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Union Minister Amit Shah were among the many leaders who offered their condolences, praising her dedication to the profession.

In public engagements, Nara Lokesh addressed students during the ‘Bharateeyam’ programme, urging them to face failures with determination. He encouraged the youth to view soldiers as their "real heroes" and to maintain courage in their academic and personal pursuits.
