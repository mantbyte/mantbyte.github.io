# Legislative Turmoil and Regional Political Friction

The Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly witnessed significant disruption this week as Chief Minister Vijay launched a pointed critique of the DMK leadership. The Chief Minister’s remarks, which touched upon the historic Sarkaria Commission report and the Veeranam project, prompted a fierce reaction from DMK lawmakers. The opposition staged a sit-in protest demanding the expunction of the CM’s comments regarding the commission and the Maintenance of Internal Security Act (MISA), eventually leading to their eviction from the chamber. Simultaneously, Minister Aadhav Arjuna moved a privilege motion against E.V. Velu, alleging the irregular issuance of work orders totaling ₹2,000 crore, which he claimed would burden future budgets.

In Telangana, the Assembly’s monsoon session faced similar instability. Proceedings were adjourned for a second consecutive day following heated exchanges over the use of "unparliamentary" language directed at the Speaker. Additionally, members raised concerns regarding "indecent" behavior targeted at BRS women MLAs, stalling legislative business.

In the Northeast, Deputy Leader of the Opposition in the Lok Sabha, Gaurav Gogoi, raised alarms over the removal of a gazette notification concerning Kaziranga animal corridors from Assam’s official website. Gogoi highlighted that nine corridors, including Panbari—recently under scrutiny due to reports of rare earth elements and land sales—are now missing from the digital record.

# Electoral Integrity and Governance Challenges

Significant concerns regarding the integrity of electoral rolls have emerged in Delhi and Karnataka. In Delhi, official data revealed that 13 lakh voters were deleted during the pre-SIR (Special Internal Revision) mapping phase, the highest among Phase-3 states. Officials attributed the mass deletions to "continuous updation," though no separate list detailing the reasons for these removals has been published.

In Karnataka, the SIR process has faced criticism for logistical failures and controversial notices. Reports indicate that notices were issued only in Kannada, with some hearings scheduled for midnight, leaving many electors struggling to navigate the process. High-profile cases have fueled the debate, including a notice issued to Padma Shri awardee Harekala Hajabba and concerns raised by the Congress party regarding the grandson of a former MP whose name was flagged despite his family’s records being in order.

State-level governance also saw active interventions in Kerala and Uttar Pradesh. The Kerala Civil Supplies department has mandated that District Collectors ensure multiplexes provide free drinking water following complaints of overpricing. Meanwhile, Kerala’s Power Minister Sunny Joseph warned of impending power cuts due to rising temperatures and low hydel generation caused by a weak monsoon. In Uttar Pradesh, Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath directed officials to provide immediate assistance to flood-affected families and ordered a safety audit of infrastructure along the Mandakini river to remove encroachments.

# National Relations and Public Safety

On the diplomatic front, Prime Minister Narendra Modi hailed the strengthening of India-France relations, noting that the strategic partnership has reached "new heights." The Prime Minister confirmed that 2026 will be celebrated as the "India-France Year of Innovation," emphasizing the cultural and political foundations of the bilateral tie.

Domestic safety and law enforcement remained in focus with several critical incidents. In Kochi, police are considering charges under the Kerala Anti-Social Activities (Prevention) Act (KAAPA) against a private bus employee, Muhammed Ansar, following allegations of motorist intimidation and the assault of an on-duty officer. In Punjab, the death by suicide of a Sangrur resident, Gulzar Singh, has sparked political controversy after he reportedly blamed an AAP minister in connection with drug-related issues he had raised. 

Public safety risks associated with social media trends were also highlighted by an incident where a man fractured both legs after jumping 30 feet into a dam to record a "reel."

# Media and Social Discourse

In the cultural sphere, the Kannada film industry is preparing for the release of *Video*, a found-footage horror drama directed by Srinidhi and Dheekshith, set to premiere on September 11. In social commentary, actor-politician Kangana Ranaut weighed in on the ongoing controversy involving actor Trisha, urging a deeper societal introspection regarding the treatment of women in the wake of recent events in Tamil Nadu.
