# Daily Digest: Cyclone Akvan Threatens Odisha and Andhra Pradesh, Political Developments, and Economic Updates

## Natural Disasters and Public Safety

Odisha and Andhra Pradesh are on high alert as Cyclone ‘Akvan’ approaches the coast, prompting the evacuation of over one lakh people. The India Meteorological Department (IMD) reports that the landfall corridor, anticipated around noon on November 20, stretches from Narsapur in Andhra Pradesh to Puri in Odisha, with a red alert issued for three Andhra Pradesh districts. 

In response to the cyclone, the Odisha government has closed major coastal tourist sites, including Chilika Lake, Puri beaches, Bhitarakanika, and the Nandankanan Zoological Park. Agricultural authorities are taking precautionary measures; the Acharya N.G. Ranga Agricultural University (ANGRAU) has issued crop-wise advisories urging farmers to protect standing crops and harvested produce through proper drainage and safe storage.

## Politics and Governance

Political developments made headlines across multiple states. In Tamil Nadu, TVK MLA V.S. Babu, who previously defeated M.K. Stalin in Kolathur, has passed away. Meanwhile, the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) openly mocked Rahul Gandhi’s recent remarks in the U.S., suggesting that the Congress party should consider sending an AI-generated version of the leader abroad since his speeches are routinely repetitive. The Congress party separately celebrated the birth anniversary of Indira Gandhi, with leaders praising her leadership and legacy.

In Karnataka, Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar inaugurated 16 Indira Canteens, emphasizing that development only carries meaning when the poor have food on their plates. He instructed municipal bodies to monitor food quality and mandated central CCTV surveillance for the canteens. In Jammu and Kashmir, IAS officer Sajjan Singh Yadav has assumed charge as the new Chief Secretary, stepping into a role that serves as the primary interface between the Lok Bhavan and the Chief Minister’s office. 

In law enforcement and legal news, the Tolichowki Police Inspector was caught taking a ₹20,000 bribe to permit a hotel to operate beyond 1 a.m. In Mumbai, the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC) initiated the demolition of a C-1 category dangerous building linked to Dawood Ibrahim. Additionally, a U.S. federal court ordered the delivery of summonses to Ajit Doval and R.A. Goel through Delhi courts, citing the Hague Conventions and the Indian Code of Civil Procedure.

## Crime, Economy, and Infrastructure

In major enforcement actions, the Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI) seized over 70 kg of heroin valued at ₹700 crore during a raid at an ice-manufacturing factory in Madhavaram, Chennai. In Andhra Pradesh, the CID arrested a man linked to a ₹7 crore government job fraud case in Guntur, where job aspirants were defrauded with false promises of public employment.

On the economic front, the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) informed its trustees that no legal route currently exists to recover ₹1,800 crore from Reliance Capital, a matter scheduled for discussion during the Central Board of Trustees meeting. Prime Minister Narendra Modi invited suggestions from India Inc regarding further economic reforms, challenging industry leaders on whether reduced corporate taxes have translated to increased employee strength and higher wages. Contributing to sustainable industry practices, Adani Krishnapatnam Port inducted 288 electric vehicles into its fleet, with another 193 expected to join soon.

In transport and logistics, Hyderabad is set to debut the Rail Parcel App, which enables users to book parcel shipments online, complete digital payments, and access doorstep services without visiting physical railway offices. 

## Society and Culture

In the cultural sphere, filmmaker and author Samina Mishra continues to explore themes of borders, identity, and citizenship through children's eyes, using art and storytelling to initiate broader conversations on society, equality, and constitutional values. Meanwhile, Prime Minister Narendra Modi engaged with popular culture by referencing the *Grand Theft Auto VI* craze on social media, highlighting India’s developmental "level up" since the release of *GTA V*.
