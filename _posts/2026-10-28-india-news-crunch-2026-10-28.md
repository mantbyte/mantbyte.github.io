# National Policy, Defense, and Economic Governance

In a significant update on India’s manufacturing sector, Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw reported that the government has collected ₹3.25 lakh crore in taxes against a payout of ₹19,000 crore under the mobile Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme. Official data indicates that India exported mobile phones worth ₹2 lakh crore in fiscal year 2025, while imports have become nearly negligible.

On the strategic front, Defense Minister Rajnath Singh and U.S. Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth reviewed the progress of the India-U.S. defense framework pact. Inked in October last year, the agreement serves as a roadmap to bolster bilateral defense partnerships and technological cooperation.

In the judiciary, the Supreme Court has agreed to hear a challenge regarding the definition of "industry" under the Industrial Relations Code (IRC) 2020. The petition, filed by the All India Trade Union Congress Tamil Nadu, argues that the current definition is "deliciously vague." Meanwhile, in administrative changes, the Telangana government reshuffled 12 IAS officers, including moving Jayesh Ranjan to the Environment, Forests, Science & Technology department and posting Vikas Raj to the Energy department.

In Ladakh, Chief Secretary reviewed the preparedness for the upcoming Panchayat and Municipal polls scheduled for November 21. Polling will be held for 193 panchayats, with the process expected to conclude by the end of the month.

# Regional Politics and Infrastructure Debates

The Telangana Congress is facing logistical hurdles ahead of its planned protest in Delhi on November 1. Party leaders alleged that trains have not been allocated for the mobilization of 10,000 cadres. Rising airfares on the Hyderabad–Delhi sector have further complicated the drive.

In Tamil Nadu, the state-owned dairy cooperative Aavin has increased the prices of approximately 25 milk-based products. Ghee prices rose by ₹80 per kilo, while curd increased by ₹10 per kilo. The move drew sharp criticism from opposition leaders Edappadi K. Palaniswami and T.T.V. Dhinakaran, with Palaniswami likening the government’s actions to those of a "corporate villain."

In Kerala, technocrat E. Sreedharan urged the state government to ignore objections from a technical panel regarding the proposed high-speed rail project. Sreedharan claimed the panel failed to study the proposal properly and encouraged the government to proceed with the infrastructure development.

# Law Enforcement and Public Safety

The Kochi City Police Commissioner, Kaliraj Mahesh Kumar, has been removed from his post following an investigation into a luxury car crash in Cheranalloor involving a judge’s minor son. This follows the suspension of four officers from the Cheranalloor police station. In a related case, a district court rejected the bail pleas of four accused, including prime suspect Suhail Sajeev, involved in a luxury car crash in Nedumbassery.

West Bengal reported its second police "encounter" within 12 hours. Samaul Sheikh, an accused in the murder of a BJP worker, was shot dead in a gunfight with police. This follows a similar incident where Prahlad Das, accused of the rape and murder of a minor, was killed in North 24 Parganas.

In Navi Mumbai, a fire gutted four huts, causing severe damage to household belongings and nearby vehicles. While the cause of the fire is under investigation, no casualties were reported.

# Environment, Health, and Notable Events

Cyclone Arnab made landfall on the Andhra coast as a "very severe" cyclonic storm before weakening into a severe cyclonic storm. Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu confirmed there was no loss of life. However, the storm disrupted maritime activities, with fishermen in Thoothukudi advised to stay away from the sea due to rough conditions.

A new report has highlighted that India remains among the top countries with the highest heatwave exposure for infants and older adults. While global heatwave person-days decreased slightly from 2024’s record highs, the vulnerability of these demographics remains a critical concern.

In sports, the athletic community is mourning the death of 42-year-old Neeraj Yadav, an Asian Para Games gold medallist. Yadav, who won three golds in 2018, had recently been serving a doping suspension.

On a positive note, Indian Captain Smit Machchhar and his flydubai crew were honored with medals for bravery by UAE President Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan following their response to a mid-air attack. Finally, in cultural news, the settlement of Mudalur in Tamil Nadu is being recognized for its historical significance as the first exclusive Christian settlement in the region, established in 1799.
