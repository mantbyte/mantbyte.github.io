# National Security and Defence Strategy

Prime Minister Narendra Modi, speaking during his 'Mann Ki Baat' address, reaffirmed India's "zero tolerance" policy against terrorism, citing the 2016 surgical strikes as a definitive example of the nation's counter-terrorism posture. Reflecting on the anniversary of the September 28-29 operations, the Prime Minister noted that the strikes on terror hideouts across the Line of Control followed the Uri Army camp attack and demonstrated India’s willingness to take bold action.

In the realm of future readiness, the NDTV Defence Summit 2026 featured Deputy Chief of Air Staff Tejpal Singh, who discussed the transition toward fifth-generation aircraft and beyond. This focus on modernization is echoed in strategic analyses of "Operation Sindoor," which highlights the necessity for India to prepare for the "wars of tomorrow." Lessons from the operation, which included strikes on sites such as Bahawalpur in Pakistan, are being used to refine current military doctrines.

# Political Contention and Electoral Integrity

A major political row has erupted in West Bengal as Leader of the Opposition Suvendu Adhikari accused Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee of being responsible for the deletion of 27 lakh names from the state’s electoral rolls. Adhikari claimed the deletions were executed by judicial officers following Supreme Court directions in a case initiated by Banerjee herself, dismissing allegations that the BJP or the Election Commission were involved.

Simultaneously, the Election Commission of India is facing internal scrutiny. Commissioners Sukhbir Singh Sandhu and Vivek Joshi, appointed under the 2023 law, have reportedly raised questions regarding centralized control and changes to electoral rolls. 

In other regional political developments:
* **Tamil Nadu:** DMDK’s Vijaya Prabhakaran has announced the withdrawal of an election petition against Congress MP Manickam Tagore. The petition had previously alleged that DMK ministers exerted undue pressure on election officials during the 2024 Lok Sabha polls.
* **Andhra Pradesh:** Sachivalayam employees organized the "Chalo Vijayawada" protest at Dharna Chowk. Despite police attempts to prevent the gathering through preventive custody at transit hubs, agitators continued their demonstration.
* **Kerala:** IUML leader and Minister K.M. Shaji defended his use of a luxury car as an official vehicle, dismissing media criticism as a narrative fueled by the CPI(M).
* **Punjab:** A dispute over government bungalow allotments has surfaced between Congress MLA Sukhpal Singh Khaira and AAP leader Gayatri Bishnoi, highlighting ongoing friction between the two parties.

# Public Safety and Law Enforcement

The death toll from a militant firing incident in Manipur’s Senapati district has risen to three following the death of an injured victim in the hospital. The attack occurred shortly before the United Naga Council suspended a four-month-old economic blockade. 

In Tamil Nadu, Chief Minister Vijay marked the first anniversary of the Karur stampede, a tragic crowd crush at a political rally that claimed 41 lives. While the CBI continues to investigate delays in the case, the state government has issued a controversial order exempting the Public (Law and Order) Department from the Right to Information (RTI) Act. This exemption covers sensitive subjects such as caste clashes, NSA detentions, and deaths in police custody.

Other notable incidents include:
* **Mumbai:** Authorities are investigating a suspected chemical leak in a public toilet that resulted in the deaths of two men due to suffocation.
* **Guwahati:** The father of a 22-year-old student found dead in a homestay has alleged she was gang-raped and murdered, prompting a high-level investigation.
* **Madhya Pradesh:** Oustees of a dam project have entered their 16th day of protest. Despite heavy rain and waterlogging that has inundated homes and damaged property, the protesters refuse to vacate.

# Institutional Updates and Public Services

The banking sector is bracing for a scheduled three-day strike starting tomorrow. While physical branch operations are expected to be significantly disrupted, banks have indicated that digital and self-service facilities, such as ATMs and online banking, should remain operational.

In Karnataka, the Uttaradi Math has issued a statement confirming that their seer is out of danger following an SUV accident on the Pune-Bengaluru National Highway. The accident occurred while the seer was traveling from Dharwad to Savanur.
