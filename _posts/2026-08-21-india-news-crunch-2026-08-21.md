# National Governance and Institutional Reforms

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) commenced the Civil Services Mains 2026 examinations today, beginning with Paper-I (Essay) at 9 AM. In a move toward greater transparency, the commission has announced that a question objection window will open for candidates to raise concerns regarding the paper following the conclusion of the exams.

In a significant administrative appointment, former ISRO Chief S. Somnath, who retired from the space agency in January 2025, has been appointed as a part-time member of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Board. Meanwhile, the Supreme Court has raised sharp concerns regarding the Uttar Pradesh Gangsters Act, stating the law is prone to misuse. The apex court specifically questioned the validity of "gang charts" and the practice of prolonged detention without clearly defined offenses.

On the economic and infrastructure front, the Union government has authorized the duty-free import of 10 lakh tonnes of raw sugar through October 31 to stabilize domestic prices. To further curb inflation, stockholding limits have been imposed on bulk consumers using more than 10 tonnes of sugar monthly. In Bihar, Union Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan announced that over 11.18 lakh permanent houses will be constructed under the Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana-Gramin (PMAY-G), with 2.35 lakh units already seeing completion ceremonies for the 2026-27 financial year.

# Political Friction and Legislative Developments

Political tensions have escalated in Rajasthan as the state prepares for local body elections across 309 urban areas. While the model code of conduct has come into force, the Congress party has launched a dedicated panchayat task force campaign. Simultaneously, Congress MLAs boycotted the first day of the Rajasthan Assembly’s monsoon session, staging a dharna to demand the resignation of Education Minister Madan Dilawar over controversial school entry restrictions.

At the national level, Congress MP Shashi Tharoor has criticized the new Parliament building, describing it as a "soulless conference centre" that lacks intimacy. Tharoor argued that the physical distancing within the hall serves as a metaphor for the current polarization in Indian politics. In a separate internal directive, Rahul Gandhi has instructed the Congress party to closely monitor the social media positions taken by its leaders to ensure alignment with party stands.

In Uttar Pradesh, internal party friction surfaced as BJP MLA Jai Devi Kaushal alleged she faced caste-based discrimination during a temple event. The legislator has called upon Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath to take formal notice of the incident.

# Public Safety, Social Welfare, and Environmental Concerns

A series of reports have highlighted critical issues in public safety and social infrastructure. In Telangana, investigative findings suggest that state prisons, intended for reform, are instead serving as incubators for criminal partnerships, allowing habitual offenders to forge bonds that persist after their release. In a tragic incident of corporal punishment, a six-year-old student died after being slapped by a teacher; police have registered a case and launched a full investigation.

Environmental health has become a focal point in Visakhapatnam, where residents of the One Town area report being "choked" by persistent black dust. The pollution, which settles on homes and streets daily, has sparked mounting health concerns among the local population.

Conversely, community-led initiatives are offering signs of social healing. In Muzaffarnagar, philanthropist Ameer Ahamed is working to empower Muslim youth by integrating modern education with theological values to foster harmony. Similarly, in Pulwama, the rise of night-time cricket is being credited with helping the district heal from years of turmoil, providing a semblance of normalcy and nightlife for the local community.

# Regulatory Updates and International Relations

The Centre has issued a strict directive to telecommunications operators, barring them from issuing new SIM cards to subscribers who already hold nine mobile connections. This move is aimed at tightening security and managing the distribution of mobile resources.

In international affairs, Prime Minister Narendra Modi met with Japanese Defence Minister Shinjiro Koizumi in Delhi. The meeting focused on strengthening the strategic partnership between India and Japan, with both leaders emphasizing the need for increased bilateral cooperation in defense and regional security.
