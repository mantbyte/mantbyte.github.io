# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National News and Events

## Infrastructure, Transport, and Technology
Prime Minister Narendra Modi visited Bengaluru to inaugurate and flag off key infrastructure projects, including the elevated stretch of the Namma Metro Pink Line and the Yeshwantpur-Madgaon Vande Bharat Express. During his visit, the Prime Minister also inaugurated the golden jubilee celebrations of Prashanti Kutiram and attended events marking the birth centenary of former RSS general secretary H.V. Sheshadri, noting that yoga has become a global gateway to understanding India.

In the aviation sector, a global Microsoft outage disrupted email support services for major carriers IndiGo and Air India, though both airlines confirmed that alternative customer support channels remained operational. Meanwhile, the Central Consumer Protection Authority (CCPA) slapped a ₹10 lakh fine on ride-hailing firm Ola and ordered the immediate discontinuation of its pre-ride tipping feature, ruling that prompts encouraging users to tip for faster rides created a misleading impression. In Karnataka, an accident at Sheel Phata involving a bus and multiple vehicles left 12 persons injured, with local and highway police responding to the scene alongside the Help Foundation.

## Governance, Policy, and Regional Developments
In policy news, the Citizens for Fraternity Forum urged the Union Government to issue a public assurance delinking welfare benefits from electoral-roll status, calling for adequate safeguards for recipients. In Karnataka, prominent theatre personality Prasanna resigned from the state’s special cell to promote rural industries, citing policy grievances that include the closure of the Handloom Development Corporation and the demolition of the Gandhi Ashram at the Badanavalu Khadi Kendra. Additionally, the Karnataka government announced a three-month pilot for a mid-life women's health programme in Dakshina Kannada targeting women aged 35 and above, with actor Ramya named brand ambassador.

On the diplomatic front, Union Minister Gajendra Shekhawat announced that China, Kazakhstan, Iran, and several other nations have confirmed their participation in the upcoming Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) Civilisational Forum at New Delhi's Bharat Mandapam, adding that he remains hopeful Pakistan will also attend. Meanwhile, senior Congress leader Shashi Tharoor alleged that 115 speakers scheduled for the Bharat Summit had their visas cancelled and were barred entry to India.

## Security, Law, and Investigations
Security forces launched a massive cordon and search operation following an attack on a CRPF post, picking up several suspects for questioning. In the national capital region, two suspects allegedly involved in an attack on a President's Bodyguard jawan were arrested following a police encounter near Budhpur railway station. In Bihar's Madhubani district, the dead body of an Assistant Sub-Inspector (ASI)—who had allegedly opened fire with his service pistol after being cornered at a woman's house, leaving one dead—was recovered, with the exact cause of death still under investigation. Academic circles were shaken as a second-year undergraduate student at IIT Roorkee allegedly died by suicide on campus, leaving behind a recovery note while police probes remain under way.

In state-level law enforcement, the Ernakulam Rural police in Kerala booked 17,181 motorists for traffic violations over an 11-day period. The Kozhikode-Wayanad tunnel project in Kerala remains stalled despite the submission of required documents, raising safety and durability concerns over the 110-metre stretch already completed.

## Political Developments and Controversies
Political discourse heated up as Congress leader Rahul Gandhi alleged that his phone was tapped and doubled down on his debate challenge to Prime Minister Modi. In Kerala, the CPI(M) announced plans to bolster its legal cell to counter what it termed a rising volume of "fake news and misinformation" targeting the party. A separate political controversy erupted over a "mock funeral" row at a Sanskrit varsity, where Kerala Opposition Leader V.D. Satheesan and Chief Minister Pinarayi Vijayan criticized former Governor Arlekar and the Vice-Chancellor, alleging a conspiracy to defame SFI activists over fabricated claims of a mock funeral. Concurrently, RSS chief Mohan Bhagwat emphasized during an award ceremony that service is a societal responsibility rather than a profession, aimed at alleviating human suffering.
