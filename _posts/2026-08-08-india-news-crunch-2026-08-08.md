# Daily Digest: Indian National News & Events

## Judicial Rulings & Legal Frameworks

### Supreme Court Quashes Post-Facto Environmental Clearance Guidelines
The Supreme Court has quashing the Centre’s 2021 Office Memorandum regarding ex post facto environmental clearances. The apex court ruled that any future amnesty mechanisms under the Environment (Protection) Act must strictly derive from statutory provisions rather than executive memorandums.

### Apex Court Rethinks Gender Sensitivity via Expert Handbook
The Supreme Court is updating its framework on gender sensitivity through a new handbook. The document was prepared by an expert committee led by former Supreme Court judge and current Director of the National Judicial Academy (NJA) in Bhopal, Justice Aniruddha Bose, aimed at addressing gender biases within legal discourse.

### High Courts Deliberate Menstrual Leave as National Framework Remains Absent
With both the Kerala and Madras High Courts evaluating petitions regarding mandatory menstrual leave, current data shows only five Indian states have implemented formal menstrual leave policies. India currently lacks a uniform national law governing workplace leave for menstruation.

### Tamil Nadu Welcomes Verdict on Super-Speciality Medical Seats
The Tamil Nadu Health Minister praised a recent Supreme Court verdict regarding super-speciality medical seat admissions, characterizing the judgment as "historic" for state medical education governance.

## State Governance & Political Developments

### Telangana Announces Foreign Study Tours for Government Teachers
Telangana Chief Minister A. Revanth Reddy announced that the state government will sponsor annual foreign study tours for government school teachers. Highlighting state expenditure metrics, the Chief Minister noted that Telangana allocates approximately ₹1.08 lakh annually per student in government schools, compared to the average ₹50,000 spent by parents choosing private education, prompting a call for introspection on public school enrollment.

### Leadership Shift in Kerala Police Following High-Level Suspension
S. Sreejith, a 1996-batch IPS officer, has emerged as the leading contender for the post of Director General of Police (DGP) in Kerala. The development follows the suspension of senior officer M.R. Ajith Kumar. Sreejith stands as the next senior-most officer available in the state cadre, given that officer Suresh Raj Purohit is currently serving on Central deputation.

### Protests in Delhi Over Alleged Exam Paper Leaks in Punjab
Members of the Delhi BJP held demonstrations near the Aam Aadmi Party (AAP) headquarters at Ferozshah Road, demanding the resignation of Punjab Education Minister Harjot Singh Bains. Protesters cited allegations involving six separate exam paper leaks during the AAP administration's four-and-a-half-year tenure in Punjab.

### Diplomatic Objection Raised Over Foreign Media Terminology
The Indian Embassy in the United States publicly challenged a headline published by *The New York Times*, labeling it "misleading." The objection was raised after the American news outlet referred to Pakistan-Occupied Kashmir (PoK) as "Pakistani Kashmir."

### Additional State Developments
* **Electoral Revision Assault:** Authorities registered an FIR in Bengaluru after a public official conducting Special Summary Revision (SIR) work was assaulted near Kousar Masjid in DJ Halli on July 29.
* **Property Regularisation Extended:** The Tamil Nadu government extended the deadline for regularizing unapproved layouts and plots until June 2027 under its 2017 rules, following the expiry of the previous deadline on June 30.
* **University Funding Dispute:** Kerala's Mahatma Gandhi University issued a formal notice to its SFI-led student union, threatening to withdraw financial support for an upcoming national conference (August 3–6) following a controversy over a promotional poster depicting a cockroach.
* **Political Discourse in J&K:** Discussions regarding the potential division of Jammu & Kashmir have re-emerged in political discourse, referencing historical precedents dating back to the 1950 Owen Dixon report presented to the United Nations Security Council.

## Environmental Watch, Public Utilities & Safety

### NGT Rejects Kerala Department's Report on Coastal Pollution
The Southern Bench of the National Green Tribunal (NGT) expressed dissatisfaction with an action taken report submitted by Kerala's Local Self Government Department (LSGD) regarding coastal pollution at Fort Kochi. The tribunal noted that the July 16 submission was incomplete, failing to outline specific responsibilities, timelines, or comprehensive action plans for designated local authorities.

### Heavy Water Release and Monsoon Directives
Krishna Bhagya Jala Nigam Limited (KBJNL) officials released 1.11 lakh cusecs of water from the Basavasagar reservoir. Concurrently, following updated rainfall predictions, Telangana Cabinet Ministers directed district authorities to initiate immediate precautionary safety measures across vulnerable areas.

### Sealdah Station Footfall Crosses One Million
Daily passenger traffic at Kolkata's Sealdah station has officially surpassed one million people. Station officials attributed the milestone to the introduction of additional passenger services and Suburban Electric Multiple Unit (EMU) trains.

### Wildlife Contraband Seized in Nilgiris
Law enforcement authorities in the Nilgiris district intercepted illegal wildlife trade operations, arresting two individuals found in possession of elephant tusks.

## Consumer Protection & Social Affairs

### FSSAI Takes Action Against Major Food Products
The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) has called out two prominent consumer food products, citing non-compliance with safety regulations and operational lapses. 

### Student Activism and Regulatory Challenges
National debates surrounding education policy and examination integrity continue following widespread student protests over NEET paper leaks. The fallout from the paper leaks previously led to the resignation of a Union Minister and intensified nationwide discussions regarding youth involvement in institutional accountability.

### Tributes Paid to Dr. Muthulakshmi Reddy
Governor R.N. Ravi and Chief Minister M.K. Stalin paid public tributes to pioneering physician and social reformer Dr. Muthulakshmi Reddy on her birth anniversary, honoring her contributions to public health and social reform.
