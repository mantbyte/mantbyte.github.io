### National Politics, Policy & Judicial Developments

* **Opposition Mounts Pressure on Centre Over Governance and Strategic Policy:** The Communist Party of India (CPI), led by General Secretary D. Raja, intensified its criticism of the Union Government ahead of its September 1 rally, citing concerns over economic performance, unemployment, and proposed structural changes such as simultaneous elections. Meanwhile, the Congress party raised questions regarding official narratives around China's role in 'Operation Sindoor'. Senior Congress leader P. Chidambaram also scrutinized institutional oversight following examination controversies, stating that vulnerabilities in National Testing Agency (NTA) operations were systemic due to limited permanent staffing.

* **BJP Leadership Engages Protesters on Reservation Policies:** Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) National President J.P. Nadda held talks with representatives of an anti-reservation group following demonstrations at Jantar Mantar. The discussions were described as cordial as the leadership engaged with the demonstrators' concerns.

* **Mathura Krishna Janmabhoomi Dispute Mediation Inconclusive:** Mediation proceedings regarding the Sri Krishna Janmabhoomi-Shahi Idgah dispute in Mathura have concluded without a resolution. The long-standing dispute involves claims by Hindu petitioners regarding the 17th-century construction of the Shahi Idgah mosque at the disputed site.

* **Political Dispute Erupts Over Kerala Co-operative Bank Board Dismissal:** In Pathanamthitta, the dismissal of a cooperative bank's board of directors over allegations of obstructing an official inquiry has triggered a political confrontation, with ousted members alleging administrative bias and announcing plans for legal challenges.

### Education Sector Trends, Institutional Developments & Student Welfare

* **AYUSH Higher Education Expands Rapidly in the Private Sector:** Official data indicates a significant increase in Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homoeopathy (AYUSH) education, with institutional numbers rising by nearly 23% from 789 in 2021 to 971 in 2024. The expansion has been predominantly driven by non-governmental and private colleges.

* **Campus Protests at IIT Delhi; NLSIU Adjusts Convocation Formats:** 
  * The Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Delhi highlighted its mental health framework and student support initiatives amid ongoing campus demonstrations following the death of student Rishikesh Kumar.
  * The National Law School of India University (NLSIU), Bengaluru, announced the cancellation of its traditional convocation ceremony for the graduating class of 2026, offering students alternative administrative options to receive their degrees.
  * In primary and secondary education, the application window for the Bihar Simultala Residential School entrance examination enters its final day ahead of the two-shift selection test.

* **State Welfare Measures for Marginalized and Minority Students:** 
  * The Tamil Nadu government announced a revision to the monthly food allowance for students residing in SC/ST hostels, alongside plans to construct 150 new Social Justice hostels.
  * Tamil Nadu also announced a new government college in Chennai dedicated to minority women, the extension of educational aid for Muslim female students beyond Class 8, and the establishment of a dedicated Most Backward Classes (MBC) Welfare Board with an outlay of ₹3 crore.

### State Governance, Law Enforcement & Public Infrastructure

* **CB-CID Accuses Six Police Officers in Sivaganga Custodial Case:** The Crime Branch-CID (CB-CID) in Tamil Nadu has officially named six police personnel as accused in connection with the custodial death of Sivaganga youth Akash Delison, who was originally detained following a local dispute in Manamadurai.

* **Telangana Cyber Police Intervene in Major Financial Fraud:** The Telangana Cyber Security Bureau (TGCSB) successfully recovered ₹85 lakh belonging to a senior citizen targeted in an elaborate "digital arrest" cyber scam. Concurrently, election administration data from the state revealed that half of all recent Special Summary Revision (SIR) voter list deletions were heavily concentrated across 20 assembly constituencies in and around Hyderabad.

* **Legislative Debates and Procurement Scrutiny in Tamil Nadu:** 
  * In the Tamil Nadu Legislative Assembly, a procedural debate emerged over recommendations to advance ministerial announcements of new schemes prior to debates on demands for grants.
  * Concurrently, opposition leaders, including AMMK chief T.T.V. Dhinakaran, raised allegations of corruption at Direct Procurement Centres (DPCs) in Cuddalore district, demanding stricter enforcement against local graft.

* **Urban Aviation Infrastructure and Cross-Border Disaster Response:**
  * Historical planning records highlighted why a long-proposed parallel runway project at Chennai Airport by the Airports Authority of India (AAI) was deferred due to alternating state-level greenfield airport strategies.
  * Following severe flash floods in Nepal, Karnataka authorities confirmed that eight travelers from Bengaluru stranded in the region are undergoing coordinated rescue and repatriation efforts led by state relief officials.
