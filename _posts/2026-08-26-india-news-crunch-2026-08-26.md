# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National News and Events

## Accidents, Crime, and Law Enforcement
A tragic bus crash in Telangana’s Suryapet left six people dead and 12 injured. The incident occurred around 5 a.m. when a speeding bus carrying 42 passengers from Hyderabad to Eluru via Vijayawada collided with a lorry. In other grim news, a Gujarat boy, Kevalji Thakor, was murdered by a villager amid a land dispute and witchcraft claims after going missing on Sunday. Meanwhile, in Uttarakhand, a man shot and killed his two sisters just days before Raksha Bandhan, leaving both dead on the spot from gunshot wounds. In Gujarat, Anti-Corruption Bureau (ACB) officials caught a forest guard and his private associate red-handed accepting a Rs 100 bribe at the Surajbari checkpost.

## Education and Public Health
The National Medical Commission (NMC) has ordered medical colleges to submit monthly reports on student suicides. Concurrently, the Uttar Pradesh Cabinet has doubled the monthly stipend for MBBS interns from Rs 12,000 to Rs 25,000, alongside approving 25 lakh tablets for youth under the Swami Vivekananda scheme. For prospective medical postgraduates, the NEET PG 2026 admit cards have been released, with the exam scheduled for August 30 in a single shift from 9 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. In Odisha, the State Human Rights Commission has ordered a probe after a parentless student faced bureaucratic hurdles and academic deprivation because orphanages recorded imaginary parents' names on his certificates. Meanwhile, tribal students in Pune continued their protest into its 13th day as talks with the Tribal Minister failed to yield a breakthrough regarding a disputed Government Resolution issued on August.

## Politics and International Affairs
Political developments saw Asaduddin Owaisi launch a sharp critique against the RSS and BJP over their connections to the Manusmriti, questioning their commitment to the Indian Constitution. In West Bengal, political friction persisted as Suvendu Adhikari vowed to keep Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee off the streets, prompting a swift response from the TMC chairperson asserting she is not taking political *sanyas*. In Punjab, BSP chief Mayawati announced that her party will contest upcoming elections entirely alone, ruling out any alliances. Internationally, the United States imposed sanctions on four India-based firms and three Indian individuals as part of a broader penalization of 60 entities, individuals, and vessels aimed at punishing Iran. Additionally, a UN committee has formally called on India to urgently address and ensure accountability for discrimination, hate speech, and hate crimes. 

## Infrastructure, Environment, and Culture
Severe weather and urban transit made headlines in Gurugram, where heavy rains and resultant waterlogging caused massive traffic jams, leaving residents stranded for up to four hours to cover an 8-kilometer distance. Looking at economic evolution, a retrospective on Chennai highlights its transformation from a colonial trading hub to a diversified global business destination housing leading international firms. In military history reports, it was revealed that weather conditions spared Pakistan's Skardu base during 'Op Sindoor,' though the Bholari base sustained a direct hit from the Indian Air Force. Finally, cultural retrospectives by *The Hindu* explored the cinematic legacy of the historic Prarthana Theatre and the changing local seafood dynamics along regional rivers.
