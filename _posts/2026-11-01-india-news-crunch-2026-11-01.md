# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National News & Current Affairs

### Political Face-Off in Delhi: INDIA Bloc Rallies Against Election Commission as BJP Mounts Counter-Offensive

The national capital witnessed sharp political polarization as the opposition INDIA bloc organized a massive rally at Ramlila Maidan, demanding the immediate removal of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar. Top opposition leaders, including Sonia Gandhi, Rahul Gandhi, and West Bengal Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee, led the march in what was framed as a "public impeachment" of the CEC. Speakers, including Congress MP Shashi Tharoor and Trinamool Congress MP Sagarika Ghose, criticized the poll body over alleged electoral roll discrepancies, citing voter disenfranchisement and the deletion of names as a threat to institutional credibility. Amid the rally, Rahul Gandhi also targeted the ruling administration over the forced closure of commercial establishments in Connaught Place.

In response, the Bharatiya Janata Party held a simultaneous counter-rally, dismissing the opposition's mobilization as a manufactured crisis. Senior BJP leader and Union Minister J.P. Nadda termed the protest a "foolish drama" designed to instigate disorder, predicting it would mark the final blow to the Congress party's political viability. Concurrently, BJP leader Nitin Nabin accused Rahul Gandhi of operating at the behest of foreign powers, asserting that the opposition only questions democratic processes following electoral setbacks.

### Regional Flashpoints: Polling Violence in Andhra Pradesh, Policy Moves in Karnataka and Maharashtra

Regional developments saw acute friction in Andhra Pradesh, where violent clashes erupted between Telugu Desam Party (TDP) and YSR Congress Party (YSRCP) cadres in Pulivendula, Kadapa district, ahead of civic polling. Fourteen vehicles were set ablaze in the early hours before voting commenced, with voter turnout recorded at 37.13 percent by 1:00 p.m. Elsewhere in the state, Minister Nara Lokesh confirmed that a Class 10 student injured in a chainsaw attack in Anantapur has been stabilized at Manipal Hospital following blood transfusions. 

In Karnataka, the state administration announced it is evaluating official recognition of the Kannada flag. The government plans to enable private entities and mandate government-supported institutions to fly the state flag. On municipal revenue, Mysuru City Corporation reported a collection of ₹210 crore in property taxes against an annual target of ₹286.72 crore. 

In Maharashtra, administrative efforts shifted toward agricultural mitigation, with the government allocating 5 percent of district planning funds to counteract fodder shortages. The initiative involves distributing seeds through Mahabeej and encouraging the cultivation of low-water-requirement crops such as maize, jowar, and bajra.

### Law Enforcement and Security: Drug Busts and Strategic Defense Directives

Law enforcement agencies took critical steps to curb illicit trafficking across state corridors. The West Bengal Special Task Force (STF) arrested four individuals in Malda, effectively dismantling an interstate drug network. The operation comes amid heightened public concern regarding narcotics across northern corridors, particularly in Punjab, where the murder of anti-drug campaigner Parvinder Singh Jhota has intensified scrutiny on enforcement efficacy.

On the national defense front, Air Chief Marshal Dixit delineated a renewed operational doctrine for the Indian Air Force under the acronym **VAYU**—standing for Vigilance and operational readiness, *Aatmanirbharta* (self-reliance), and *Yukti* (tactical innovation). Addressing personnel, the IAF Chief emphasized that every squadron, air warrior, and air defense unit must maintain high readiness to deploy at a moment’s notice.

### Judicial Rulings, Accidents, and Rescue Operations

* **Supreme Court Clears IT Reassessments:** A Supreme Court bench comprising Justices J.B. Pardiwala and K. Vinod Chandran dismissed four appeals filed by Chetan and Nitin Sabharwal, the businessman sons of late former Chief Justice of India Y.K. Sabharwal. The order clears the pathway for the Income Tax Department to proceed with reassessment proceedings dating back to the 2008–09 assessment period.
* **Uttarakhand Helicopter Crash:** Recovery teams successfully retrieved the mortal remains of all six victims involved in the recent Uttarakhand helicopter crash. The remains have been airlifted to AIIMS Rishikesh for post-mortem examinations and identification formalities.
* **Search Operations in Nepal:** Authorities launched a search-and-rescue operation in Nepal after six Indian tourists were reported stranded. Officials noted the group had pushed forward into hazardous terrain despite prior warnings from security personnel and their local guide.
* **Education and Technology Updates:** IIT Guwahati announced the launch of new Diploma and BTech programs in semiconductor technology aimed at expanding specialized technical training to affiliated engineering colleges. Meanwhile, CISCE is slated to release the upcoming Class 10 (ICSE) and Class 12 (ISC) examination schedules.
