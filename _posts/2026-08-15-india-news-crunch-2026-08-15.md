# Mantbyte Daily Digest: Independence Day Celebrations, National News, and Current Events

## Nation Celebrates 80th Independence Day with Historic Addresses and Tributes

India marked its 80th Independence Day with grand celebrations led by Prime Minister Narendra Modi at the historic Red Fort. Addressing the nation, PM Modi urged citizens to embrace self-reliance and focus on the collective goal of realizing a 'Viksit Bharat' (Developed India) by 2047, describing it as the nation's biggest dream. 

In a historic first for the celebrations, the national song 'Vande Mataram' was rendered during the event at the Red Fort, commemorating 150 years of the song. Political leaders across the spectrum—including Amit Shah, Mallikarjun Kharge, and Rajnath Singh—joined the Prime Minister in extending greetings, paying tributes to freedom fighters, and emphasizing that the visions of historical figures continue to inspire the country's future progress. PM Modi also dedicated a portion of his address to express solidarity with those affected by natural disasters, stating that "India stands with flood victims."

As part of the historical reflection on August 15, 1947, archives also recalled regional milestones, such as how Hyderabad uniquely witnessed two contrasting observances on that day: Congress and Communist workers defying restrictions to hoist the tricolour, alongside supporters of the Nizam celebrating ‘Independent Hyderabad Day’.

## Crime, Accidents, and Public Safety

A series of tragic incidents and security demands surfaced across various states:
* **Bihar:** Four people died after inhaling toxic gas inside a well in Bihar. Police successfully recovered all the bodies from the site.
* **Uttar Pradesh:** A speeding government SUV bearing a "Magistrate" label allegedly rammed into a motorcycle, leaving two people dead and one injured.
* **Tamil Nadu:** Activist A. Kathir, executive director of Evidence, called for a Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) probe into the Coimbatore Scheduled Caste youth murder case. Citing the brutality of the daylight attack near college grounds by drugged assailants using rods and logs, Kathir stated that the available evidence demands a CBI intervention.

## Governance, Elections, and Administrative Updates

* **Telangana:** The Special Intensive Revision (SIR) enumeration phase has faced administrative hurdles, leaving approximately 94 lakh electors dependent on 119 Electoral Registration Officers (EROs). With quasi-judicial powers concentrated among these 119 officials, reports note that adjudicating the vast volume of cases leaves less than a minute per elector, even under round-the-clock working conditions.

## Education and Examinations

Academic and recruitment bodies have announced key schedules for students and aspirants:
* **UPSC:** The Union Public Service Commission released the UPSC Civil Services Mains Admit Card 2026 online on August 14. Candidates can now access and download their hall tickets.
* **UGC NET:** The provisional answer key for the UGC NET June 2026 examination is scheduled for release.
