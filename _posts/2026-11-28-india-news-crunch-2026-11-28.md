# Daily National Digest: Delimitation Debate Dominates Political Landscape, Yogi Adityanath Outlines UP Vision

### Political Showdown Over the Proposed Delimitation Bill
Ahead of the upcoming special parliamentary session, the proposed Delimitation Bill has triggered intense national debate and political realignment. Congress President Mallikarjun Kharge, speaking from 10 Rajaji Marg in New Delhi, strongly denounced the legislation, labeling it a "threat" to democracy and warning that it could be used as a weapon by the ruling party to remain in power permanently. The INDIA bloc has collectively resolved to oppose the Bill, arguing that it undermines federalism, distorts political representation, and adversely impacts Scheduled Castes (SCs) and Scheduled Tribes (STs). 

The opposition front has faced internal friction, however. While Shiv Sena (UBT) leader Sanjay Raut criticized the NCP(SP) leadership over strategic positioning regarding the Bill, the Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (DMK) announced a contrasting stance. Senior DMK leader A. Raja stated that the party would support the Centre's proposal, asserting that it entails no political loss for Tamil Nadu. Conversely, BJP leader Suvendu Adhikari urged all Members of Parliament to support the measure, rising above party lines.

### Yogi Adityanath Highlights Uttar Pradesh Governance at NDTV Creators Manch
Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath addressed a wide range of political and social issues at the NDTV Creators Manch event held at Namo Ghat in Varanasi. Expressing absolute confidence in the Bharatiya Janata Party's electoral prospects, Adityanath stated that the party would secure a "hattrick" in the state, emphasizing that "driving a bulldozer needs willpower." 

Addressing critics and youth engagement, the Chief Minister remarked on the state's massive population of 26 crore, noting an absence of disruptive Gen Z protests under his administration. Adityanath also targeted Samajwadi Party chief Akhilesh Yadav over his planned Ayodhya visit. Framing Hindutva as his own "homeground," Adityanath challenged the opposition leader to "play on our pitch."

### Judicial Directives, Legal Disputes, and Law Enforcement Updates
In Tamil Nadu, heightened security and strict regulatory measures were implemented at the Thirupparankundram temple ahead of the scheduled 'Deepathoon' lamp lighting at 5:00 p.m. Following a Supreme Court declaration that executive authorities had willfully disobeyed orders regarding the disputed stone pillar, former High Court judges reviewed crowd management logistics, capping hill access at 50 devotees to maintain public order. 

In another significant legal development, the Supreme Court set aside Orissa High Court orders and cleared the path for the Hockey India elections scheduled for December 5, directing that grievances from Hockey Odisha be addressed by a committee led by former apex court judge Justice A.K. Sikri. Meanwhile, the legal validity of a 1968 agreement between the Shri Krishna Janmasthan Seva Sangh and the Shahi Eidgah Trust—which historically addressed the Mathura temple-mosque dispute—came under formal fire by Hindu litigants 58 years later.

In law enforcement updates, Gurugram Police successfully deported 27 Bangladeshi nationals detained for illegal residency, escorting them via train. In Andhra Pradesh, DGP Harish Kumar Gupta disbursed ₹74 lakh in financial aid to surrendered Maoists, announcing that rehabilitation assistance would soon be extended to 32 additional individuals.

### Cultural Reflections and Regional Politics
The nation marked the birth centenary of legendary cultural figure Bhupen Hazarika, with retrospectives celebrating how his powerful voice and philosophy continue to provoke deep critical thought a hundred years later. 

In regional politics, AIADMK leader Edappadi K. Palaniswami hailed the arrest of a TVK Kallakurichi MLA as a direct victory resulting from his party's sustained public agitations. Meanwhile, at Sabarimala, the temple priest issued an earnest appeal to pilgrims, urging them to refrain from discarding discarded clothing in the Pampa River.
