# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National News and Events

## Political Developments and Governance

A fresh political confrontation has erupted between the Congress and the Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) following Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s remarks regarding “intellectual Naxals” during his Deepavali visit to Bastar. In response to the comments, Congress leader Rahul Gandhi has challenged Prime Minister Modi to an open debate. Meanwhile, publication of senior Congress leader Sonia Gandhi’s memoir, *Belonging: A Journey of Love*, has drawn significant attention. Penguin Random House India (PRHI) announced on August 28 that it is not the publisher of the book in India, leading to questions over the controversy surrounding its local release. 

In regional politics, former Tamil Nadu Chief Minister and DMK leader M.K. Stalin has criticized the disbanding of the Union-State relations committee in the state. He rejected the argument that the mandate of the High-Level Committee on Union-State Relations overlapped with that of the Tamil Nadu State Law Commission. In Keralam, a fresh "Vande Mataram" controversy emerged after Health Minister K. Muraleedharan returned to his seat before the full rendition ended at a state government event, prompting the BJP to demand an apology. Defending his actions, Muraleedharan stated he would not allow anyone to hijack a state government event.

## International Relations and Legal Matters

The U.S. State Department has formally served notice on India regarding a civil lawsuit filed by Gurpatwant Singh Pannun. The State Department emphasized that it was legally obliged to take this action and is not taking a position on the case. On bilateral cooperation, India and the United States have vowed to jointly act against emerging drug threats following the 2nd India-United States of America Drug Policy Executive Working Group Meeting, hosted by the Narcotics Control Bureau on November 2-3.

## Infrastructure, Economy, and Agriculture

In Andhra Pradesh, the state government is expected to make significant allocations for the Neradi barrage on the Vamsadhara river. According to BRR Vamsadhara Project Chairman Aravala Ravindrababu, additional funds may follow the administrative sanction of ₹461 crores granted a couple of months ago. Concurrently, Andhra teachers’ associations have flagged anomalies in new service rules, noting they had repeatedly requested comprehensive and uniform service rules for Government and Panchayat Raj teachers following the Presidential Order, 202...

In Maharashtra, a delegation of farmers has demanded a uniform sugarcane rate of ₹4,200 across the state. Furthermore, the delegation requested a ₹2 lakh incentive grant for farmers who regularly repay their loans and ₹1 lakh per hectare in financial assistance for drought-affected cultivators. In urban and transit infrastructure, the Telangana Road Transport Corporation (RTC) announced plans to improve passenger information services and security at 86 bus stations, including the installation of CCTV cameras. Additionally, Hyderabad is gearing up for Chhath Puja from November 13–16, 2026, with the Bihar Association urging civic bodies to prioritize sanitation and hygiene, and the Telangana government directing disaster management authorities to facilitate the event. 

## Education, Technology, and Society

In the education sector, Germany and Ireland have emerged as preferred destinations for higher education among Indian students. According to Karan Lalit, Executive Director of South Asia for TOEFL and GRE, approximately 20% of the more than 60,000 students heading to Germany originate from Karnataka. In testing updates, the correction window for the CSIR NET December 2026 exam has opened, with the test scheduled to be conducted on December 19, 22, and 27, 2026.

In the technology and scientific domains, a U.S. healthcare tech firm has opened an AI engineering center in Hyderabad with plans to hire 1,000 employees. Meanwhile, India’s deep-sea exploration initiative, featuring the MATSYA-6000 submersible designed to reach 6,000 meters below the ocean surface, remains on track for its expected launch in 2028. In consumer health technology, billionaire Deepinder Goyal has begun selling "Temple," a temple-attached wearable intended to monitor brain health and aging by measuring cerebral blood flow outside a laboratory, though critics and doctors have questioned its usefulness.

Administrative preparations are also underway in Telangana, where dates for Census-2027 Phase-II have been announced, featuring self-enumeration from January 10–24. In law enforcement news, a police sub-inspector in Vellore shot at a history-sheeter who attacked him during an escape bid; both the sub-inspector (Raja) and the suspect (Poovarasan) were subsequently admitted to the Government Taluk Hospital in Gudiyatham town.
