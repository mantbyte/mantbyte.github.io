# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National News and Events

## Public Health, Infrastructure, and Regional Developments

A statewide vaccination drive is currently underway to protect children aged 10 and 16 against tetanus and diphtheria using the TD vaccine, supplementing the Pentavalent immunisation program administered during early childhood. In infrastructure and connectivity updates, Alliance Air announced the launch of flight services on the Imphal-Silchar route starting November 24, operating under the Viability Gap Funding facility twice a week on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Meanwhile, a German delegation, including the Ambassador, visited Auroville to observe the founders' spiritual vision, with plans for a return visit in December.

## Environmental Shifts and Public Safety

Deteriorating air quality has driven a massive surge in air purifier sales across India, with the Delhi-NCR region experiencing significant demand spikes following Diwali, and sales trends expanding beyond major metropolitan areas. Meteorological updates indicate that a developing depression over the Bay of Bengal is projected to intensify into a severe cyclonic storm by November 17, bringing rainfall to north coastal districts of Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, and Karaikal, though no alerts have been issued for Andhra Pradesh thus far. In Assam, authorities arrested the owner of the vehicle involved in the November 13 Kaziranga crash near Hathikuli Tea Estate, with overspeeding records currently under police scrutiny. Additionally, an IndiGo Guwahati-bound flight carrying 192 persons safely returned to Pune after experiencing a technical issue shortly after takeoff.

## Political Discourse, Governance, and Security

Political tensions have escalated around the proposed Mekedatu dam project following assertions from Karnataka Minister Priyank Kharge that the law supports the project and that both states must adhere to Supreme Court directions. In response, Tamil Nadu political leaders including T.T.V. Dhinakaran, G.K. Vasan, and Edappadi K. Palaniswami have urged actor-politician Vijay to raise the issue with the Congress leadership and protect farmers' livelihoods, criticizing Karnataka Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar's stance. In Chhattisgarh, BJP chief Nitin Nabin stated that the government would take strict action against 'Dimagi Naxals', while the Jharkhand Mukti Morcha (JMM) voiced support for Rahul Gandhi's critique of the BJP-led government, alleging an "undeclared Emergency" under party rule. 

In administrative and financial matters, the Maharashtra government has urged drought-hit farmers to complete e-KYC and obtain Farmer IDs to seamlessly access direct benefit transfers. In cybercrime enforcement, the IFSO unit arrested three individuals in Bhopal for running a 'digital arrest' scam that defrauded a Delhi woman of ₹71 lakh by impersonating an IPS officer in a fake money laundering case. पंजाब National Bank (PNB) also addressed a fund misappropriation case, confirming that an FIR has been registered in Greater Noida, genuine claims will be honored, and full cooperation is being extended to authorities. 

Defense Minister Rajnath Singh called for stricter checks and embedding quality into every stage of defence manufacturing. On the diplomatic front, the United States has assured India of a comparative tariff advantage over competitors, clearing a major hurdle for ongoing trade deal negotiations. Finally, the Bureau of Police Research has invited research proposals for its 2026-27 programme, and the political establishment mourned the passing of veteran Telugu actor Giribabu at the age of 80, commemorating a career spanning over five decades and more than 600 films.
