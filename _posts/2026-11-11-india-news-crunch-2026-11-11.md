# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National News and Events

## Crime, Security, and Investigations

* **Tiruchi Murder Case:** Six individuals have been arrested for the murder of two minor boys at Samayapuram near Tiruchi. During the operation, one of the accused was shot in the left knee by a police officer using a service weapon after allegedly attempting to assault personnel during a weapon recovery attempt. In a separate incident, police are investigating a case where a woman and a minor boy allegedly killed a 15-year-old.
* **Olympic Medals Recovered:** Bengaluru police successfully recovered the 1980 Olympic gold medal belonging to M.M. Somaya, alongside seven other international medals. Authorities arrested two suspects and recovered a traditional Kodava community dagger, a camera, an Olympic kit bag, and the two-wheeler allegedly used in the crime.
* **Illegal Oocyte Racket in Telangana:** Following an ongoing investigation into an illegal oocyte collection racket, authorities inspected 27 Assisted Reproductive Technology (ART) centres across Telangana and issued notices to seven hospitals. The probe examines the involvement of doctors, IVF centers, intermediaries, and associated women.
* **J&K Market Fire Probe:** The Jammu and Kashmir Lieutenant Governor has constituted a committee to investigate the Nehru market fire. The panel has been instructed to probe any lapses or negligence, fix responsibility, and submit its report within 15 days.
* **Drunk Driving Crackdown in Kerala:** Kerala Police have booked over 25,500 drunk driving cases since October 13, initiated following an accident in Nedumbassery that claimed three lives. Authorities will seek licence suspension for first-time offenders and cancellation for repeat offenders.

## Governance, Policy, and Environment

* **Lithium-Ion Battery Waste Export Ban:** The Indian government has barred the export of lithium-ion battery waste and scrap containing black mass. The particulate powdered mixture is critical for the nation's electronic, electric vehicle, and renewable energy storage sectors.
* **Agricultural Waste and Biogas:** An initiative in Barabanki converting crop waste into energy via biogas plants has saved farmers up to ₹3,000 per acre on stubble collection while generating employment across cutting, baling, transportation, and processing sectors.
* **Air Pollution Public Health Concerns:** A former Chief Scientist at the World Health Organisation (WHO) stated that air pollution constitutes a public health emergency comparable to COVID-19. Meanwhile, Uttar Pradesh Chief Minister Yogi Adityanath defended the bursting of crackers during Diwali celebrations in Gorakhpur, asserting that pollution subsides.
* **Administrative and Electoral Updates:** The Election Commission of India officials stated there were no discrepancies between votes polled and counted during the Mumbai North Lok Sabha polls. In administrative appointments, retired judge Justice M.V. Muralidharan was appointed chairperson of the Tamil Nadu SC/ST Commission, filling a vacancy left by the March demise of retired judge S. Tamilvanan. Additionally, Karnataka education officials are evaluating the pros and cons of implementing a cluster kitchen system for midday meals to relieve teachers of administrative burdens.

## Infrastructure, Transport, and Regional Politics

* **Vande Bharat Fare Discrepancies:** Passengers traveling on the Bengaluru-Mangaluru-Goa Vande Bharat Express are paying nearly ₹200 extra compared to equivalent distances on other services. The inflated pricing stems from fare calculations on the Konkan Railway network between Thokur and Madgaon, alongside the Sakleshpur-Subrahmanya Road Ghat section.
* **Political Developments:** Former YSRCP leader Vijayasai Reddy has launched the Parivartana Rajaneethi Party, vowing to contest all 175 Andhra Pradesh Assembly seats and end "two-family" politics. In internal political discourse, Congress leader Sonia Gandhi addressed the DMK alliance dilemma, questioning potential electoral implications, while Kerala Chief Minister Pinarayi Vijayan defended collective cabinet decision-making amid regional critiques.

## Society and Human Interest

* **NCW Intervention:** National Commission for Women (NCW) Chief Vijaya Rahatkar is scheduled to meet with senior police officers, district administration officials, and the media in Ranchi regarding the Nisha Bhagat case.
* **Fitness Meets Faith in Kerala:** St. Joseph’s Church in Kuriachira, Thrissur, has established an open gym for parishioners, integrating physical fitness with community life by allowing members to drop in at their convenience for workouts like pull-ups.
