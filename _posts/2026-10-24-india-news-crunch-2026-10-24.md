# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National Round-Up

### Extreme Weather Alerts and Critical Developments in Andhra Pradesh

Authorities in Andhra Pradesh are on high alert as a low-pressure system over the Bay of Bengal threatens to intensify into a cyclonic storm. The Andhra Pradesh State Disaster Management Authority (APSDMA) has warned of imminent rainfall across several vulnerable regions, including Konaseema, West Godavari, Krishna, Guntur, Bapatla, Prakasam, and the Rayalaseema districts. In response, the Chief Minister convened emergency discussions directing administrative officials to bolster preparedness while ensuring the timely execution of 36 priority irrigation projects.

Simultaneously addressing broader climatic challenges, an Andhra Pradesh Cabinet Sub-Committee has initiated department-wise planning to manage El Niño-induced drought conditions, provisionally classifying 358 mandals as drought-affected based on erratic rainfall patterns and compromised crop coverage. 

In local governance, District Collector P. Raja Babu confirmed that the nomination phase for the ZPTC and MPTC parishad elections concluded peacefully and transparently across Nellore, Prakasam, and Markapuram districts. Tragedy, however, struck the Alluri Sitharama Raju (ASR) district, where at least four people lost their lives following a catastrophic fire triggered while diesel was being transferred between a tipper and a truck.

### Law Enforcement, Crime Inquiries, and Political Rallies

In an international law enforcement operation, two high-profile Indian gangsters wanted for murder, Himanshu Bhau and Neeraj Faridpuria, were apprehended in the United States. Domestically, the Mumbai Crime Branch recovered two firearms from a hideout in Surat, Gujarat, after tracing the logistics trail connected to a recent shooting incident.

In the national capital, Delhi Police registered a case following the discovery of unauthorized posters questioning the government inside a Delhi Metro train; the material directed commuters via QR code to an external online group. Tensions also surfaced at Jamia Millia Islamia, where Congress MP Imran Masood was detained during demonstrations backing student demands for union elections and protesting disciplinary measures.

In Kerala, the Vigilance and Anti-Corruption Bureau has initiated an inquiry against a former Samagra Shiksha Kerala (SSK) official. The investigation stems from allegations brought by Youth Congress leader V.P. Dulkhifil, linking teachers' contract renewals to a narcotics case involving MDMA.

### Political Friction and Urban Crackdowns in Delhi and Hyderabad

Traders in New Delhi have raised alarms over potential commercial disruptions ahead of major festive celebrations. The Chamber of Trade and Industry (CTI), led by Chairman Brijesh Goyal, urged Congress leader Rahul Gandhi to postpone the scheduled November 1 INDIA bloc rally at Ramlila Maidan, warning that demonstrations during peak Dhanteras and Deepavali shopping could result in business losses exceeding ₹10,000 crore for local retail and wholesale hubs. 

On foreign affairs and national defense, AIMIM Chief Asaduddin Owaisi publicly demanded the convening of a special session of Parliament to discuss Chinese border incursions into Arunachal Pradesh.

In Telangana, enforcement agencies carried out multiple civic interventions across Hyderabad:
* **Water Resource Protection:** The Hyderabad Disaster Response and Asset Protection Agency (HYDRAA) dismantled dozens of illicit pipelines siphoning water from Sunnam Cheruvu lake into adjacent commercial and residential structures in Guttala Begumpet.
* **Food Safety Enforcement:** Ahead of Deepavali festivities, food safety regulators suspended the operating licenses of three sweet shops and confiscated 1,035 kg of substandard food products.
* **Civic Infrastructure:** Citizen advocates launched an online petition urging the state government to address traffic gridlock by prioritizing public transit and rethinking road infrastructure policies.

### Public Health, Medical Research, and Community Initiatives

In medical science, researchers at GITAM—backed by funding from the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR)—have launched a clinical study aiming to develop an accessible blood test for early detection of triple-negative breast cancer. The research relies on comparative biomarker analysis between early-stage cancer patients and healthy donors. Meanwhile, at the SAT Hospital's Spinal Muscular Atrophy (SMA) clinic, clinical leads affirmed that comprehensive counselling protocols had been extended to parents concerning the use of generic therapeutics.

At the community level, Bengaluru’s Indian Institute of World Culture on B.P. Wadia Road hosted the hands-on "Repair maadi" workshop, encouraging citizens to restore household items rather than dispose of them. Addressing occupational wellness, specialists announced an upcoming educational webinar on modern posture, focusing on mitigating spine and neck issues arising from workplace ergonomics and physical inactivity.
