# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National News & Current Affairs

### Politics, Legal Affairs, and Governance

**Delhi Police Register 10 FIRs in Anti-CEC Protest Violence**  
Following sustained public pressure and scrutiny from civil rights advocates, the Delhi Police have registered at least 10 First Information Reports (FIRs) against right-wing members for allegedly assaulting demonstrators during the ongoing anti-Chief Election Commissioner (CEC) protests in the national capital. Authorities stated that CCTV footage is actively being scrutinized to establish the sequence of events and pinpoint individual culpability. Concurrently, activist Abhijeet Dipke is scheduled to be produced before a Special Executive Magistrate (SEM) court, as legal observers and activists, including Neha Bora, condemned the police detentions of protesters as unlawful.

**Veteran BJP Leader L.K. Advani Hospitalized**  
Former Deputy Prime Minister and senior Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) leader L.K. Advani, 98, remains in stable condition after being admitted to Apollo Hospitals in New Delhi. BJP National President J.P. Nadda visited the facility to review the veteran leader's medical progress and treatment regimen.

**Political Friction Intensifies in Tamil Nadu and West Bengal**  
In Tamil Nadu, Chief Minister M.K. Stalin launched a sharp political attack on the Tamizhaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK), labeling it a "Breaking Bad" party following the Directorate of Revenue Intelligence's (DRI) arrest of TVK functionary Boobalan in connection with the seizure of 135 kg of methamphetamine. 

Meanwhile, in West Bengal, traditional Durga Puja book stalls became the center of a political face-off between Left parties and right-wing groups, with several stalls experiencing targeted crackdowns amid rising ideological polarization in the state.

**Key Panel and Judicial Observations: KIIFB and Reservation Rulings**  
In Kerala, the state assembly reviewed the Sudha Pillai Committee report, which issued a critical assessment of the Kerala Infrastructure Investment Fund Board (KIIFB), noting that there is "no case" to sustain the body in its current role of fund-raising and project financing. 

Separately, the Supreme Court of India deliberated on the intersection of religious conversion and constitutional quota benefits in the *Secretary to Government v. Sameer Ahamed N.* case, which centers on a petition concerning an individual who converted to Islam and subsequently claimed community-based reservations.

### Policy, Education, and Diplomatic Affairs

**Parliamentary Panel Urges CBT Mode for NEET-UG and Central Blacklist**  
In the wake of controversies surrounding the NEET-UG examination, a parliamentary committee has advised shifting National Testing Agency (NTA)-run exams to a Computer-Based Testing (CBT) format. The committee's recommendations include the establishment of a centralized registry to permanently bar compromised or blacklisted examination vendors, the creation of robust question banks to prevent recurrent paper leaks, and the deployment of structural safeguards against administrative failures.

**India Rejects UN Human Rights Report on Youth Retaliation**  
Indian representative Anuj Swarup strongly pushed back against allegations made by a United Nations human rights expert claiming that Indian youth face systemic retaliation or stigmatization when discussing gender justice and mental health. Dismissing the findings as "hearsay," India maintained that the assertions fail to reflect ground realities and lack substantiated backing.

**Demands for Caste Census and Academic Conventions**  
An Other Backward Classes (OBC) students' organization has submitted a formal representation to the President of India urging the immediate execution of a national caste census. Concurrently, preparations were initiated by the Madras Management Association (MMA) for its annual students' convention, centered on "Future-Ready Leadership: Reinventing Management for a Changing World," while the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) released procedural directives and schedules for upcoming board examinations.

### Technology, Infrastructure, and Regional Developments

**Starlink Confirms Indian Infrastructure Readiness**  
Satellite internet provider Starlink announced that it has established its local infrastructure in line with Indian government regulatory requirements and stands prepared to roll out high-speed commercial connectivity. The announcement follows statements from Reliance Industries advocating for fair satellite-communication competition while urging that all global entrants strictly comply with domestic regulatory mandates.

**Urban Civic Actions and Regional Weather Distress**  
* **Civic Action in Mumbai:** Municipal authorities conducted a demolition drive targeting alleged unauthorized construction at Bollywood actor Ajay Devgn’s bungalow in the Juhu locality of Mumbai.
* **Preparations in Chennai:** The Greater Chennai Corporation (GCC) Joint Commissioner (Works), C. Saravanan, inspected development work across the Thiru. Vi. Ka. Nagar Zone ahead of an upcoming visit by the Prime Minister.
* **Agricultural Crisis in Telangana:** Severe arid conditions have gripped Telangana’s Khammam district, where authorities report that 17 out of 21 mandals are officially drought-hit, leaving 12 mandals grappling with critical fodder shortages.

### Public Safety and Accidents

**Fatal Minibus Crash on Delhi-Mumbai Expressway**  
A road accident on the Delhi-Mumbai Expressway near Kota, Rajasthan, claimed the lives of eight people and left 15 others injured when a minibus plunged into a 15-foot roadside drain. Preliminary investigations suggest the driver may have fallen asleep at the wheel, causing the vehicle to drift off the eight-lane highway.

**Deadly Reactor Blast in Gujarat Triggers Gas Leak**  
At least five people were killed following an industrial chemical reactor explosion at a facility in Gujarat. The blast released heavy gas and industrial dust, shrouding adjacent areas in a thick haze. Emergency response units were deployed, though the direct operational cause of the reactor failure remains under investigation.
