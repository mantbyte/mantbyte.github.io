# Indian National News Digest: Major Developments in Judiciary, Security, Diplomacy, and Governance

## Judicial Rulings and Legal Frameworks

### Supreme Court Grants Bail to Two Convicts in Bilkis Bano Case
The Supreme Court of India has suspended the sentences and granted bail to two convicts in the Bilkis Bano gang-rape and murder case. A bench considered that the appellants had completed over 17 years of incarceration, with one individual facing a severe disability. The two convicts were part of a group of 11 whose premature release orders were previously struck down by the apex court in January 2024.

### Apex Court Upholds Maharashtra Local Body Reservation Policy
The Supreme Court dismissed a plea challenging Maharashtra’s rotation policy for reserved Scheduled Caste (SC) and Scheduled Tribe (ST) seats in local governance bodies. A bench comprising Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V. Mohana upheld the September 19, 2025 judgment of the Bombay High Court's Nagpur Bench, rejecting the appeal filed by Sau. Rupali Indrabhushan Sonde.

### Kerala High Court Recommends Lowering Juvenile Age Threshold
The Kerala High Court has suggested a re-examination of the legal age defining juvenile status, proposing a reduction of the threshold from 18 to 16 years. The court indicated that the matter will be taken up for further hearing on November 24.

## Defense, Counter-Terrorism, and International Relations

### Security Forces Neutralize Two Lashkar Terrorists in J&K Encounter
In Jammu and Kashmir's Kulgam district, security forces killed two heavily armed Lashkar-e-Taiba terrorists during an anti-terror operation. The deceased terrorists were identified as those involved in the November 11 attack on a Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF) post near Mir Bazar, which had left three personnel injured.

### IAF Chief Outlines Timeline for Upgraded Tejas Mark 1A Delivery
Air Chief Marshal announced that the Indian Air Force expects to receive its first squadron of the upgraded Light Combat Aircraft (LCA) Tejas Mark 1A within the current fiscal year. The indigenously developed multirole fighter jet represents a core component of India's ongoing military modernization effort.

### PM Modi Arrives in Manila for ASEAN-India and East Asia Summits
Prime Minister Narendra Modi arrived in Manila for a two-day visit on November 16-17 to attend the 23rd ASEAN-India Summit and the 21st East Asia Summit. The visit comes at the invitation of Philippine President Ferdinand R. Marcos Jr. to strengthen bilateral and regional strategic partnerships.

### New Delhi to Host Inaugural SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum
India’s Ministry of Culture, in collaboration with the Ministry of External Affairs, will host the first Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) Civilisational Dialogue Forum at Purana Qila in New Delhi from November 20-22. The regional event will include participation from SCO member nations, including Pakistan.

## Political Developments and State Governance

### BJP Responds to Congress Criticism over Economic Growth
The Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) countered opposition leader Rahul Gandhi's remarks characterizing the nation's economy as sluggish. BJP national media convenor Ashish Usha Agarwal stated that India continues to maintain a strong growth trajectory despite global economic uncertainties and geopolitical instability. Meanwhile, Congress President Mallikarjun Kharge stated that the party, along with its INDIA bloc partners, will present a robust political alternative in upcoming elections in Uttar Pradesh.

### Rajasthan Mandates Strict Anti-Ragging Policies in Medical Colleges
The Health Minister of Rajasthan issued strict directives to all medical colleges across the state to strictly enforce a zero-tolerance policy against ragging. The administration warned that institutional leadership and college authorities will face disciplinary action if found negligent in managing compliance.

## Science, Environment, and Cultural Briefs

### Agricultural Scientist K. Prabhakaran Nair Passes Away
Renowned agricultural scientist Dr. K. Prabhakaran Nair has passed away. Over several decades of research, Dr. Nair made landmark contributions to soil science, agronomy, and sustainable agricultural practices.

### Regional Air Quality and Weather Variations
* **Environmental Data:** Air quality in Kolkata's northern fringes degraded during Chhath celebrations, with Barrackpore recording an Air Quality Index (AQI) of 230, categorizing it in the 'poor' bracket.
* **Temperature Drops:** Winter conditions intensified in Telangana, with Sangareddy recording a low of 11.7°C and Hyderabad registering 15.3°C on November 15.

### Cultural Events: Beatlemania Tour Hits Indian Cities
Sixty-five years after the Liverpool rock band revolutionized global music, a Beatlemania tribute tour is hosting live performances across major Indian cities, including Chennai and Bengaluru, throughout November.
