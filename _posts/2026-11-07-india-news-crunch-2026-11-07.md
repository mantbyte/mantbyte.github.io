# Mantbyte Daily Digest: Indian National News and Events

## Political Developments and Policy Debates

Political friction heightened as Congress leaders targeted the central administration over fuel policies and governance. Karnataka Minister Priyank Kharge alleged that the Central government is misleading citizens regarding ethanol blending levels in E20 petrol, claiming lab reports indicate ethanol content exceeds safe margins and amounts to "experimenting on 1.3 billion people." Echoing these concerns, Rahul Gandhi launched a scathing critique, dubbing E20 as "420-petrol" and accusing the government of cheating and stealing from the public's vehicles. 

In regional politics, West Bengal Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee condemned an attack at the house of Abhishek Banerjee ahead of Kali Puja, labeling the incident "complete hooliganism." In Telangana, a political war of words erupted between the ruling Congress and the BRS, with Government Whip Aadi Srinivas asserting that the Congress government lifted more water from the Godavari than the previous BRS administration, while accusing BRS leader Harish Rao of finding "vicarious pleasure in drought conditions." Meanwhile, political reactions and controversies continued to unfold following announcements regarding Sonia Gandhi’s upcoming memoir—which revisits her personal life, loss, and her decision to decline India's top job—with Rahul Gandhi remarking that his late father would be smiling down proudly. Rahul Gandhi also challenged Amit Shah to convince Prime Minister Narendra Modi to engage in an open debate, a call supported by Congress President Mallikarjun Kharge.

## Crime, Legal, and Public Safety

Law enforcement and judicial matters commanded attention across several states. Police have sought a lookout circular against a Kochi doctor accused of sexual abuse. In Jharkhand, the death of tribal activist Nisha Bhagat has sparked a major political row. Meanwhile, Sibsagar MLA Akhil Gogoi has accused Assam Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma of encouraging protests against judges in the Zubeen Garg death case, claiming the remarks amount to contempt of court.

On the international front, Hari Boxer, an aide of Lawrence Bishnoi who issued threats against prominent Bollywood figures including Salman Khan, Kapil Sharma, Rohit Shetty, Tusshar Kapoor, and Ranveer Singh, has been detained in the United States. In public safety news, seven people were injured in Tirupati after a bus overturned when the driver reportedly dozed off and applied sudden brakes, causing the vehicle to slip toward an adjacent canal obscured by stagnant rainwater.

## Infrastructure, Economy, and Education

In a landmark legal resolution, the Supreme Court settled a 32-year legal battle, granting the State of Telangana 550 acres of land valued at over ₹55,000 crores tied to historic jagir deeds. However, external economic pressures are also being felt; soaring freight costs driven by the West Asia conflict are squeezing Kerala’s export sector by making local products less competitive in overseas markets. 

In the medical and education sectors, Osmania General Hospital in Hyderabad has introduced a strict 48-hour weekly duty limit for junior doctors and interns to regulate working hours. Additionally, the results for the INI Combined Entrance Test (CET) 2026, conducted on November 1, 2026, were released, recording 34,236 qualified candidates.

## Society, Culture, and Environment

Unique cultural and environmental stories marked regional updates. In Thrissur, Kerala, St. Joseph’s Church in Kuriachira has bridged faith and fitness by setting up an open gym for parishioners, allowing flexible access without fixed timings. Environmentalists and birdwatchers noted a significant sighting in Karnataka, where greater flamingos were spotted for the first time at Kere Bhosga.
