# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National News and Events

## Judiciary and Legal Developments

The Supreme Court is handling several high-profile legal matters across the country. A Bench comprising Chief Justice Surya Kant and Justice Joymalya Bagchi heard a plea filed by Kerala Chief Minister Pinarayi Vijayan, who challenged a High Court order directing the registration of an FIR in the CMRL bribery case. Meanwhile, the apex court refused to grant an urgent hearing concerning the Central Park closure, and adjourned the UAPA bail plea hearing for Athar Khan in connection with the 2020 Delhi riots for four weeks after the filing of a reply by Additional Solicitor General S.V. Raju. 

In other legal news, a court in Telangana sentenced ten individuals, including the victim's wife and in-laws, to life imprisonment for a man's murder, relying heavily on CCTV footage recovered from a camera at one of the accused's residences. Additionally, actor Imran Khan is set to move the High Court against a "leave India" directive issued after his participation in a protest, arguing in his draft petition that Overseas Citizen of India (OCI) rules do not prohibit such participation.

## Press Freedom, Internal Security, and Governance

Security remained tight for the Committee to Protect Journalists’ (CJP) 'Tiranga' march following the detention of journalists in Delhi, Ahmedabad, Pune, and Mumbai. In response to recent detentions and injuries during demonstrations, the Press Council of India (PCI) issued comprehensive safety guidelines for journalists covering protests, focusing on identification, neutrality, personal safety, and ethical reporting.

On the political front, Union Minister Rajnath Singh accused the Congress party and its allies of seeking a "Bangladesh-like regime change" during a press conference in Lucknow, emphasizing that democracy does not permit actions that undermine constitutional institutions. In administrative developments, Ladakh Lieutenant Governor Vinai Kumar Saxena announced Sankoo as the region's eighth district, just seven months after five new districts were established. In state leadership updates, former MP and Kargil war widow Sudha Yadav was sworn in as the Governor of Karnataka, becoming the second woman to hold the post.

Meanwhile, the Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA) is set to seek responses from the Punjab government regarding recent narcotics-related deaths. Union Home Minister Amit Shah highlighted growing concerns over the expanding drug trade and organized crime in the state. 

## Crime, Defense, and National Security

In national security updates, law enforcement deported and subsequently arrested K. Srinivas in connection with the 2025 Visakhapatnam naval dockyard spy case. Srinivas allegedly leaked sensitive details regarding ships and submarines to an operative posing as a Dubai shipping firm employee. 

Externally, India addressed the United Nations Security Council (UNSC), describing the recent escalation of hostilities in West Asia as "deeply worrying" and reiterating the urgent need for de-escalation and diplomatic dialogue.

## Economy, Regional Updates, and Culture

In economic adjustments, the government hiked the export levy on petrol while lowering it for diesel, with no alterations made to retail fuel duties intended for domestic consumption. 

At the regional level, Visakhapatnam’s restaurant and food safety standards came under scrutiny following concerns over pre-cooked, frozen food being served amid rapid culinary expansion. Concurrently, law enforcement agencies in Andhra Pradesh are ramping up security measures for upcoming local body polls, with Vizianagaram and Nellore SPs deploying thousands of personnel and initiating bind-over proceedings against individuals to curb social media misinformation and maintain order. 

In entertainment and culture, exhibitors in Kerala are experiencing a surge in ticket sales as Rajinikanth’s Tamil film *Jailer 2* achieved record first-day collections, prompting theaters to schedule additional screenings. Preparations are also underway nationwide for Durga Puja 2026, with artisans across cities beginning preparations for the upcoming festivities.
