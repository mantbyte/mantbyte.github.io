# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National News & Current Affairs

### Diplomacy, Trade, and National Affairs

* **India-New Zealand Free Trade Agreement Enters into Force:** The bilateral Free Trade Agreement between India and New Zealand has officially come into operation. The pact introduces zero-duty market access for Indian exports and establishes two new Temporary Employment Entry (TEE) visa categories for Indian nationals seeking work opportunities in New Zealand.

* **Rajnath Singh Issues Strong Counter-Terror Warning at SCO:** Addressing the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) meeting with Pakistani delegates present, Defence Minister Rajnath Singh asserted that there can be "no double standards on terrorism," urging member nations to adopt a unified stance against cross-border terror networks.

* **RSS Chief Delivers Vijayadashami Address:** In his annual Vijayadashami speech, Rashtriya Swayamsevak Sangh (RSS) chief Mohan Bhagwat warned against forces attempting to deepen internal divisions within India. Citing international unrest in France, Nepal, and Bangladesh, he urged national unity rooted in the "Hindu spirit" and highlighted values exemplified by individuals such as flydubai pilot Captain Smit Machchhar. Concurrently, national leaders, including Prime Minister Narendra Modi, Union Home Minister Amit Shah, and Jammu and Kashmir Chief Minister Omar Abdullah, extended festive greetings emphasizing mutual respect, unity, and peaceful coexistence.

### Domestic Politics, Governance, and Policy

* **Tamil Nadu Expands Guaranteed Employment with ‘Vetri 150’:** The Tamil Nadu government has sanctioned ₹100 crore for fiscal year 2026–27 to launch the ‘Vetri 150’ scheme. The policy guarantees an additional 25 days of work—raising total coverage to 150 days under VB-G RAM G—for vulnerable rural households, including women-headed families and SC/ST communities.

* **Opposition Criticizes Rural Employment Scheme Implementation:** Congress leader Jairam Ramesh cited media reports alleging pervasive administrative fraud under the VB-G RAM G framework in drought-affected Maharashtra, claiming zero physical workers were present at five designated ground sites despite active muster rolls. Separately, the Youth Congress staged a protest burning an effigy with the face of Gyanesh under the slogan ‘Vote Chori Ki Raasleela’.

* **Environmental Pushback Against Kerala River Sand Mining:** The Society for the Protection of the Environment, Kerala (SPEK) voiced strong opposition to the state government's decision allowing sand mining across 17 rivers. While authorities frame the move as a flood mitigation measure, environmentalists flagged the lack of prior Environmental Impact Assessments (EIA). 

* **State Politics in Uttar Pradesh and Tamil Nadu:** The Samajwadi Party announced it will release its Uttar Pradesh election manifesto on November 26, incorporating targeted documents for youth, women, law and order, and its PDA social justice platform. In Tamil Nadu, police booked DMK councillor Sudha Deenadayalan of Villivakkam following a complaint alleging defamatory and objectionable statements against Chief Minister Vijay.

### Judiciary, Security, and Institutional Developments

* **Supreme Court Restricts Mid-Term Fee Hikes in Professional Colleges:** The Supreme Court ruled that educational institutions cannot impose mid-term fee increases for professional courses barring extraordinary circumstances, observing that course fees consume a substantial portion of an average household's income and savings. Additionally, a special Supreme Court bench comprising Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V. Mohana is set to hear Kerala Chief Minister Pinarayi Vijayan's plea in the CMRL matter and the YSRCP's petition regarding Andhra Pradesh civic poll nomination deadlines on October 21.

* **Arrests Made in Tiger Shroff Firing Case:** Mumbai Police transported three suspects from Kota in connection with the attack on actor Tiger Shroff. Charges have been registered under attempt to murder and the Arms Act, while Maharashtra Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis stated that neither the shooters nor their handlers would be spared.

* **Tragedy Strikes Indian Air Ambulance:** An Indian air ambulance en route from Agartala to Kolkata was forced to make an emergency landing in Dhaka, Bangladesh, following an in-flight technical failure. The 56-year-old critical patient on board subsequently died at a hospital in Dhaka.

* **Developments Across Campuses and Institutions:** 
  * At IIT Bombay, parents of the deceased student Sahil Wakode have appealed for Chief Ministerial intervention as students established a "Justice Corner 2.0" sit-in near the institute’s Ambedkar statue, following the removal of a month-long protest camp.
  * In Muzaffarnagar, an MBBS student was discovered dead inside a medical college hostel room; local police confirmed no suicide note was recovered and investigations remain underway.
  * On an entrepreneurial front, IIT Kanpur invited startup founders and working professionals to submit proposals for the Youth Entrepreneurs Challenge 2027 ahead of the October 22 deadline.
  * In Kerala, Congress leader Ramesh Chennithala pledged to coordinate with Chief Minister V.D. Satheesan to secure government employment for the daughter of the Pothundy murder victims, complying with a trial court directive.
