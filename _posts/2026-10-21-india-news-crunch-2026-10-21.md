### Politics, Governance, and Inter-State Affairs

**CBI Assumes Control of Parvinder Singh Jhota Murder Probe**  
The Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) has officially taken over the investigation into the murder of anti-drug activist Parvinder Singh Jhota in Punjab’s Mansa district. The transfer of the case to the central agency follows a recent visit by Union Home Minister Amit Shah to the region. Led by a senior DIG, the CBI investigative team has arrived in Mansa to examine the victim's family, while the post-mortem examination is scheduled at the Post Graduate Institute of Medical Education and Research (PGIMER) in Chandigarh. 

**Opposition Targets Hardeep Puri Over Past Jeffrey Epstein Interaction**  
Opposition parties, including the Congress, Trinamool Congress (TMC), and Nationalist Congress Party (NCP), have intensified pressure on Union Minister Hardeep Singh Puri over surfacing photographs documenting a meeting with the late convicted sex offender Jeffrey Epstein. While opposition leaders questioned the propriety of the engagement, Puri maintains that the interactions occurred strictly in a professional capacity during an official delegation visit.

**Tensions Persist Over Border and Civic Spaces in the States**  
In Odisha, opposition parties have criticized the state government for its perceived silence regarding the longstanding territorial dispute with Andhra Pradesh over the Kotia Gram Panchayat, where both states claim jurisdiction over 21 of the 28 villages. Meanwhile, in Andhra Pradesh, the Supreme Court has instructed the State Election Commission to decide by Thursday on whether to extend the deadline for candidate nominations. Separately, West Bengal continues to witness political friction, with book stalls organized by Left parties at Durga Puja pandals facing recurring disruptions, reflecting the ongoing contestation for public and cultural spaces in the state.

**Institutional Trust and the Election Commission**  
Discourse around institutional transparency has sharpened as analysts assess the Election Commission of India's (ECI) framework for addressing political grievances. Recent policy commentaries emphasize that while the constitutional authority is not obligated to entertain unsubstantiated allegations from political parties, establishing public trust requires grounding procedural decisions in accessible, verifiable empirical evidence.

### Economy, Trade, and Cybersecurity

**Critical Web Security Keys of Certifying Authority eMudhra Leaked**  
A significant cybersecurity vulnerability has emerged after eight web security keys belonging to Bengaluru-headquartered digital identity firm eMudhra were exposed. The firm supplies digital certificates and cybersecurity solutions to major government portals and public sector units, including the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI) and the national income tax filing platform. While official assessments indicate no immediate compromise of citizen data, the leak has raised serious questions regarding institutional cybersecurity protocols.

**Framework of the India–New Zealand Free Trade Agreement**  
Analysis of the recently enacted India–New Zealand Free Trade Agreement (FTA) outlines substantial mutual economic benefits. The pact provides structured concessions aimed at boosting Indian trade and inbound investment, alongside clear mechanisms to facilitate the bilateral mobility of students and skilled professionals, while maintaining protections for domestic sensitive sectors.

### Climate, Environment, and Urban Infrastructure

**Southern States Brace for Northeast Monsoon**  
Meteorological authorities have flagged heightened activity across the Bay of Bengal, prompting southern states to review their disaster readiness. A newly formed weather system is projected to deliver sustained, intense precipitation across Tamil Nadu through October 26, prompting a high-level review on monsoon preparedness by Chief Minister Vijay. In neighboring Andhra Pradesh—which registered a 59% rainfall deficit between October 1 and 20—the India Meteorological Department (IMD) has forecast severe thunderstorms and heavy rainfall for the southern coastal districts of Nellore and Tirupati.

**Kochi Air Quality Monitoring Stations Suffer Breakdown**  
Urban environmental tracking in Kerala has experienced setbacks following reports that real-time Continuous Ambient Air Quality Monitoring Stations in Kochi’s major hubs, Vyttila and Kaloor, have ceased functioning. Authorities are currently relying on manual air quality testing. Meanwhile, the region's third station in Eloor remains operational, though its annual maintenance contract expired over a year ago.

**Delhi-NCR Tightens Security for Dussehra**  
Ahead of Dussehra festivities, local administrations across Delhi-NCR have implemented stringent security arrangements and traffic diversions. The Delhi Traffic Police issued dedicated advisories detailing movement curbs across several major corridors, notably in the Dwarka sub-city, to manage high-volume festive gatherings.

### Society, Crime, and Culture

**Festive Celebrations Across Regions**  
Public celebrations marking Dasara saw wide civic and political engagement. Telangana Chief Minister A. Revanth Reddy observed the festival alongside family members at his native village of Kondareddipalli in Nagarkurnool. In Karnataka, final preparations are underway for the historic Mysuru Torchlight Parade, which will feature 18 cadet platoons, two mounted horse platoons, and elaborate stunt performances.

**Cultural Clashes and Entertainment Developments**  
In Uttarakhand, a cultural event sparked controversy when state minister Subodh Uniyal publicly confronted veteran singer Daler Mehndi on stage over alleged lip-syncing. The incident drew criticism from actor Anupam Kher, who publicly questioned the necessity and appropriateness of the minister's public intervention. In the southern film industry, the teaser for the Sundar C-directed film *Mookuthi Amman 2*—starring Nayanthara—was unveiled, confirming its theatrical release date for November 6.
