# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National Round-up

### Internal Security, Law Enforcement, and Public Safety

* **Volatile Conditions Persist in Manipur:** Authorities have clamped prohibitory orders across affected pockets of Thoubal district following violent clashes that left one person dead and several injured, alongside incidents of arson targeting homes and vehicles. Concurrently, Kuki groups announced a shutdown of a key highway following armed assaults on rural settlements, prompting the state administration to transfer the Superintendent of Police in Kamjong district.
* **Crime Branch Takes Over Firing Probe in Mumbai:** The Mumbai Crime Branch has assumed control of the investigation into the firing incident involving Tiger Shroff, lodging a First Information Report (FIR) against three unidentified assailants. Maharashtra Deputy Chief Minister Devendra Fadnavis stated that law enforcement has identified the suspects and handlers operating from within the state, indicating that arrests are imminent.
* **Dalit Man Assaulted at Uttar Pradesh Temple:** Police in Ballia have booked two individuals under relevant sections after a Dalit man was subjected to caste-based slurs and physically assaulted for residing and offering prayers inside a local temple premises on October 18.
* **Deadly Overbridge Collision in Varanasi:** Four individuals lost their lives after a truck collided with an SUV on an overbridge in Varanasi. The victims—identified in part as Aman Yadav, Golu Yadav, and Alok Mishra—were reportedly taking selfies at the site when the collision occurred. Two other injured individuals remain in critical condition.
* **Narcotics Seizures in Karnataka:** Enforcement agencies intercepted substantial contraband across two operations. In one case, authorities seized 202.75 kg of cannabis valued at over ₹1.01 crore, which had been concealed within a red gram crop. Separately, police in Gadag apprehended drug peddlers transporting ganja sourced from Solapur, Maharashtra.

### Political Affairs and Governance

* **Gubernatorial Transition in Uttarakhand:** Dhal Singh Bisen was officially sworn in as the Governor of Uttarakhand, succeeding Lt Gen Gurmeet Singh (retd.), who concluded a tenure spanning more than five years.
* **Discrepancies Uncovered in Delhi Electoral Rolls:** A public audit conducted across two polling booths in Delhi revealed that more than 100 registered voters, many residing at their addresses for decades, were erroneously categorized under the ‘Absent, Shifted, or Dead’ (ASD) lists. The affected residents were instructed to re-apply through Form 6 to regain voting status.
* **L.K. Advani Discharged from Hospital:** Veteran Bharatiya Janata Party (BJP) leader and former Deputy Prime Minister L.K. Advani was discharged from Indraprastha Apollo Hospital in New Delhi after recovering from a chest infection and associated pulmonary complications.
* **Opposition Confrontations on Recognition and Infrastructure:** Senior Congress MP Shashi Tharoor criticized the Union Government over its silence regarding the Nobel recognition awarded to Indian-origin jurist Navanethem "Navi" Pillay. In West Bengal, the Trinamool Congress (TMC) questioned state honors granted to the Chorbagan community puja, alleging authorities prioritized visual spectacle over civic safety. Meanwhile, former Kerala Finance Minister T.M. Thomas Isaac addressed Kerala's legacy infrastructure deficit, noting that resolving long-term developmental gaps will require alternative financing models beyond direct budgetary resources.

### Science, Technology, and Cybersecurity

* **Cyber Breach Hits Hyderabad Airport X Handle:** The official X handle of Rajiv Gandhi International Airport (@RGIAHyd) was temporarily compromised in an early-morning breach. Unauthorized posts promoted "StonkFun Season 1," a token verification platform linked to the Solana cryptocurrency ecosystem. Airport authorities subsequently restored account access and secured the handle.
* **Academic Distinction for Davangere University:** Five professors from Karnataka’s Davangere University have been ranked among the top 2% of scientists globally, earning institutional recognition for their research contributions across diverse scientific disciplines.

### Climate and Culture

* **Seasonal Shift in the National Capital:** New Delhi logged its lowest temperature of the month, with the minimum falling to 17.1 degrees Celsius. The India Meteorological Department (IMD) projects clear skies ahead, with daytime highs remaining around 32 degrees Celsius.
* **Diaspora Celebrations in Austria:** The Telugu community in Vienna, Austria, commemorated the Bathukamma and Dasara festivals to preserve regional cultural traditions among the diaspora and younger generations abroad.
* **Medical Update on Anbumani Ramadoss:** PMK leader Dr. Anbumani Ramadoss underwent an elective knee procedure at Apollo Hospitals, Greams Road, Chennai, with medical bulletins confirming stable post-procedure recovery.
