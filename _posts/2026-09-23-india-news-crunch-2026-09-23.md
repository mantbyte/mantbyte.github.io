# Political Volatility and Institutional Friction

In a landmark decision, the Election Commission of India (ECI) has frozen the name and ‘grass and flowers’ symbol of the All India Trinamool Congress (TMC). The move comes nearly 28 years after Mamata Banerjee founded the party, marking a significant shift in West Bengal’s political landscape. Simultaneously, the ECI is facing internal and external pressure as reports of dissent among Election Commissioners have surfaced. The controversy centers on alterations to ‘Form 6’ for voter registration, which now reportedly asks if applicants or their ancestors "exist" on previous rolls. This development has prompted Opposition parties to demand the removal of Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar.

On the legislative front, the debate over the Uniform Civil Code (UCC) has intensified. The Congress party recently characterized the UCC as a "bulldozer" intended to permanently polarize society. This statement followed the resignation of former Bombay High Court judge Justice R.C. Chavan from the Maharashtra government’s UCC drafting panel. Meanwhile, the Supreme Court has granted the Union Government additional time to consider a proposal to exempt current Class 6 students from the three-language policy, with Chief Justice Kant suggesting the government provide incentives for voluntary language adoption instead.

In regional party matters, the BJP has constituted a special committee to investigate "unexpected events" within its Tamil Nadu unit following complaints against several functionaries. In a separate incident, the teenage son of a Jammu-based BJP MLA, Vijay Sharma, was successfully traced to Delhi within hours of being reported missing.

# Judicial Rulings and Regulatory Crackdowns

The Bombay High Court has dismissed a plea by the Adani Group regarding the jurisdiction of domestic laws over duty-free shops. The petitioner argued that shops located in international departure areas exist beyond India’s territorial jurisdiction and should be exempt from domestic regulations. The court’s rejection reaffirms the reach of national law within airport premises.

In the corporate legal sphere, the Delhi High Court has granted bail to former ADAG executive Amitabh Jhunjhunwala in a case brought by the Enforcement Directorate (ED). The ED had opposed the plea, arguing that the petitioner did not meet the specific legal thresholds for being "sick" or "infirm" required for such relief.

Public health enforcement saw a major escalation in Mumbai as the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) took action against five prominent establishments, including the well-known restaurant Gallops. Inspections revealed severe hygiene violations, including the presence of live cockroaches, water stagnation, and cross-contamination of food items. Across the state, the FDA has seized stocks valued at ₹30.8 lakh as part of a broader crackdown on food safety violations.

# Environmental Hazards and Public Safety

The death toll from the fire at the RIMS Adilabad Special Newborn Care Unit (SNCU) has risen to five after two more infants succumbed to their injuries. The tragedy has raised urgent questions regarding fire safety protocols in state-run medical facilities. 

Weather conditions are deteriorating across Southern India as a depression in the Bay of Bengal is expected to intensify and cross the coast between Visakhapatnam and Gopalpur on the night of September 23. Authorities have declared school holidays in Visakhapatnam, Srikakulam, and surrounding districts. Telangana is also on high alert, with heavy rainfall forecasts and red alerts issued for eight districts. In Hyderabad, brief rain spells combined with idol immersion processions have led to significant traffic congestion.

In Kerala, the state cabinet has announced financial assistance for the families of nine individuals who lost their lives in recent rain-related incidents. However, the Kochi Corporation faces a fiscal crisis, reporting that it has received only a fraction of its allocated Plan funds from the state government after various deductions at source.

To address safety in the hospitality sector, Andhra Pradesh has launched the 'SafeStay AP' app. The platform allows guests to book verified accommodations while providing police with real-time occupancy data to strengthen emergency response capabilities. Additionally, to bridge information gaps in environmental policy, a coalition has published a Kannada translation of the seventh Western Ghats Ecologically Sensitive Area (ESA) draft, noting that the Union Government had previously only provided the document in English and Hindi despite Karnataka holding the largest share of the proposed protected area.

# National Sports: Asian Games 2026

India’s campaign at the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games 2026 has seen early success in weightlifting and shooting. Mirabai Chanu secured a silver medal in the women’s 48kg weightlifting category, maintaining her status as a premier global athlete. The shooting contingent also added to the national tally, with both the men’s and women’s skeet teams clinching bronze medals. These victories place India on the early leaderboard as the multisport event continues in Japan.
