# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National News and Events

## Weather Systems and Regional Infrastructure
Cyclone Murasu continues to impact southern coastal regions, though the National Disaster Response Force (NDRF) confirmed that no loss of life has been reported in Andhra Pradesh due to the storm. The cyclone is forecasted to weaken into a deep depression, with wind speeds reaching up to 75 kmph. Meanwhile, in Chennai, floodwaters have receded following light-to-very-light rainfall recorded across multiple stations, though damaged roads remain a significant concern for residents and local authorities. In a related transport incident on the Chennai-Bengaluru Highway in Vellore, three individuals—including drivers—were injured after an omnibus carrying 22 passengers collided with a lorry transporting over 40,000 empty TASMAC liquor bottles.

## Political Developments and Internal Unrest
In Jammu & Kashmir, the National Conference (NC) faces internal turmoil as Srinagar MP Aga Syed Ruhullah has tendered his resignation from his post, with posters in Budgam pointing toward a potential new political move by the leader. In national politics, the BJP has launched sharp attacks against the opposition, accusing Rahul Gandhi of treason and labeling him 'Bhasmasur' over his foreign engagements and the party's memorandum of understanding with the Communist Party. Concurrently, political sparring intensified in West Bengal, where state leadership accused Rahul Gandhi and Mamata Banerjee of collaborating to protect infiltrators. 

In regional state matters, the BRS party criticized the Congress administration in Telangana over withered paddy crops in Wanaparthy district's Palamuru region. In Goa, the state government announced plans to move the Supreme Court against a High Court order that stalled the Scheduled Tribe (ST) seat reservation process by restricting the Census Commissioner from notifying ST population estimates based on anything other than the primary census.

## Economic Pressures, Industry Updates, and Governance
The domestic poultry industry is facing severe headwinds driven by rising maize prices and availability shortages linked to El Niño conditions, prompting stakeholders in Hyderabad to push for a live chicken price of ₹110 per kg. In Tamil Nadu, the implementation of a ₹10 MRP hike across all liquor variants led to widespread chaos and confusion, resulting in numerous Tasmac shops pulling down their shutters without prior notice. 

In legal and administrative updates, the Bombay High Court expressed deep pain over the controversy surrounding a deleted eco-friendly Deepavali video message featuring actor Dia Mirza, directing the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation (BMC) to re-upload the appeal. Meanwhile, the Ladakh administration directed the Sonam Wangchuk-founded Desert Flower School in Leh to suspend activities pending a High Court verdict, citing a lack of requisite clearances. Aligarh Muslim University (AMU) also faced criticism regarding gender equity after approximately 4,000 women students at the Women’s College were excluded from voting in the AMUSU elections.

## Crime, Security, and Foreign Affairs
The Ministry of External Affairs (MEA) confirmed the tragic death of an Indian seafarer, identified as Gandhar Nahim Ajij from Gujarat, following an attack on a ship near Oman. Thirteen other crew members were successfully rescued, with India reiterating its urgent call for regional de-escalation. In domestic law enforcement, a special police team successfully apprehended Raghavan in his native area after he had escaped from police custody on October 31 while being escorted to the Coimbatore Central Prison. Additionally, a domestic fire broke out on the first floor of a multi-story residential building in East Delhi, resulting in two individuals being hospitalized.
