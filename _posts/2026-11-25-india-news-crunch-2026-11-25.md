# Mantbyte Daily Digest: National News and Events

## Governance, Administration, and Public Safety

The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) has initiated a pan-India enforcement drive targeting packaged drinking water. According to the FSSAI CEO, the regulatory scope was widened following a lab test failure in a sample of Bisleri packaged drinking water, with comprehensive lab results expected within one to two months. 

In Tamil Nadu, the legal and political fallout surrounding allegations against a TVK MLA has intensified. The Madras High Court was informed by the State Public Prosecutor that the T.N. Chief Secretary did not receive a formal complaint from a woman IAS officer regarding the alleged misbehaviour. Justice V. Lakshminarayanan has directed the Public Prosecutor to take instructions directly from the officer and report back by November 24, 2026. Meanwhile, Kallakurichi police have served personal summons to TVK MLA Arul Vignesh. DMK leader Udhayanidhi Stalin has submitted a petition to the Tamil Nadu DGP demanding the arrest of the Kallakurichi and Thuraiyur TVK MLAs, a CBI probe into the matter, and an inquiry into the Chief Secretary’s handling of the IAS officer's complaint.

In Jammu and Kashmir, local clerics issued an apology and performed repentance prayers—including reciting the ‘kalima’—after standing during the rendition of Vande Mataram at the first Waqf Day function. The Muttahida Majlis-e-Ulama stated it may seek legal opinion on the incident. On the security front, the J&K Director General of Police revealed that 30 terrorists hailing from Mahore and Chassana who went missing between 1991 and 2003 have exfiltrated to Pakistan for arms training to disrupt peace in India. Additionally, a tragic incident at the Thrissur Medical College Hospital saw a missing patient found dead in a washroom 36 hours after disappearing.

## Infrastructure, Investments, and Regional Development

The Union Cabinet has cleared the construction of a new airport at Silchar with an outlay of ₹2,014 crore. The existing Silchar facility is currently a civil enclave spanning 70 acres and is owned by the Indian Air Force and the Airports Authority of India. 

Internationally, Karnataka Chief Minister-led delegation in Japan secured a significant investment commitment, with Kawasaki pledging up to ₹800 crore in the State over the next 10 years. The expansion of the Harohalli plant is projected to create 600 jobs, alongside separate discussions held with Panasonic Automotive.

In Telangana, Chief Minister Revanth Reddy reviewed preparations for the upcoming Khelo India event scheduled from December 11 to 22. The CM emphasized utilizing the platform to build sports infrastructure and identify local talent, appointing specific district in-charges for the games. Furthermore, the Telangana government announced plans to establish a single-window system to support the poultry sector as a priority area. In Hyderabad, the Hyderabad Metropolitan Urban Transport Authority (HUMTA) identified 120 locations for pedestrian infrastructure, including a 1-km skywalk connecting the Secunderabad Railway Station, Metro stations, and bus terminals, as part of a vision for a pedestrian-friendly city by 2050.

## Regional Tensions and Political Developments

The Thirupparankundram hill controversy in Madurai continued for a second consecutive day, with devotees barred from the site and heavy security deployed. The Madurai District Collector stated that the Devasthanam did not authorize the specific lamp-lighting team, even as the Board of Trustees recorded a unanimous resolution to light the lamp at the traditional location based on historical continuity, Agamic evidence, and Sthanikar opinions. The Madras High Court issued a 'John Doe' order restraining any violations of the November 20 Supreme Court order, which permitted the lighting of the lamp strictly as a limited, one-time arrangement.

In Uttar Pradesh, political friction surfaced within the opposition alliance as the Samajwadi Party (SP) announced a list of five candidates for the upcoming assembly elections. The move occurred hours after a second round of meetings between the Congress party and the SP. While Congress remarked that the announcement "should have avoided" premature disclosures, SP President Akhilesh Yadav urged Congress to align with public expectations and realities to secure alliance victories.
