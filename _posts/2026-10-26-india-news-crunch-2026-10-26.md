# Cyclone Arnab Intensifies: Coastal States on High Alert

The Bay of Bengal is witnessing the intensification of a deep depression into Cyclonic Storm ‘Arnab,’ prompting large-scale emergency preparations across India’s eastern seaboard. In Odisha, Chief Minister Mohan Charan Majhi has deployed eight ministers to oversee operations in vulnerable districts, while the state government has officially banned fishing and tourist entry in six southern districts, including Ganjam and Koraput.

In Andhra Pradesh, the Eastern Power Distribution Company (APEPDCL) has established 41 control rooms and mobilized over 4,000 workers to address anticipated power outages in Kakinada and East Godavari. Despite the looming storm, State Election Commissioner Anil Chandra Punetha expressed hope that municipal polls would proceed as scheduled, anticipating a return to normalcy within 48 hours of the cyclone making landfall.

Compounding environmental concerns in the region, a natural gas leak was reported from an ONGC pipeline in Andhra Pradesh’s Konaseema district. Operations at nearby wells have been suspended as technical teams work to plug the leak and install a replacement pipeline. Meanwhile, in Kerala, authorities are drafting preventive measures to curb escalating human-wildlife conflicts in the Perambra estate following a series of wild animal incursions in the Peruvannamuzhi forest range.

# Judicial Reforms and High-Profile Legal Proceedings

The Supreme Court of India announced a significant update regarding judicial administration, noting that 14 states have now agreed to raise the retirement age of judicial officers from 60 to 62 years. Chief Justice D.Y. Chandrachud expressed optimism that remaining states would soon follow suit to ensure institutional stability.

In specific legal cases, the Supreme Court granted interim relief to Ashish Mishra, the primary accused in the Lakhimpur Kheri violence case, allowing him to visit his home for the Diwali festival. However, the court was also informed of a new FIR registered against Mishra for allegedly threatening a witness. In a separate matter, the Madras High Court denied anticipatory bail to Anbil Mahesh Poyyamozhi in a case involving "party fund" collections currently under investigation by the Chennai Central Crime Branch.

Further legal developments include the acquittal of Rahul Gandhi in a 2018 defamation case regarding remarks made against Amit Shah. In Kochi, a tragic incident has drawn scrutiny to the judiciary after a minor, reportedly the son of a senior judge, was taken into custody following a fatal collision between a luxury car and a scooter.

# Political Friction Over Inflation and Public Rhetoric

Political tensions have escalated as the Congress party criticized Prime Minister Narendra Modi for continuing his "Mann Ki Baat" broadcasts while food inflation remains a pressing concern. Citing recent RBI data, Congress leader Jairam Ramesh urged the Prime Minister to pivot focus toward the rising costs of essential household items.

Simultaneously, a war of words has broken out between the BJP and Congress leadership. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh condemned Congress President Mallikarjun Kharge for remarks directed at the Prime Minister, characterizing the rhetoric as an insult to the nation. 

In Bihar, RJD MP Sudhakar Singh has sought the intervention of the Lok Sabha Speaker regarding a ₹5 crore defamation notice issued by a senior police official. Singh has demanded a probe into the allegations, questioning whether the legal action had the formal approval of the state government.

# Resource Management: Water Diversion and Agricultural Shifts

Faced with declining water levels in the Singur and Manjira reservoirs, the Telangana government has initiated emergency measures to secure Hyderabad’s drinking water supply. Engineering works are currently underway near the Outer Ring Road to interconnect the Godavari ring main with existing Manjira pipelines, ensuring a steady flow of water to the capital.

In the agricultural sector, Telangana farmers are seeing favorable returns in the open market, with rice prices reaching ₹3,000 to ₹3,500 per quintal as consumers pre-book stocks to hedge against future price hikes. Conversely, in Karnataka, PWD Minister Satish Jarkiholi highlighted the grievances of the sugar industry, calling for a revision of the Minimum Support Price (MSP) and the settlement of pending power bills to stabilize the sector.
